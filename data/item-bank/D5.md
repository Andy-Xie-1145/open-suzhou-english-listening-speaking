# D5 类 · 分类对应表与对比表表格填空（10 份）

> 来源：并行子任务产出｜共 10 份 / 30 空｜每份词库 5 个短语，恰好 2 个为干扰项
> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本题型（材料 + 表格 + 挖空 + 从词库选短语填入）依据用户记忆生成，**结构未经官方证实**。
> 本批表格类型限定：分类对应表与对比表——人物—属性对应 3 份（D5-01/07/08）、需求—物品对应 1 份（D5-03）、两个方案逐项对比 2 份（D5-02/06）、场所定位对应 2 份（D5-04/09）、前后变化对照 2 份（D5-05/10），10 份互不雷同。

## D5-01 科技节上的三份贡献

Xu Lei likes making models, and she built a small rocket for the show in the physics room. Lin Hui wanted more students to join in, so she wrote a story about a moon trip and read it to the younger students in the reading room.
**Table**
| Student | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Lin Hui | ① |
| Zhou Min | ② |
| Xu Lei | ③ |
**Word Bank**
A. a photo show for new students
B. a big poster about the festival
C. a small rocket for the show
D. a short talk about smart robots
E. a story about a moon trip
**Answers**
① E　② B　③ C

## D5-02 两种借书方案对比
**Dialogue**
W: I want to borrow some books from the city library this term. Do you know how much it costs?
M: Plan A is 15 yuan for half a year. A whole year costs twice as much, so 30 yuan. And with Plan A you can borrow two books at a time.
W: What about Plan B?
M: Plan B is 25 yuan for half a year, and a whole year is 50 yuan, but you can borrow four books at a time with Plan B.
W: Then Plan B is better for me, because I read a lot.
**Table**
| Point of comparison | Plan A | Plan B |
|---|---|---|
| Price for half a year | ① | ② |
| Price for a whole year | ③ | — |
**Word Bank**
A. 30 yuan
B. 50 yuan
C. 15 yuan
D. 40 yuan
E. 25 yuan
**Answers**
① C　② E　③ A

## D5-03 春游要带的三样东西
**Passage**

We should take a plastic sheet, because there is a lot of sand on the grass and we can keep the boxes off it, so we put the sheet down before we take the food out. An old towel is useful too, since the grass is damp in the morning and we can spread it out to sit on when we have our picnic at noon. A small bottle of insect spray will keep the mosquitoes away from us when we eat dinner on the hill in the evening. The weather report says there will be no rain that day, so nobody needs to bring a raincoat. And there is only one path up the hill, which we all know well, so we will not take a map.
**Table**
| What we are doing on the hill | Which thing we take out of the bag |
|---|---|
| When we have our picnic at noon | ① |
| When we eat our dinner on the hill in the evening | ② |
| Before we take the food out of the boxes | ③ |
**Word Bank**
A. a paper map
B. an old towel
C. a small bottle of insect spray
D. a big raincoat
E. a plastic sheet
**Answers**
① B　② C　③ E
