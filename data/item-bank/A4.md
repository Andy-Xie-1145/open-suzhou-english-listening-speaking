> A4 家庭、朋友、邻里与人际交往（15 题）

## A4-01 电话约好见面时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Hi, is that Ben? This is Emma. Are you free this Friday afternoon?
Boy: Let me check. I have a piano lesson at four, but I am free after five.
Girl: Great. Let's meet at the school library at half past five.
Boy: See you then.
**Question**
What time will the two students meet?
- A. At four o'clock.
- B. At five o'clock.
- C. At half past five.
- D. At half past four.
**Answer** C

## A4-02 邀约看球被婉拒
**Dialogue**
Girl: Would you like to watch the basketball game with us on Sunday?
Boy: I would love to, but I am going to a friend's birthday party that afternoon.
Girl: That is a pity. Perhaps next Sunday.
Boy: Thanks for asking me anyway.
**Question**
Why can't the boy go to the game with the girl?
- A. He has to go to a birthday party.
- B. He is going to play in the game.
- C. He has too much homework that day.
- D. He doesn't like watching basketball.
**Answer** A

## A4-03 打碎杯子后的道歉
**Dialogue**
Boy: I am really sorry. I broke the cup in the kitchen this morning.
Girl: That is all right. It was only an old cup.
Boy: I will buy a new one for you this weekend.
Girl: You don't need to.
**Question**
What does the boy say he will do?
- A. Clean the kitchen this weekend.
- B. Pay for the cup at once.
- C. Ask the girl to choose a new cup.
- D. Buy a new cup this weekend.
**Answer** D

## A4-04 感谢邻居代为照料
**Dialogue**
Girl: Thank you for looking after my cat and watering the flowers.
Woman: They are easy to care for. My son was happy to help.
Girl: How can I pay you for the week?
Woman: Bring me some tomatoes from your garden.
**Question**
What will the girl bring the woman?
- A. Some flowers from her garden.
- B. Some tomatoes from her garden.
- C. A cake from the shop.
- D. A box of fish from the market.
**Answer** B

## A4-05 在朋友家做客帮忙
**Dialogue**
Boy: Good evening. May I come in?
Woman: Of course. My parents are in the kitchen.
Boy: Can I help you with the dinner?
Woman: Thanks. Please wash the fruit and put it on the table. Dinner will be ready in ten minutes.
Boy: No problem.
**Question**
What is the boy going to do?
- A. Wash the fruit and put it on the table.
- B. Watch TV in the living room.
- C. Wash the plates after the meal.
- D. Buy some fruit at the shop.
**Answer** A

## A4-06 借字典并约定归还时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Excuse me, could I borrow your bicycle this afternoon?
Boy: I am sorry. I need it to go to the dentist.
Girl: Then may I borrow your dictionary instead?
Boy: Sure. Please bring it back before dinner tomorrow.
**Question**
When will the girl return the dictionary?
- A. This evening.
- B. After dinner tomorrow.
- C. Before dinner tomorrow.
- D. Next Friday.
**Answer** C

## A4-07 把表妹介绍给大家
**Dialogue**
Girl: Come in, everyone. This is my cousin. She is staying with us this week.
Boy: Nice to meet you.
Girl: She is in Grade Nine like me, and she plays the guitar very well.
**Question**
What instrument does the girl's cousin play?
- A. The guitar.
- B. The piano.
- C. The violin.
- D. The drum.
**Answer** A

## A4-08 邻居帮忙搬箱子
**Dialogue**
Woman: Excuse me, could you help me carry this box upstairs?
Man: Of course. Where are you taking it?
Woman: To the flat on the fourth floor. My old flat was on the second floor, and my son is away at school this week.
Man: Let me take it. It looks heavy.
**Question**
Which floor is the woman's new flat on?
- A. The second floor.
- B. The third floor.
- C. The fifth floor.
- D. The fourth floor.
**Answer** D

## A4-09 周末的早餐安排
**Dialogue**
Man: On weekdays I get up at six and leave home at seven twenty.
Woman: Tomorrow is Saturday. You don't have to leave so early.
Man: You are right. I'll have breakfast with you at eight.
**Question**
What will the man do tomorrow morning?
- A. Have breakfast with the woman at eight.
- B. Leave home at seven twenty.
- C. Get up at six o'clock.
- D. Go to work by bus.
**Answer** A

## A4-10 请邻居代收快递
**Dialogue**
Woman: Hello. I am in flat 302. A parcel came for me this morning, but I was out.
Man: I am your neighbour. I have it here.
Woman: Could you leave it at my door? I am home at six.
Man: Sure. I am going up now, so I will put it there.
**Question**
What will the man do with the parcel?
- A. Take it to the post office.
- B. Give it to another neighbour.
- C. Leave it at the woman's door.
- D. Open it and check the address.
**Answer** C

## A4-11 改约看电影的时间
**Dialogue**
Boy: Shall we go to the film on Saturday afternoon?
Girl: I would love to, but I have to look after my little brother then. Could we go on Sunday morning?
Boy: Sunday morning suits me. Shall we meet outside the cinema at nine in the morning?
Girl: See you there.
**Question**
When will the two friends meet?
- A. Saturday afternoon.
- B. Sunday morning.
- C. Saturday morning.
- D. Sunday evening.
**Answer** B

## A4-12 送给奶奶的围巾
**Dialogue**
Girl: This scarf is for you, Grandma. I bought it with my own money.
Woman: How kind of you! But you students should save your money.
Girl: Please accept it. You always help me with my homework.
Woman: All right. Thank you, my dear. I will wear it this winter.
**Question**
When will the woman wear the scarf?
- A. This summer.
- B. This winter.
- C. Next spring.
- D. On her birthday.
**Answer** B

## A4-13 晚餐面条里放什么
**Dialogue**
Man: I am making noodles for dinner. Would you like some beef in them?
Woman: I am afraid I can't eat beef. Could you make them with vegetables instead?
Man: All right. I have mushrooms and cabbage in the fridge.
Woman: That would be lovely, thank you.
**Question**
What will be in the noodles?
- A. Beef and carrots.
- B. Mushrooms and tomatoes.
- C. Mushrooms and cabbage.
- D. Cabbage and potatoes.
**Answer** C

## A4-14 打电话找不在家的家人
**Dialogue**
Girl: May I speak to Tom, please?
Man: I am sorry, Tom is not here. He has gone to the sports centre with his father.
Girl: Oh. When will he be back?
Man: He said he would be home at half past four.
**Question**
Where is Tom now?
- A. At the sports centre.
- B. At the school library.
- C. At his friend's home.
- D. At the dentist's.
**Answer** A

## A4-15 约定下次互相帮忙
**Dialogue**
Boy: Thanks for helping me clean my room yesterday.
Girl: You are welcome. You helped me with my maths last week.
Boy: Let's help each other from now on.
Girl: Good idea. I am free next Monday, but I am busy this weekend.
**Question**
When will the two students work together again?
- A. This weekend.
- B. Tomorrow.
- C. Next Monday.
- D. This Sunday.
**Answer** C
