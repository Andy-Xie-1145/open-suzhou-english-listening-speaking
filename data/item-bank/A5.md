## A5-01 请邻居照看金鱼
**Dialogue**
Woman: Ms. Chen, we are going to our hometown for the Spring Festival. Could you look after my goldfish?
Ms. Chen: Of course. Two fish, an orange one and a white one, right?
Woman: That's right. Please feed them in the morning and in the evening.
Ms. Chen: All right. I'm at home all day, so they won't go hungry.
**Question**
How many goldfish will Ms. Chen look after, and what colour are they?
- A. One orange goldfish.
- B. One orange goldfish and one white goldfish.
- C. Two goldfish, both white.
- D. One white goldfish.
**Answer** B
