## D-01 一周社团活动
**Passage**
Tom takes part in three after-school activities this term. He goes to the chess club on Monday afternoon. On Wednesday afternoon he practises speaking English with his classmates in the reading room. On Friday afternoon he plays basketball on the playground.
**Table**
| Day and time | Activity |
|---|---|
| Monday afternoon | ① |
| Wednesday afternoon | ② |
| Friday afternoon | ③ |
**Word Bank**
A. go to the chess club
B. play basketball on the playground
C. clean the school library after school
D. practise speaking English with his classmates
E. go on a school trip next week
**Answers**
① A　② D　③ B
