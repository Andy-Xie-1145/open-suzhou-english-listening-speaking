## A2-01 补办公交卡
**Dialogue**
Girl: Sorry, my bus card doesn't work today.
Man: Is something wrong with it? You can try it again on the machine over there.
Girl: It still doesn't work. Can I get a new one here?
Man: Yes, but a new card costs ten yuan.
Girl: Here is the money. And here's my old card.
**Question**
How much must the girl pay for the new card?
- A. Nothing.
- B. Five yuan.
- C. Ten yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** C
