## B3-01 两种书包比价
**Dialogue**
Man: Good afternoon. My old schoolbag broke last week, so I need a new one.
Ms. Doyle: I'm sorry about that. Do you need something to carry books and a lunch box?
Man: Yes, and I don't want it too heavy.
Ms. Doyle: Then try the blue one on this table. It is two kilos, with two pockets inside and strong straps.
Man: How much is the blue one?
Ms. Doyle: One hundred and sixty yuan. The dark blue ones cost one hundred and eighty yuan.
Man: Then I'd like the dark blue one. Do you have it?
Ms. Doyle: I'm afraid not. They all sold out last week. The green one in that corner costs only ninety-eight yuan, but it has one pocket and thinner straps.
Man: Then I'd rather pay more for the blue one. Can I pay with my phone here?
Ms. Doyle: Yes, just scan the code on the counter. Here is your receipt; we can repair it free within one year.
**Q1**
Why does the man finally choose the blue schoolbag?
- A. Because it has two pockets and much stronger straps.
- B. Because it is lighter and cheaper than the green one.
- C. Because the dark blue one is still available.
- D. Because the green one has already been sold out.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
How much does the man pay for the schoolbag?
- A. Ninety-eight yuan.
- B. One hundred and eighty yuan.
- C. One hundred and sixty yuan.
- D. One hundred and forty yuan.
**Answer** C

## B3-02 退换一件外套
**Dialogue**
Woman: I bought this jacket here last Saturday, but it is too tight for me.
Ms. Green: I'm sorry about that. Do you still have the receipt?
Woman: Yes, here it is. I paid three hundred and twenty yuan for it.
Ms. Green: Thank you. Clothes can be exchanged within seven days, shoes within three days, bags within fourteen days, but sale items can never be taken back.
Woman: Mine wasn't on sale. It was the usual price. I need a larger size, because this one is too small.
Ms. Green: Then you're lucky. We have one large left, and it costs the same three hundred and twenty yuan.
Woman: How can it be the same price after a week?
Ms. Green: The price has not changed this month; only sale items get cheaper.
Woman: All right. I'll take the large one. Do you take the old jacket back here?
Ms. Green: Yes, please give it to me, and here is the receipt for your new jacket.
Woman: Thank you. Could you wrap it for me? I haven't brought a bag.
Ms. Green: Certainly. Here is a paper bag. I hope the new jacket fits you better.
**Q1**
How long does the woman have to exchange her jacket?
- A. Within three days.
- B. Within fourteen days.
- C. Within seven days.
- D. Within thirty days.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
What does the woman do at the end of the talk?
- A. She keeps the small jacket and buys a large one.
- B. She gives back the old jacket and takes the new one.
- C. She asks the shop to post the large one to her.
- D. She leaves the jacket and comes back next week.
**Answer** B

## B3-03 试鞋选码付现
**Dialogue**
Man: I'd like to try on this pair of black shoes. I usually wear size 41.
Mr. White: Certainly. Every size costs the same, four hundred and ninety yuan.
Man: Thank you. I'm afraid size 41 is too tight; my toes hurt after a moment.
Mr. White: Then please try size 42. We still have two pairs of 42, and that is our largest size.
Man: Size 42 feels much better. Will the shop take them back if they don't work?
Mr. White: Yes. Bring the shoes, the box and the receipt back within fourteen days, and we will change them.
Man: Good. How much do I pay now?
Mr. White: Four hundred and ninety yuan, five yuan off if you pay in cash.
Man: I'll pay in cash, please. Here are four hundred and eighty-five yuan.
Mr. White: Thank you. Here is the receipt, and here is the box; please keep them both.
Man: Of course. I'll keep them safe at home.
Mr. White: Then come back to us within fourteen days if there is any problem.
**Q1**
Why does the man choose size 42?
- A. Because the shop has already sold out size 41.
- B. Because size 41 feels too tight on his feet.
- C. Because size 42 is much cheaper than size 41.
- D. Because size 42 is the only pair left in the shop.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
How does the man pay for the shoes?
- A. He pays by card for 490 yuan.
- B. He pays in cash for 490 yuan.
- C. He pays by card for 485 yuan.
- D. He pays in cash for 485 yuan.
**Answer** D

## B3-04 特卖日挑毛衣
**Dialogue**
Woman: I heard that you are having a special sale this week. What's in it?
Mr. Baker: All our winter sweaters are sixty percent of the old prices, from Monday to Saturday.
Woman: So a sweater that was two hundred yuan is now a hundred and twenty yuan?
Mr. Baker: That's right. And if you buy two, the second one is half price.
Woman: I only need one. I can't choose between the blue and the green one.
Mr. Baker: The blue one is a hundred and twenty yuan, but the green one was two hundred and fifty yuan and is now a hundred and fifty yuan.
Woman: Why is the green one thirty yuan more expensive?
Mr. Baker: It is made of wool, so it is thicker, and it has a higher collar.
Woman: That sounds right. I get up at five for work, and I need something warm.
Mr. Baker: Then the green one is a good choice. Would you like to pay at the counter now?
Woman: Yes, please. Can I pay by card?
Mr. Baker: Of course, just tap your card here. Please keep this receipt; the sale ends on Saturday.
**Q1**
How much does the woman save on the green sweater?
- A. 150 yuan.
- B. 200 yuan.
- C. 250 yuan.
- D. 100 yuan.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
Why does the woman choose the green sweater?
- A. Because it is made of wool and is thicker.
- B. Because it is cheaper than the blue one.
- C. Because it is half price this week.
- D. Because it is the only one in the sale.
**Answer** A

## B3-05 预订靴子改期取货
**Dialogue**
Woman: I'd like to order this pair of boots and collect them on Sunday afternoon.
Ms. Hall: Certainly. May I have your size, please?
Woman: Thirty-seven. Could I change the collection day if something goes wrong?
Ms. Hall: We keep an order until the end of the week. The boots are two hundred and sixty yuan, and we need a fifty-yuan deposit.
Woman: Here is the deposit. But my trip has been put off, so I cannot come on Sunday.
Ms. Hall: I'm sorry about that. The deposit comes out of the price, but if you cancel less than two days before, we can't give it back.
Woman: Then please don't cancel it. Could you move the collection to next Saturday?
Ms. Hall: I can, but that's the last change. We close at half past five on Saturdays, so come before five.
Woman: I'll come at half past four. Can you post the boots to me?
Ms. Hall: I'm afraid we don't post goods. If anything changes, please phone us on Friday morning.
Woman: All right. I'll phone on Friday and collect the boots on Saturday afternoon.
Ms. Hall: Thank you. We will have the boots ready for you then.
**Q1**
How much will the woman pay when she collects the boots?
- A. 210 yuan.
- B. 50 yuan.
- C. 260 yuan.
- D. 310 yuan.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
What will the woman do on Saturday?
- A. She will ask the shop to post the boots to her.
- B. She will cancel the order and get her deposit back.
- C. She will collect the boots at half past four on Saturday.
- D. She will collect the boots at seven o'clock on Saturday.
**Answer** C

## B3-06 集市上讨价还价
**Dialogue**
Woman: Excuse me, how much is this old table lamp?
Mr. Cole: Twelve yuan. I bought it last year, and it still works well.
Woman: It looks rather old. Would you take eight yuan?
Mr. Cole: Eight yuan? No. The lamp is already much cheaper than in the shops, and I have to carry it home.
Woman: The top part is a little broken. How about nine yuan fifty?
Mr. Cole: Let me think. Nine fifty is too little for an old lamp. Ten yuan, and I'll give you a free light bulb too.
Woman: Ten yuan with a bulb is fair. Can I come back and get it on Sunday morning?
Mr. Cole: The market is only open on Saturdays, and we start at seven. Good lamps go early.
Woman: Then I'll come at half past eight. Do you take cards here?
Mr. Cole: Cash only, I'm afraid. If you need to change money, there is a machine at the gate.
Woman: All right. I'll bring twenty yuan to be safe.
Mr. Cole: Ten is quite enough. Come early, and I'll put the lamp aside for you.
**Q1**
How much will the woman pay for the lamp at last?
- A. Eight yuan.
- B. Nine yuan fifty.
- C. Ten yuan.
- D. Twelve yuan.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
How will the woman pay for the lamp?
- A. She will pay by card on Saturday morning.
- B. She will pay in cash on Saturday morning.
- C. She will pay by card on Sunday morning.
- D. She will pay in cash on Sunday morning.
**Answer** B
