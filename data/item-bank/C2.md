## C2-01 上山的两个小时
**Passage**
When Gao Wei was a university student, his first trip into the forest ended badly. He wanted to know why a small white plant grew well on a hill but died in the valley below it. He collected plants from both places and put them in the same pot at home. Within a week the valley plants were dead, and so were the hill plants. He felt sorry for himself and nearly gave up. Then his teacher took him back to the hill, and the climb took them two hours. When they arrived, Gao Wei noticed something he had missed before: the air on the hill was much colder, and water came out of the rocks all year round, while the valley was hot and dry in summer. He understood at last that nothing had gone wrong in his own work; the plant needed cool air and clean water, and a pot in a room could give it neither. Gao Wei has studied this one plant for twenty-six years since then. His team knows that only about four hundred plants like it are still alive, and every spring they climb up and count them. The number is always smaller. He says the work is slow and often disappointing, but every spring he goes up the hill again.
**Q1**
What did Gao Wei do after his first trip failed?
- A. He climbed the hill again with his teacher.
- B. He asked his classmates to help with the plants.
- C. He changed his school subject to maths.
- D. He looked for a job in the city.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
What made the hill different from the valley?
- A. The wind was stronger and the soil was dry.
- B. The air was colder and the rocks gave out water.
- C. The air was warmer and the rocks gave out sand.
- D. The soil was thinner and the plants grew taller.
**Answer** B
**Q3**
How many plants like Gao Wei's are still alive?
- A. About six hundred.
- B. About two hundred.
- C. About four hundred.
- D. About fourteen hundred.
**Answer** C
**Q4**
What can we learn about Gao Wei?
- A. He hopes to leave the forest for an easier job.
- B. He believes careful watching can turn a wrong answer right.
- C. He thinks counting the plants is enough for him.
- D. He wishes his teacher would do the counting for him.
**Answer** B
**Q5**
What is the passage mainly about?
- A. How a young researcher came to see his subject differently.
- B. How a forest near a university supplies plants for research.
- C. How a small white plant is used in university lessons.
- D. How a teacher trains his students to walk in the mountains.
**Answer** A

## C2-02 河边的第二个学年
**Passage**
Mei Lin is seventeen years old and goes to a middle school in a small town. She began running when she was eleven, and the reason was practical: her school is six kilometres from her home, and no bus went there. She ran the way to school every morning, in the rain and in the snow. At fourteen she joined the county running team, where she was the shortest and her results were ordinary, so her coach did not give her much attention. In her second year at school she started to run beside the river after dinner, one hour, five evenings a week. She was hard on herself and kept a small notebook: the date, the distance and how she felt. She still keeps it today. Last autumn she ran her first half-marathon and finished in under two hours. This spring she won a place on the city team. Her coach now says the notebook was the best thing in her training, but Mei Lin does not agree with him. She says the notebook did not make her fast; it only made her honest about what she could and could not do on each day.
**Q1**
Why did Mei Lin begin running at the age of eleven?
- A. Because her coach asked her to join a team.
- B. Because no bus went between her home and school.
- C. Because she wanted to win a school race.
- D. Because she hoped to be a professional runner.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What does Mei Lin write in her small notebook?
- A. The date, the distance and the name of each race.
- B. The weather, the time and the speed of each run.
- C. The date, the distance and how she felt.
- D. The judges, the prizes and the teams of the race.
**Answer** C
**Q3**
What did Mei Lin do last autumn?
- A. She began to run beside the river after dinner.
- B. She moved to the city with her new coach.
- C. She won a place on the city running team.
- D. She ran her first half-marathon in under two hours.
**Answer** D
**Q4**
What does Mei Lin mean by saying the notebook only made her honest?
- A. It helped her see her real progress clearly.
- B. It stopped her from training too hard.
- C. It made her coach change the plan.
- D. It let her compare her notes with other runners.
**Answer** A
**Q5**
What is the passage mainly about?
- A. Why small towns need better sports facilities.
- B. How coaches choose runners for the city team.
- C. How an ordinary schoolgirl became a city team runner.
- D. Why a notebook is better than a training plan.
**Answer** C

## C2-03 剪纸的邻居
**Passage**
I live next door to Tan Bo, and for four years I have watched him cut paper. Last autumn he handed me the scissors. Tan Bo is sixty-eight. He began when he was ten, the winter his grandfather died. His family had no money for paper, so he drew his first birds on old newspapers, following the black lines of the sports page as branches. Later the paper factory in the town gave away its waste paper every Spring Festival, and he had white paper of his own. He has cut more than six hundred designs in his life. Many show animals and plants, but he says the paper in his village is full of rivers and bridges, so bridges are what he cuts most often. A few years ago a young designer asked why none of his work had people in it. Tan Bo was quiet for a long time. Then he said a face is the difficult part; if the eyes are wrong, the whole thing looks like a mask. Since then he has cut three windows with people in them, and he keeps a paper face to check the eyes. He says that in a village paper is not decoration. It is what a family puts on a window and looks at every day.
**Q1**
What did Tan Bo use for his first birds?
- A. The waste paper from a paper factory.
- B. The blank pages of an old book.
- C. The shopping lists of his family.
- D. The old newspapers from his own home.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
Why does Tan Bo cut bridges more often than animals?
- A. Because his grandfather left him a bridge design.
- B. Because bridges are quicker to cut than animals.
- C. Because village paper always shows rivers and bridges.
- D. Because a young designer once asked him to.
**Answer** C
**Q3**
What happened after the young designer visited him?
- A. He stopped cutting animals and plants for good.
- B. He began to cut windows with people in them.
- C. He taught the designer to cut paper faces.
- D. He gave away his old six hundred designs.
**Answer** B
**Q4**
What can we learn about Tan Bo?
- A. He wants to stop working now that he is old.
- B. He believes faces are impossible to cut.
- C. He takes his time before he changes his art.
- D. He prefers to work without anybody's advice.
**Answer** C
**Q5**
What is the passage mainly about?
- A. Why a village factory gives away its waste paper.
- B. How a young designer learnt to cut paper.
- C. Why paper animals are easier to cut than faces.
- D. How an old artist keeps learning while he works.
**Answer** D

## C2-04 药瓶上的小画
**Passage**
Zhou Meilan has worked as a nurse in a small community clinic for eleven years. The clinic has three doctors and four nurses, and she likes the place because everyone there knows her name. In her first years she was afraid of the older patients, who often came in with angry faces and said very little. One winter evening an old man came in, put a small paper bag on the desk and walked out without a word. Inside the bag were three medicine boxes he had never opened. Zhou Meilan ran after him into the snow and asked what was wrong. He said he could not read the instructions on the boxes, so for months he had put the boxes away and never taken the medicine at all. That night she began to draw a small picture of how to take the medicine on the label of every bottle she handed out, and to ask each patient to repeat the instructions back to her before leaving. She also learnt to speak more slowly and to wait for an answer, because she noticed that old people often said yes too quickly. Zhou Meilan says a picture is understood by everybody, but handwriting is not.
**Q1**
Why did the old man stop taking his medicine?
- A. Because he had lost the paper bag.
- B. Because he did not like the taste.
- C. Because he could not read the instructions.
- D. Because the clinic was too far from his home.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
What does Zhou Meilan draw on a medicine bottle?
- A. A small picture that shows the price.
- B. A small picture that shows the maker.
- C. A small picture that shows the year.
- D. A small picture that shows the way to use it.
**Answer** D
**Q3**
Why did Zhou Meilan learn to speak more slowly?
- A. Because old people often said yes too quickly.
- B. Because her clinic had more patients that year.
- C. Because her teacher spoke very quickly.
- D. Because the medicine names were difficult.
**Answer** A
**Q4**
What can we learn about Zhou Meilan?
- A. She changed her job because of that winter evening.
- B. She looks for simple ways to solve real problems.
- C. She believes doctors should do the talking for old patients.
- D. She thinks old people cannot be taught to use medicine.
**Answer** B
**Q5**
What is the passage mainly about?
- A. Why some old people do not like hospitals.
- B. What an old man once put in his paper bag.
- C. Why a small clinic is hard to run.
- D. How a nurse made her work easier for old people.
**Answer** D

## C2-05 每周五的信
**Passage**
Xu Lan has walked the same road for sixteen years. Every morning at seven she takes forty-three letters and goes up to Green Stone Village, four kilometres from the post office. Her daily walk has never been easy: in winter the path is covered with ice, and in summer the river rises so high that she must wait for the water to go down. Still, she knows every family on the road. Seven years ago an old man asked her a favour. He wanted to write to his son far away, but his hands shook too much to hold a pen. Xu Lan bought a writing pad and began to write his letters for him. Since then she has gone to his door every Friday for seven years. She says the letters are always short, but she has learnt to ask the right questions, because a letter that says only hello makes people worry. Last year the old man's son came back for two weeks to visit him. Now the letters are coming back unopened, and Xu Lan is waiting.
**Q1**
Why did the old man ask Xu Lan for a favour?
- A. Because his hands shook too much to write.
- B. Because he had lost his son's address.
- C. Because he wanted to learn to read and write.
- D. Because his son had stopped writing back.
**Answer** A
**Q2**

- B. She asks him questions about his week.

- D. She writes the letter from memory.
**Answer** B

**Answer** D
