> **What can we learn about Zhou Meilan?**
> - A. She changed her job because of that winter evening.
> - B. She looks for simple ways to solve real problems.

> - D. She thinks old people cannot be taught to use medicine.

- **D 被材料直接否定** —— 「ask each patient to **repeat** the instructions back to her」要求老人当场复述说明，正是「老人学得会」的证据。
