## 2. 中等 1：C3-02 的 180 公斤量级矛盾 —— 已修复

### 2.1 先核对任务书声明（逐条回原文验证）

任务书写「根因在**第 5 句**的 **takes everything out**」。我把 C3-02 材料按句切分后实测：

> 1 : Every Wednesday an old man walks slowly around the block with two cloth bags.
> 2 : He has done this for six years, and the bags are for the wrong things he takes out of the box.
> 3 : People should not put used batteries, old medicine or broken lamps in the normal rubbish bin.
> 4 : The city has put a small red box at the corner of the block, and anyone can drop these things there free of charge.
> 5 : The box is often full by the end of the week, and the city weighs the whole box and tells him the number.
> 6 : He writes the number in a book, and a sudden rise in it tells him that someone has put the wrong thing in the box.
> 7 : His record for one week is a hundred and eighty kilograms, while in his first year it was less than fifty.
> 8 : He says this is the most useful thing he has ever done, not because the numbers are important, but because many neighbours still put a paper cup or a broken phone into the red box.
> 9 : He does not get angry.
> 10 : He simply takes each one out and shows it to its owner at the next gate.
> 11 : Last year four hundred families learned to use the box correctly because of those short talks.

逐条结论：

| 任务书声明 | 核对结果 |
|---|---|
| 「第 5 句的 takes everything out」 | **该声明不成立。** 反证：子串 `takes everything out` 在整个 C3-02 材料中出现 **0 次**（全文 `everything` 亦为 0 次）；第 5 句是「the city weighs the whole box」称重句，根本不含任何取物动作。真正的取物句是**第 10 句** `He simply takes each one out...`。 |
| 「第 7 句的 180 公斤」 | **成立。** 第 7 句确为 `His record for one week is a hundred and eighty kilograms`，子串 `hundred and eighty kilograms` 实测出现 1 次。 |
| 「上一轮只改了第 2 句的表述，把布袋用途锚定在『从箱里取出的错放物』上」 | **成立。** 第 2 句逐字为 `the bags are for the wrong things he takes out of the box`。 |
| 「第 5 句所称的与第 7 句的 180 公斤正是同一批物」 | **成立。** 第 5 句称的是「整箱重」，第 7 句的 180 公斤即该箱内物一周的重量，二者所指同一批物。 |

**结论：任务书的实质诊断（量级矛盾）成立，只是句号与引文指针写错（应为第 10 句、`takes each one out`）。我按原文实际形态修复，不按任务书的错误指针改。**

### 2.2 原文逐字引用（矛盾所在）

改前，第 42 行第 10 句：

> He simply takes each one out and shows it to its owner at the next gate.

与之并存、构成矛盾的三处：

> 第 1 句：Every Wednesday an old man walks slowly around the block with two cloth bags.
> 第 2 句：He has done this for six years, and the bags are for the wrong things he takes out of the box.
> 第 5 句：The box is often full by the end of the week, and the city weighs the whole box and tells him the number.
> 第 7 句：His record for one week is a hundred and eighty kilograms, while in his first year it was less than fifty.

矛盾机理（我的独立判定）：`takes **each one** out` 字面义为「把**每一个**错放物都取出来」，主语是每周只带两只布袋走一圈的老人。那么箱子将永远不会被装满、也永远不可能一周积到 180 公斤——第 5 句「周末常满」与第 7 句「一周 180 公斤」被第 10 句直接否定，且 180 公斤也无法靠两只布袋分批带走、逐户递给物主。**这是材料内部自相矛盾，属严重—中等边界，本轮按 V-C3 定级为中等处理。**

### 2.3 改动对照（唯一一处）

**旧文本 → 新文本**

> 旧：He simply **takes each one out and shows it to its owner** at the next gate.
> 新：He **takes out only a few wrong things and shows them to their owners** at the next gate.

改动词：`each one out` → `out only a few wrong things`；`it` → `them`；`its owner` → `their owners`。
实测词数：该句 **16 词 → 18 词（净 +2）**。

### 2.5 为什么这样改就闭合了

`only a few`（每趟只取少数几件）取代 `each one`（每一个全取出）后：

1. 第 10 句不再否定第 5 句：他只取走少数，其余错放物留在箱内，箱子得以在周末装满。
2. 第 10 句不再否定第 7 句：既然只取少数，180 公斤一周的积存成立。
3. 量级自洽：两只布袋装「a few」件不再夸张。
4. 第 11 句的 400 户反而更顺：一年 52 趟，每趟少数几件谈话，量大但可行；改前「each one」会推导出他一年内逐件上门 400 次以上，更不合理。**新文本严格优于旧文本，未引入新矛盾。**
5. 时态一致：全篇一般现在时，改后句仍为现在时。
