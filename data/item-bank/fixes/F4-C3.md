## 3. 新答案的两句依据（逐字引用）

答案 B `He would rather talk to people than simply get angry.` 由**两个彼此独立的句子**合并推出，两个半命题在材料中**没有任何单句同时具备**：

- 依据二（他去跟物主谈）——第 10 句：`He takes out only a few wrong things and shows them to their owners at the next gate.`
- 佐证（谈话确实奏效，故为「选择谈而非发火」）——第 8 句：`He says this is the most useful thing he has ever done, not because the numbers are important, but because many neighbours still put a paper cup or a broken phone into the red box.`

答案句本身（`would rather ... than simply get angry`）在材料中**无任何近似表述**，不是查词题。
