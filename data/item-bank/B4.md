## B4-01 调课
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Zhou, I wonder if I can change my Wednesday English lesson.
Ms. Zhou: What's the trouble?
Boy: Our science club meets in Room 302 at half past four. Mr. Li is the club teacher, and I joined the club in the first week of this term. So I've left your class five minutes early ever since.
Ms. Zhou: Only five minutes?
Boy: Five minutes isn't much, but we clean the room for ten minutes after the club. It's only a five-minute walk to the bus stop, so I get there at a quarter to five. Still, the quarter-to-five bus often leaves without me. I missed it twice last month.
Ms. Zhou: Did you speak to Mr. Li?
Boy: Yes, I spoke to him after school. He said another group needs Room 302 on Wednesdays, so from next month the club will meet on Thursday afternoon instead.
Ms. Zhou: Is Thursday afternoon free for you?
Boy: On Thursdays I only have PE and art, so the club won't be in the way.
Ms. Zhou: Then there's no need to change your lesson. Come on Wednesdays as usual and go to the club on Thursdays.
**Q1**
When will the science club meet next month?
- A. At half past four on Wednesday afternoon.
- B. At four o'clock on Thursday afternoon.
- C. At half past four on Thursday afternoon.
- D. At half past three on Thursday afternoon.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
Why does the boy often miss the quarter-to-five bus?
- A. Because the walk to the bus stop takes a long time.
- B. Because he cleans the club room for ten minutes.
- C. Because his English lesson ends five minutes late.
- D. Because Mr. Li asks him to stay for questions.
**Answer** B

## B4-02 值日名单上的名字
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Liu, could you look at the new duty list with me? I can't find my name.
Ms. Liu: Let me see. Here it is. Group Three is on duty next Tuesday, and your name is on the list.
Girl: I have a hospital appointment next Tuesday afternoon. My mother booked it last Friday, so I can't stay after school.
Ms. Liu: I see. Under the new rule, you must find another student in the group to take your place.
Girl: I asked this morning, but two girls in our group are going to music class, and the three boys are playing in the school football match.
Ms. Liu: All five busy? You have two choices: clean the classroom before the first lesson on Tuesday morning, or ask Mr. Xu, the head teacher, to sign the list.
Girl: My bus only gets me here at ten past eight, so I cannot clean the classroom before eight.
Ms. Liu: Then I'll move your name to Friday's line. You'll clean with Group One, and Mr. Xu doesn't need to sign anything.
Girl: Thank you, Ms. Liu. I'll tell our group monitor tonight.
**Q1**
What will the other students in the girl's group do next Tuesday afternoon?
- A. Go to the hospital appointment in the afternoon.
- B. Clean the classroom before the first lesson on Tuesday morning.
- C. Tell the group monitor about the new duty list.
- D. Go to music class or play in a football match.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
How will the girl's duty be settled?
- A. She will clean the classroom with Group One on Friday.
- B. She will ask Mr. Xu, the head teacher, to sign it.
- C. She will clean the classroom on Tuesday morning instead.
- D. She will do the duty for one of the other students.
**Answer** A

## B4-03 候补名单
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Huang, I was ill last Friday, and now I can't find the photography club list.
Ms. Huang: The list was closed last Friday. Twenty-four students put their names down, but the club has room for only eighteen.
Boy: Then I can't join this term, can I?
Ms. Huang: Two students changed their minds yesterday, so two names came off the list. That leaves four students still waiting for places.
Boy: Will you tell me the result today?
Ms. Huang: Not today. The club teacher decides on Monday morning, and the new names go up on the door at once.
Boy: Monday? I thought the new list would go up on Tuesday.
Ms. Huang: It goes up on Monday. The sports meeting is on Tuesday, so nothing changes. The club meets on Wednesday afternoons, and new members come to Room 105 at four o'clock.
Boy: May I borrow a school camera if I get in?
Ms. Huang: Of course. Then you must sign your name in the book by the door. Cameras have to be back before five, because the room is locked at five.
Boy: Thank you. I'll check the list on Monday.
**Q1**
When will the boy learn the result of the sign-up?
- A. On Friday afternoon.
- B. On Tuesday morning.
- C. On Monday morning.
- D. On Wednesday afternoon.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
What must the boy do if he borrows a school camera?
- A. Sign his name in the book by the door.
- B. Come to Room 105 at four o'clock on Wednesday.
- C. Check the new list on Monday morning.
- D. Go to the sports meeting on Tuesday.
**Answer** A

## B4-04 家长会改期
**Dialogue**
Mr. Zhao: Ms. Sun, I got a note about the parents' meeting, but I can't come on Friday evening.
Ms. Sun: I'm sorry to hear that. Why?
Mr. Zhao: I drive a taxi, and on Fridays I often don't finish until seven o'clock.
Ms. Sun: The meeting starts at half past four and usually ends about six, so some parents come late.
Mr. Zhao: Coming late isn't my problem. My passengers wait at the station, and I can't leave my car.
Ms. Sun: Then what can I do for you?
Mr. Zhao: Could I come at six instead, or could it be moved to another day?
Ms. Sun: I'm afraid it cannot be moved, because the head teacher is away until Tuesday. But there's another way. On Monday morning at eight, I'll show the same slides in my classroom.
Mr. Zhao: Will you talk about the same things?
Ms. Sun: Yes, the same slides, but in smaller groups, so everyone gets time for questions.
Mr. Zhao: Then I'll come on Monday. How will I know my turn?
Ms. Sun: I'll put a list on my door on Friday afternoon, and you can telephone the office before eight.
**Q1**
Why can't Mr. Zhao come to the meeting on Friday evening?
- A. He is waiting for the head teacher to come back on Tuesday.
- B. He is still driving his taxi at seven o'clock.
- C. He has to telephone the office before eight o'clock.
- D. He has to wait for parents who come late to the meeting.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What will Ms. Sun show the parents on Monday morning?
- A. She will put a list on her door on Friday afternoon.
- B. She will move the meeting to Tuesday morning.
- C. She will let the parents telephone the office before eight o'clock.
- D. She will show them the same slides.
**Answer** D

## B4-05 三小时的零工
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wu, our football kit costs eighty yuan, and my parents can't pay for it this term.
Ms. Wu: I'm sorry to hear that. How will you get the money?
Boy: My aunt has a bakery near the school and can give me a job on Saturdays. She needs me from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, so six hours, at fifteen yuan an hour.
Ms. Wu: That could be a problem. During term time our school rule says students may work only at weekends, and no more than three hours a week.
Boy: Three hours? Then I could work from eleven to two on Saturday, after football practice from nine to half past ten.
Ms. Wu: That is exactly three hours. But you need your parents' permission in writing, and the form must stay at the school office.
Boy: Three hours at fifteen yuan is only forty-five yuan. I still need thirty-five yuan.
Ms. Wu: You're right. But the school has a fund for sports kits, and I'll write you a letter for the head teacher.
Boy: Thank you, Ms. Wu. I'll ask my aunt to keep the Saturday job.
**Q1**
How much can the boy earn in one Saturday under the school rule?
- A. Forty-five yuan.
- B. Sixty yuan.
- C. Ninety yuan.
- D. Fifteen yuan.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
What must the boy get before he can take the Saturday job?
- A. Money from the school's sports kit fund.
- B. A letter for the head teacher.
- C. His parents' permission in writing.
- D. A form from the head teacher.
**Answer** C

## B4-06 请假两天
**Dialogue**
Mr. Wu: Ms. Deng, I hope this is a good moment to talk about my son's lessons.
Ms. Deng: Of course, Mr. Wu. Please come in.
Mr. Wu: My family is going to my parents' home on Thursday, and we will stay until Saturday morning.
Ms. Deng: I see. How many lessons will your son miss?
Mr. Wu: He has six lessons on Thursday: Chinese, history, music and art in the morning, and only the last two, maths and physics, in the afternoon. We will leave school at half past three.
Ms. Deng: Then he will miss two lessons, with no homework for them. I'll ask the class monitor to give him the homework sheet on Wednesday.
Mr. Wu: That's kind of you. What about the notes of the two lessons?
Ms. Deng: I'll lend him my notes, and he can use the study room at lunchtime on Monday.
Mr. Wu: Monday lunchtime. Then he doesn't need to come back on Saturday?
Ms. Deng: Right, don't send him back on Saturday; the classrooms are locked and the study room is closed that day.
Mr. Wu: Very kind of you. I'll telephone the office on Monday.
**Q1**
Which two subjects will the boy miss on Thursday afternoon?
- A. Music and art.
- B. Maths and physics.
- C. Chinese and history.
- D. History and physics.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
How will the boy get the notes of the two lessons?
- A. The class monitor will give him the homework sheet on Wednesday.
- B. He will study in the study room at lunchtime on Monday.
- C. He will get the notes when he comes back to school on Saturday.
- D. Ms. Deng will lend him her notes on Monday.
**Answer** D
