> A15 家庭安全与校园日常（15 题）

> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。

## A15-01 阳台的花两天浇一次水

**Dialogue**
Boy: Grandma, my flowers look dry. Shall I water them today?
Grandma: I water them every two days. Not every day.
Boy: Every three days, or once a week?
Grandma: Two days, not a week. Water them before breakfast.

**Question**
How often should the boy water the flowers?
- A. Every day.
- B. Once a week.
- C. Every two days.
- D. Every three days.

**Answer** C

## A15-02 水龙头滴水先关总闸

**Dialogue**
Boy: Dad, the tap in the bathroom drips all night.
Dad: Turn off the main switch under the sink first.
Boy: Shall I buy a new tap, or put a towel under it?
Dad: No towel. It still drips. Call the plumber and send him a video.

**Question**
What should the boy do first?
- A. Turn off the main switch.
- B. Buy a new tap.
- C. Put a towel under it.
- D. Send a video to the plumber.

**Answer** A

## A15-03 作文要写什么体裁

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hou, our English writing task is due on Friday. What should I write about?
Mr. Hou: A letter to a pen friend, not a diary page.
Boy: A diary page is easier for me.
Mr. Hou: Easier, but the marks want a letter. Start with Dear and keep it to one page.

**Question**
What will the boy write?
- A. A diary page.
- B. A letter to a pen friend.
- C. A poem.
- D. A short report.

**Answer** B

## A15-04 牛奶要放哪一层

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Qi, the milk is warm. Which shelf should it go on?
Mr. Qi: The second shelf from the top, not the door side.
Boy: My father put it on the bottom last time.
Mr. Qi: That is why it went bad. Take it out at night.

**Question**
Where should the boy put the milk?
- A. On the bottom shelf.
- B. On the door side.
- C. On the second shelf from the top.
- D. On the top shelf.

**Answer** C

## A15-05 老年食堂周日不开门

**Dialogue**
Boy: Grandpa, shall we eat at the old people's canteen on Sunday?
Grandpa: It is closed on Sunday. Your mother is coming at twelve.
Boy: Then shall I buy lunch at the shop?
Grandpa: No need. I cook at half past eleven. We eat together at twelve.

**Question**
What will Grandpa do at half past eleven on Sunday?
- A. Take the boy to the old people's canteen.
- B. Cook the lunch at home.
- C. Buy lunch at the shop.
- D. Cook the lunch at twelve.

**Answer** B

## A15-06 写作比赛最多写三百字

**Dialogue**
Mr. Ma: The writing contest is next Monday. Read the notice.
Girl: How many words can I write?
Mr. Ma: No more than three hundred. Five hundred is too many.
Girl: So one hundred words is too few?
Mr. Ma: Two hundred is fine. Three hundred is the limit.

**Question**
How many words at most can the girl write?
- A. One hundred.
- B. Two hundred.
- C. Three hundred.
- D. Five hundred.

**Answer** C

## A15-07 图书角的新书谁登记

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wu, four new books are on the shelf today.
Mr. Wu: Who writes them in the book corner book?
Girl: The monitor did it last term. Shall I do it now?
Mr. Wu: No. Ms. Hu keeps that book. I only keep the reading list.

**Question**
Who writes the new books in the book corner book?
- A. Ms. Hu.
- B. The monitor.
- C. The girl.
- D. Mr. Wu.

**Answer** A

## A15-08 寒假读书每天二十分钟

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Bai, I read for half an hour, but it's only cartoons.
Ms. Bai: How many minutes of a real book?
Boy: Ten minutes, I think. An hour is too long.
Ms. Bai: Twenty minutes is enough. Do it at the same time each day.

**Question**
How long should the boy read a real book each day?
- A. Ten minutes.
- B. Twenty minutes.
- C. Half an hour.
- D. An hour.

**Answer** B

## A15-09 钓鱼六点半出发

**Dialogue**
Dad: Tomorrow is Saturday. We are going fishing at the lake.
Boy: Shall we set out at six, or at seven?
Dad: At half past six. It takes an hour to get there.
Boy: Shall I bring my hat?
Dad: Bring a hat and some water. We come back at ten.

**Question**
What time will they set out on Saturday?
- A. At six o'clock.
- B. At seven o'clock.
- C. At ten o'clock.
- D. At half past six.

**Answer** D

## A15-10 班会讲故事讲五分钟

**Dialogue**
Mr. Qi: The class meeting is at three on Monday. How long can you talk?
Girl: Can we talk for ten minutes?
Mr. Qi: Ten is too long. Five for the story, two for the questions.
Girl: Half an hour for our group, then?
Mr. Qi: Half an hour is for the whole class.

**Question**
How long can the girl's group talk about its story?
- A. Five minutes.
- B. Two minutes.
- C. Ten minutes.
- D. Half an hour.

**Answer** A

## A15-11 洗照片四点去取

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Song, how long do photo prints take?
Ms. Song: One hour at the shop near the cinema.
Boy: Then at one, or at six after my dinner?
Ms. Song: Come at four. The shop closes at five, and at six it is dark.

**Question**
When should the boy come back for the photos?
- A. At one o'clock.
- B. At six o'clock.
- C. At four o'clock.
- D. At five o'clock.

**Answer** C

## A15-12 体育课穿哪双鞋

**Dialogue**
Mr. Fu: Tomorrow's PE class is on the playground, not in the gym.
Boy: Shall I wear my new shoes?
Mr. Fu: No. New ones are too narrow.
Boy: What about my old canvas ones, or my running shoes?
Mr. Fu: The canvas pair is flat. Take the running shoes, not leather ones.

**Question**
Which shoes should the boy wear for the PE class?
- A. His new shoes.
- B. His running shoes.
- C. His canvas shoes.
- D. His leather shoes.

## A15-13 三盒彩笔二十四元

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, I want three boxes of crayons for our art class.
Mr. Guo: One box is eight yuan.
Girl: Two boxes would be sixteen. I have twenty yuan.
Mr. Guo: Three boxes are twenty-four, and twenty is not enough.
Girl: What about thirty-two?
Mr. Guo: No. Three boxes for your class.

**Question**
How much do the three boxes of crayons cost?
- A. Twenty-four yuan.
- B. Sixteen yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Thirty-two yuan.

**Answer** A

## A15-14 周日素描课八点半开始

**Dialogue**
Ms. Zheng: Are you going to the Children's Palace this Sunday?
Boy: Yes. My sketch class begins at nine.
Ms. Zheng: The gates open at eight, but your class is half past eight.
Boy: Then not at nine? Shall I go at ten?
Ms. Zheng: Take your drawing board. Ten is too late.

**Question**
What time does the boy's sketch class begin this Sunday?
- A. At nine o'clock.
- B. At ten o'clock.
- C. At eight o'clock.
- D. At half past eight.

**Answer** D

## A15-15 月底自己抄水电表

**Dialogue**
Ms. Sun: The water office sends someone to read our meter.
Boy: When, Mum? I never see anyone.
Ms. Sun: On the last day of the month, before six.
Boy: Shall I leave the number on the door for them?
Ms. Sun: Read it yourself on Friday. We pay online, not in cash.

**Question**
What should the boy do on Friday?
- A. Leave the number on the door.
- B. Wait at home for the office worker.
- C. Read the meter himself.
- D. Pay the water bill in cash.

**Answer** C
