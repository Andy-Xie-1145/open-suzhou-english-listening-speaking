## A29-01 续借图书的次数
**Dialogue**
Ms. He: You may renew a book twice.
Girl: I have already renewed this one once.
Ms. He: Then once is left.
Girl: May I renew it if somebody else has asked for it?
Ms. He: No. Give it back on Friday.
Girl: Friday it is.
**Question**
How many times can the girl still renew the book?
- A. Once.
- B. Twice.
- C. Three times.
- D. Not at all.
**Answer** A

## A29-02 水果摊的分档计价
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Lu, I need four kilos of oranges.
Ms. Lu: The first three kilos are four yuan a kilo. After that it is two yuan.
Boy: So twelve plus two?
Ms. Lu: Four kilos is fourteen yuan. Twelve for the first three, two for the last.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for the oranges?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Sixteen yuan.
- C. Eight yuan.
- D. Fourteen yuan.
**Answer** D

## A29-03 药店的糖浆瓶数
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fang, how many small spoons do I need?
Mr. Fang: One twice a day, and I need it for six days.
Girl: Twelve small spoons? Then how many bottles?
Mr. Fang: Each bottle has five small spoons in it. Buy three bottles.
Girl: And no more than that?
Mr. Fang: No more.
**Question**
How many bottles will the girl buy?
- A. Two.
- B. Three.
- C. Five.
- D. Twelve.
**Answer** B

## A29-04 眼镜店验光预约
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Tan, do I need an appointment?
Ms. Tan: Yes, we are full from ten on Saturday. Come at half past nine.
Girl: My brother needs one too?
Ms. Tan: One person per twenty minutes. He can come at ten, right after you.
Girl: More for two people?
Ms. Tan: No. Forty yuan each.
**Question**
At what time will the girl's brother have his eye test?
- A. At half past nine.
- B. At nine.
- C. At ten.
- D. At ten thirty.
**Answer** C

## A29-05 修钟表的报价
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Qian, how much to mend a wall clock?
Mr. Qian: Twenty-five yuan if it only needs a new spring.
Boy: Mine needs a new spring.
Mr. Qian: Then twenty-five. I do not charge for the parts.
Boy: How long will it take?
Mr. Qian: A day. I open at nine and close at six.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for the repair?
- A. Twenty-five yuan.
- B. Sixty yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Forty yuan.
**Answer** A

## A29-06 租自行车的押金
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, how much is the deposit for a bicycle?
Mr. Bai: Fifty yuan. It comes back when you return it.
Girl: I want it for two hours.
Mr. Bai: Still fifty. By the hour: four for the first hour, two after it.
Girl: Two hours would be six yuan.
Mr. Bai: Six yuan, and fifty yuan back.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay for the two hours, after the fifty yuan deposit comes back?
- A. Six yuan.
- B. Fifty-six yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Two yuan.
**Answer** A

## A29-07 衣柜挂衣杆的长度
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wu, how long a rail do I need inside the wardrobe?
Ms. Wu: The wardrobe is ninety centimetres wide. The rail must be ten centimetres shorter, or the doors will not close.
Boy: Then eighty. Is that long enough for my coats?
Ms. Wu: Yes. Anything under seventy will not hang straight.
Boy: I will measure again.
**Question**
How long a rail will the boy buy?
- A. Ninety centimetres.
- B. Seventy centimetres.
- C. Eighty centimetres.
- D. Sixty centimetres.
**Answer** C

## A29-08 买猫粮要几袋
**Dialogue**
Girl: How many days does a one-kilo bag last?
Mr. Wu: Sixty grams twice a day, so a hundred and twenty a day.
Girl: And a kilo is a thousand grams.
Mr. Wu: Yes.
Girl: So one bag is a little over eight days.
Mr. Wu: Right, and an opened bag must be used inside a month.
**Question**
How many bags will the girl need for one month?
- A. Three.
- B. Two.
- C. Five.
- D. Four.
**Answer** D

## A29-09 换纱窗的钱
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, how much is it to have new screens put in?
Mr. Yu: Twenty yuan a window, and the material is included.
Girl: We have four windows.
Mr. Yu: The kitchen one is half price, so seventy yuan.
Girl: Can you do it next week?
Mr. Yu: Yes, on Saturday morning.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay for all four windows?
- A. Eighty yuan.
- B. Seventy yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Ninety yuan.
**Answer** B

## A29-10 陶艺课的费用
**Dialogue**
Girl: How much is one pottery class?
Mr. Pan: Twenty yuan an hour, and a class is two hours.
Girl: I only have an hour on Saturday.
Mr. Pan: Then it is twenty yuan, but you will only get half a bowl.
Girl: I will pay for two hours.
Mr. Pan: The clay is five yuan extra, not included.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay in all?
- A. Forty-five yuan.
- B. Forty yuan.
- C. Twenty-five yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** A

## A29-11 买年历送邻居
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, how much is the wall calendar for next year?
Mr. Guo: Twelve yuan each. Two for twenty.
Girl: I need one for my grandmother and one for the neighbours.
Mr. Guo: Then two, and it is twenty, not twenty-four.
Girl: Shall I bring the money tomorrow?
Mr. Guo: Yes, or pay on the spot.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Twenty-four yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Ten yuan.
**Answer** C

## A29-12 修剪阳台的树
**Dialogue**
Girl: My balcony tree has three long branches.
Mr. Sun: Cut them to twenty centimetres. A balcony tree should be a little above the rail.
Girl: The rail is a metre high and the tree is a metre forty.
Mr. Sun: Then take twenty centimetres off the top, not thirty.
Girl: Will it grow back?
Mr. Sun: It will.
**Question**
How tall will the tree be after the cut?
- A. One metre ten.
- B. One metre.
- C. One metre thirty.
- D. One metre twenty.
**Answer** D

## A29-13 电风扇的噪音
**Dialogue**
Boy: My old fan is very loud at setting two.
Ms. Gu: Is there dust behind the back?
Boy: I have never looked.
Ms. Gu: Take the back off and clean it. A loud fan is full of dust.
Boy: Is setting one enough?
Ms. Gu: Setting one is a breeze. Clean it before you buy a new one.
**Question**
What will the boy do first?
- A. Take the back off and clean it.
- B. Use setting two instead.
- C. Buy a new fan.
- D. Take the fan to a shop.
**Answer** A

## A29-14 存钱买足球
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, I want to buy a football.
Mr. Gu: How much is it?
Boy: Ninety yuan.
Mr. Gu: Put aside five yuan a week and do not touch it.
Boy: Five a week is a lot of weeks.
Mr. Gu: A year is fifty-two weeks, not a hundred. Divide, do not guess.
**Question**
About how many weeks will the boy need?
- A. Sixteen weeks.
- B. Eighteen weeks.
- C. Twenty weeks.
- D. Ninety weeks.
**Answer** B

## A29-15 缩水衬衫的退换
**Dialogue**
Girl: My new shirt shrank in the wash.
Ms. Pan: Did you wash it in hot water?
Girl: I followed the label; it says thirty.
Ms. Pan: It is not your fault, and it cannot be made bigger.
Girl: Shall I cut the sleeves?
Ms. Pan: Take it back to the shop within seven days, with the receipt.
**Question**
What will the girl do now?
- A. Wash it again in hot water.
- B. Cut the sleeves shorter.
- C. Take it back to the shop within seven days.
- D. Iron it for a longer time.
**Answer** C
