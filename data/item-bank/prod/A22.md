> A22 社区与邻里办事（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A22-01 健身器材九点关

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, when do the exercise machines stop?
Mr. Guo: At nine. They are locked before nine, not after.
Girl: Can I use them on Sunday morning?
Mr. Guo: Yes, from seven. Sunday is the only day it opens early.

**Question**
What time will the girl have to stop using the machines?
- A. At seven.
- B. At nine.
- C. At ten.
- D. After nine.

**Answer** B

## A22-02 单元门要刷卡

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hu, the door by the gate is open.
Mr. Hu: Use your card at the main door, not that side door.
Boy: My card is in my bag. Is that all right?
Mr. Hu: Yes, take it out first. The side door has no reader.

**Question**
Where should the boy use his card?
- A. At the side door.
- B. At the gate.
- C. On the second floor.
- D. At the main door.

**Answer** D

## A22-03 早市七点开门

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Sun, when does the morning market open?
Ms. Sun: At seven. It closes at eleven, not at noon.
Girl: Is it open on Sunday?
Ms. Sun: Yes, every day except Monday.

**Question**
When does the morning market open?
- A. At seven.
- B. At six.
- C. At eight.
- D. At noon.

**Answer** A

## A22-04 办老年证要两张照片

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Lu, what do I need for my grandmother's card?
Mr. Lu: Two photos and the old card. One photo is not enough.
Boy: Is there anything to pay?
Mr. Lu: The photo costs ten yuan. The card itself is free.

**Question**
How many photos does the boy need?
- A. One.
- B. Three.
- C. Two.
- D. Four.

**Answer** C

## A22-05 打疫苗要带健康本

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Ren, do I need anything for the flu shot?
Ms. Ren: Take your health book, not the old card. The nurse writes in it.
Girl: Is there a charge?
Ms. Ren: No, it is free. The shot is given in the hall, not outside.

**Question**
What should the girl take to the flu shot?
- A. Her old card.
- B. Her health book.
- C. Some money.
- D. Her umbrella.

**Answer** B

## A22-06 活动室最多两小时

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hao, can our group book the activity room this afternoon?
Mr. Hao: Yes, but only two hours. Three hours is too long.
Boy: We want to start at two.
Mr. Hao: Then you must be out at four. The room closes at five, but your time ends at four.

**Question**
How long can the boy use the activity room?
- A. Two hours.
- B. Three hours.
- C. Five hours.
- D. One hour.

**Answer** A

## A22-07 居委会开会改周四

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wang, when is the residents' meeting?
Mr. Wang: On Thursday at seven in the evening, not on Tuesday.
Girl: Do I need to bring my number card?
Mr. Wang: Just come. Don't sign the list at the door.

**Question**
When is the residents' meeting?
- A. On Tuesday.
- B. At seven in the morning.
- C. On Wednesday.
- D. On Thursday at seven in the evening.

**Answer** D

## A22-08 旧衣服送到回收点

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Kong, my old coats are too small. Where do they go?
Ms. Kong: Take them to the recycling point on Friday, not to the bin.
Boy: Do I have to pay anything?
Ms. Kong: No. Leave them in the green box by the gate.

**Question**
Where should the boy take his old coats?
- A. To the rubbish bin.

## A22-09 关东煮晚上才有

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Long, can I have the hot food now?
Mr. Long: The hot pot is ready only in the evening, not at noon.
Girl: What time does it start?

**Question**
When is the hot food ready?

Boy: Mr. Gao, what should I take to the free clinic?

## A22-11 草地不能踩要绕行

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Gu, the short way goes over the grass.
Mr. Gu: Walk round by the trees. The grass is not a path.
Girl: Is it only in summer?
Mr. Gu: All year. The sign by the gate says keep off the grass.

**Question**
How should the girl walk across the square?
- A. Over the grass.
- B. Through the flower bed.
- C. Along the sign.
- D. Around by the trees.

**Answer** D

## A22-12 信报箱要用小钥匙

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Tong, my letter box is full and I can't open it.
Mr. Tong: You need the small key from the post office, not a knife.
Boy: Is the post office open now?
Mr. Tong: From half past one to five. The security man has no key, so don't ask him.

**Question**
How should the boy open his letter box?
- A. With a knife.
- B. With the small key from the post office.
- C. By asking the postman to force it.
- D. By asking the security man for his key.

**Answer** B

## A22-13 保安要查出入证

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ni, can I walk into the building without my pass?
Mr. Ni: No. Show the pass at the desk. Call your host from there.
Boy: Can I ring the bell of his flat?
Mr. Ni: No, that wakes the family up. Use the desk phone.

**Question**
What should the boy do at the gate?
- A. Ring his friend's doorbell.
- B. Walk in without his pass.
- C. Show his pass at the desk.
- D. Wait outside until his friend comes down.

**Answer** C

## A22-14 早市的秤要复称

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zheng, is this stall's scale honest?
Mr. Zheng: Some stalls are short. Weigh it again at the desk by the gate.
Girl: Does that cost anything?
Mr. Zheng: No. Then pay the right money, not the small sum.

**Question**
What should the girl do with the vegetables?
- A. Weigh them again at the desk by the gate.
- B. Buy them at once and leave.
- C. Pay the small sum the stall asks for.
- D. Ask the stall owner to add one kilo.

**Answer** A

## A22-15 讲座要先签到

**Dialogue**

Mr. Xie: Sign in at the desk first. Without a name card they send you out.

**Question**
What should the girls do before the talk?
- A. Come back at three.
