> A12 居家、社区与课外活动（15 题）

> 来源：并行子任务产出｜共 15 份 / 15 题｜A 类：一段短对话配 1 道四选一题

> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本型呈现结构（极短对话 + 1 题四选一）依据公开材料推定，**结构未经官方证实**。

## A12-01 洗衣店取衣不是周六
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. He, my blue coat is at the laundry. When can I get it?
Ms. He: We wash clothes on Tuesday. Pick it up on Friday, not on Saturday.
Girl: What time on Friday? I leave school at four.
Ms. He: Not at four. Come at five. We close at six.
**Question**
When will the girl pick up her coat?
- A. At four on Friday afternoon.
- B. On Tuesday.
- C. On Saturday.
- D. At five on Friday afternoon.
**Answer** D

## A12-02 配眼镜一共六十元
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Dai, I need glasses for the maths test. How much will they cost?
Mr. Dai: The blue frames are eighty yuan. The black ones are fifty.
Boy: Eighty is too much for me. Which ones do you advise?
Mr. Dai: Take the black ones. Glass lenses cost ten yuan, so sixty in all.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for his glasses?
- A. Eighty yuan.
- B. Sixty yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Ten yuan.
**Answer** B

## A12-03 阳台的花三天浇一次
**Dialogue**
Girl: Dad, the leaves of my plant on the balcony are yellow. What should I do?
Dad: Water it today. The soil is dry, not wet. Don't move it at all.
Girl: How often should I water it?
Dad: Every three days. Not every day, or the roots rot.
**Question**
What should the girl do today?
- A. Move the plant into the sun.
- B. Water it every day.
- C. Water the plant once today.
- D. Move the plant into her room.
**Answer** C

## A12-04 停水从九点停到五点
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Guo, the notice says water is off all day. Is that right?
Mr. Guo: Not all day. It stops at nine and comes back at five, not at two.
Boy: My mother cooks at six. Will she have water?
Mr. Guo: Yes. Fill two buckets with cold water before nine.
**Question**
How long will the water be off?
- A. From nine in the morning to five in the evening.
- B. All day.
- C. From nine in the morning to two in the afternoon.
- D. From two in the afternoon to five in the evening.
**Answer** A

## A12-05 水管漏水报给物业
**Dialogue**

Girl: My father is at work, and my neighbour in 503 fixed my bike.
Ms. Qian: Neither can fix a pipe. Leave it with the office. Room 502.
**Question**
Who should the girl tell about the leak?
- A. The shop downstairs.
- B. Her father at work.
- C. Her neighbour in room 503.
- D. The property office.
**Answer** D

## A12-06 乒乓球台两小时六十元
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Fu, how much is one hour at a table?
Mr. Fu: Twenty yuan an hour on weekdays. On Saturday it is thirty.
Boy: We want two hours on Saturday. That is sixty, right?
Mr. Fu: Right. But half an hour is ten yuan.
**Question**
How much will the boys pay for two hours on Saturday?
- A. Twenty yuan.
- B. Thirty yuan.
- C. Sixty yuan.
- D. Ten yuan.
**Answer** C

## A12-07 手机没电在他家充
**Dialogue**

Mr. Ren: Don't buy a new phone. Use my charger here.
Girl: But I need it for the maths test on Friday.
Mr. Ren: Charge it at my home tonight, not at school. Bring it back on Saturday.

## A12-08 墙报上我写标题和日期

## A12-11 这月电费二百四
**Dialogue**
Girl: Dad, the bill says we used two hundred degrees this month.
Dad: The air conditioner used a hundred and forty. What about the rest?

Dad: Then we pay two hundred and forty yuan. Last month it was three hundred.
**Question**
How much will the family pay for the electricity this month?
- A. Two hundred yuan.
- B. Two hundred and forty yuan.
- C. Sixty yuan.
- D. Three hundred yuan.

## A12-12 压岁钱捐五十元

## A12-15 宠物店洗澡留两小时
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Pei, my dog needs a bath. How long will he stay at the pet shop?
Ms. Pei: He goes in at twelve and comes out at two.
Girl: Can I wait for him there?
Ms. Pei: The bath takes one hour, but he rests in a box until two. We close at nine.
**Question**
How long will the dog stay at the pet shop?
- A. One hour.
- B. From nine in the morning until two.
- C. Two hours.
- D. From twelve until one.
**Answer** C
