> A28 购物与居家细节（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A28-01 看成分标签才知道缩不缩

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Fu, how do I know if this shirt will shrink?
Ms. Fu: Read the label inside. Cotton shrinks, pure linen does not.
Girl: The label says sixty percent cotton. Is that bad?
Ms. Fu: Ask for a hundred percent linen if you can wash it often.

**Question**
What should the girl do to find out about the shirt?
- A. Wash it at home first.
- B. Read the label inside the shirt.
- C. Buy two and keep the better one.
- D. Wait until it shrinks.

**Answer** B

## A28-02 退鞋要带盒子和小票

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Dai, these shoes hurt my feet. Can I bring them back?
Mr. Dai: Yes, in seven days. Bring the box and the receipt.
Boy: I lost the receipt. Is the card enough?
Mr. Dai: The card is not enough. Without the receipt there is no refund.

**Question**
What must the boy bring when he returns the shoes?
- A. The shoes only.
- B. The bank card.
- C. A written promise from his mother.
- D. The box and the receipt.

**Answer** D

## A28-03 保修卡放在抽屉第一格

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, I cannot find the warranty card for the kettle.
Mr. Pei: It goes in the top drawer, under the papers. Not in the cupboard.
Girl: How long does the warranty last?
Mr. Pei: Two years. Take the card with you when you go back.

**Question**
Where is the warranty card?
- A. In the top drawer, under the papers.
- B. In the food cupboard.
- C. In the box in the hall.
- D. In the bag at the shop.

**Answer** A

## A28-04 缺货登记预订星期四到

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Huo, the blue size is not on the rail.
Mr. Huo: Write your name in the book. The parcel comes on Thursday.
Girl: Do I pay now?
Mr. Huo: No, not until it arrives. Pay at the door then.

**Question**
What should the girl do about the blue size?
- A. Pay for it at once and wait.
- B. Keep coming back every day.
- C. Write her name in the order book.
- D. Take the white size instead.

**Answer** C

## A28-05 试衣间的门要拉好

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wan, the fitting room door does not close properly.
Mr. Wan: Hold it shut with your foot, or tell me and I will fix the latch.
Girl: Should I keep my bag inside with me?
Mr. Wan: Take it in with you. Leave it outside and it will be taken away.

**Question**
What should the girl do in the fitting room?
- A. Leave her bag outside the door.
- B. Ask the shop assistant to stand at the door.
- C. Choose a different shop.
- D. Take her bag in with her.

**Answer** D

## A28-06 购物车要推回门口那一排

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Cheng, may I leave the trolley in the car park?
Mr. Cheng: No. Push it back to the row by the door.
Boy: What if the row is full?
Mr. Cheng: There is always space in the row. Leave it nowhere else.

**Question**
Where should the boy leave the trolley?
- A. In the middle of the car park.
- B. Back in the row by the door.
- C. At the side rail.
- D. Across the entrance.

**Answer** B

## A28-07 洗澡只能戴橡胶表带

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gao, may I wear my watch in the shower?
Mr. Gao: No. Take it off. The strap is leather and water ruins it.
Boy: What about my sports watch?
Mr. Gao: That one is fine. It is rubber, not leather.

**Question**
Which watch may the boy wear in the shower?
- A. His leather watch.
- B. Both watches.
- C. His sports watch with a rubber strap.
- D. Neither watch.

**Answer** C

## A28-08 鞋柜里鞋头朝外放

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Zhi, I can never find my shoes in the dark.
Mr. Zhi: Put every pair with the toes pointing out. Not the heels out.
Boy: Where do the boots go?
Mr. Zhi: Bottom shelf, toes out as well. One pair per space.

**Question**
How should the boy arrange his shoes?
- A. With the toes pointing out.
- B. With the heels pointing out.
- C. Two pairs in one space.
- D. Under the bed.

**Answer** A

## A28-09 毛巾要分开用两次一洗

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Deng, may I use the same towel for my face and my feet?
Ms. Deng: No. Two towels, one for the face and one for the body.
Girl: Do I wash them often?
Ms. Deng: After two uses. A damp towel grows things you cannot see.

**Question**
What should the girl do about towels?
- A. Use one towel for everything.
- B. Use two, and wash each after two uses.
- C. Use the face towel for the feet too.
- D. Keep them damp near the cooker.

**Answer** B

## A28-10 重书要搬去书架上

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hui, my desk drawer will not close any more.
Mr. Hui: The books are too heavy. Move them to the shelf.
Girl: What can stay in the drawer?
Mr. Hui: Pens, rulers and paper. The dictionaries, the exercise books and the paint box all go to the shelf.

**Question**
What should the girl keep in her desk drawer?
- A. Her history dictionaries.
- B. A pile of exercise books.
- C. The box of water colours.
- D. Pens, rulers and paper.

**Answer** D

## A28-11 螺丝刀要收回工具盒

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Pei, where do I keep the screwdriver after I use it?
Mr. Pei: Back in its box with the other tools. Not loose on the shelf.
Boy: Is the box under the bed?
Mr. Pei: No. On the top shelf, out of the children's reach.

**Question**
Where should the boy keep the screwdriver?
- A. In its box on the top shelf.
- B. Loose on the shelf where he left it.
- C. Under the bed.
- D. In the kitchen drawer with the knives.

**Answer** A

## A28-12 厨房抹布每顿饭后洗

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Tan, my kitchen cloth smells badly.
Mr. Tan: Wash it after every meal, not once a week.
Boy: Should I use hot water?
Mr. Tan: Hot water and soap. Then hang it over the sink, not on the rail.

**Question**
What should the boy do with the kitchen cloth?
- A. Wash it once a week in cold water.
- B. Wash it after every meal in hot water and soap.
- C. Leave it wet on the rail overnight.
- D. Keep it damp near the cooker.

**Answer** B

## A28-13 下水口每个星期六刷

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Luo, the sink takes ten minutes to empty now.
Ms. Luo: The outlet below is half full. Clean it every Saturday.
Girl: What do I use to clean it?
Ms. Luo: A wire brush and hot water, not the chemical cleaner.

**Question**
What should the girl do on Saturday?
- A. Buy a stronger chemical cleaner and use it every Saturday.
- B. Ask a plumber to clean the outlet below the sink.
- C. Clean the outlet below the sink with a wire brush and hot water.
- D. Leave the tap running all night instead of cleaning it.

**Answer** C

## A28-14 玻璃杯有裂纹要扔掉

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Li, this glass has a line across the bottom.
Mr. Li: That's a crack. Do not drink from it.
Girl: Can I cut the bottom off?
Mr. Li: No. Throw it away. Cracks grow while the glass is hot.

**Question**
What should the girl do with the cracked glass?
- A. Throw it away.
- B. Cut the bottom off and use the top.
- C. Fill it with cold water only.
- D. Give it to a friend.

**Answer** A

## A28-15 门厅地垫一个月洗一次

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ding, the mat at our door is always wet.
Mr. Ding: Turn it over each time it dries. The back holds less water.
Girl: Should I wash it?
Mr. Ding: Once a month. Not every week. Then leave it outside to dry.

**Question**
When should the mat be washed?
- A. Every week.
- B. Every day.
- C. Never.
- D. Once a month.

**Answer** D
