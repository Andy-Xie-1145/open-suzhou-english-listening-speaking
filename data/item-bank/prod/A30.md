> A30 居家维修与办事（15 题）
> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本批对话原创编写，未取自任何正式试题。

## A30-01 超市的散装坚果
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qiu, how much are these nuts?
Ms. Qiu: Two yuan fifty a hundred grams. They are loose, so we weigh them.
Boy: The label says three hundred grams.
Ms. Qiu: Seven yuan fifty. A bag of the same nuts is twenty yuan for five hundred grams.
Boy: Then loose costs less.
Ms. Qiu: It does.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for the loose nuts?
- A. Seven yuan fifty.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Twelve yuan.
- D. Two yuan fifty.
**Answer** A

## A30-02 小区宽带的付费
**Dialogue**
Girl: How much is the broadband at home?
Ms. Luo: Thirty a month for the slow one, fifty for the fast one.
Girl: I want the fast one.
Ms. Luo: Pay for six months and the seventh is free, so three hundred.
Girl: And the slow one?
Ms. Luo: A hundred and eighty for six months.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay for the fast one?
- A. A hundred and eighty yuan.
- B. Two hundred and fifty yuan.
- C. Six hundred yuan.
- D. Three hundred yuan.
**Answer** D

## A30-03 社区诊所的取药时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Kong, I had a prescription yesterday.
Ms. Kong: It will be ready at four this afternoon, not at noon.
Girl: Can I come at noon?
Ms. Kong: No. The shop upstairs is closed between twelve and two.
Girl: Then after four.
Ms. Kong: Bring the slip.
**Question**
When can the girl collect the medicine?
- A. At noon.
- B. Tomorrow.
- C. At four in the afternoon.
- D. At two.
**Answer** C

## A30-04 家具城的送货费
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Dai, do you deliver a desk?
Mr. Dai: Delivery is thirty yuan inside the town, sixty outside.
Boy: I live about ten kilometres away.
Mr. Dai: Inside is up to five kilometres. Ten is outside, so sixty.
Boy: Can I collect it myself?
Mr. Dai: Then there is no charge, but you carry it in.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for delivery?
- A. Thirty yuan.
- B. Sixty yuan.
- C. Ninety yuan.
- D. Zero yuan.
**Answer** B

## A30-05 疏通下水道的费用
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wu, the sink does not drain.
Mr. Wu: For a kitchen sink it is forty yuan, and I do not charge if nothing comes out.
Girl: Then it may cost nothing.
Mr. Wu: It may. A coin in the pipe takes ten minutes.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay at most?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Nothing at all.
- C. Eighty yuan.
- D. Forty yuan.
**Answer** D

## A30-06 抽屉滑轨的修理
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yuan, my desk drawer will not close.
Mr. Yuan: The slide is bent. Straightening is twenty yuan; a new slide is forty-five.
Boy: The desk is going to the tip next week.
Mr. Yuan: Then a new slide. I do not straighten one I will not see again.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay?
- A. Twenty yuan.
- B. Forty-five yuan.
- C. Sixty-five yuan.
- D. Twenty-five yuan.
**Answer** B

## A30-07 旧电器的回收
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ma, do you take old fridges?
Mr. Ma: Yes, but only if you bring it to the yard. We pay two yuan a kilo.
Girl: It is thirty kilos.
Mr. Ma: Sixty yuan. A working one is worth more, but that is not our business.
Girl: What about the water inside it?
Mr. Ma: Empty it.
**Question**
How much will the girl get for her old fridge?
- A. Sixty yuan.
- B. Thirty yuan.
- C. Ninety yuan.
- D. Two yuan.
**Answer** A

## A30-08 楼顶水箱的清洗
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Guo, when is the water tank on the roof cleaned?
Ms. Guo: Once a year, in the last week of June.
Boy: Our water was cloudy last week.
Ms. Guo: Then next month, not this week. It takes three hours.
**Question**
When will the tank be cleaned?
- A. This week.
- B. Next month.
- C. In the last week of June.
- D. In two weeks.
**Answer** B

## A30-09 给猫剪指甲
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Chen, can you cut my cat's claws here?
Mr. Chen: Yes, fifteen yuan. I do it on the table, not on the floor.
Girl: Will my cat be cross?
Mr. Chen: Some of them are. One claw takes about a minute.
Girl: Then about ten minutes in all.
Mr. Chen: Bring a towel, not a carrier.
**Question**
How long will the work take?
- A. About one minute.
- B. About five minutes.
- C. About half an hour.
- D. About ten minutes.
**Answer** D

## A30-10 会议室能坐几个人
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Yu, how many people fit in the meeting room?
Ms. Yu: Twelve at the table, and six more on chairs at the back.
Boy: Our club has fifteen.
Ms. Yu: Then all fifteen fit, with three chairs to spare. The hall holds forty.
**Question**
How many people can sit in the meeting room?
- A. Twelve people.
- B. Eighteen people.
- C. Fifteen people.
- D. Forty people.
**Answer** B

## A30-11 雨伞的大小
**Dialogue**
Girl: How big is the largest umbrella you have?
Ms. Wu: One metre ten, and it weighs half a kilo. That one is for two people.
Girl: I want one for my schoolbag.
Ms. Wu: Then take the ninety-centimetre one. It weighs a quarter of a kilo.
Girl: How much is it?
Ms. Wu: Thirty-five yuan.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay?
- A. Thirty-five yuan.
- B. Fifteen yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Seventy yuan.
**Answer** A

## A30-12 烧水壶的水位
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. He, how much water should I put in the kettle?
Ms. He: To the line. The kettle is one and a half litres.
Boy: I want two cups of tea.
Ms. He: Two cups are about a third of it. Fill it once for both of us.
**Question**
How much water does the boy need for two cups?
- A. Two litres.
- B. The whole kettle.
- C. A third of the kettle.
- D. Half the kettle.
**Answer** C

## A30-13 电池的充电时长
**Dialogue**
Girl: How long does this battery pack take to charge?
Mr. Liu: Four hours from empty, and about two from half.
Girl: I want it for tomorrow's trip, so it is empty now.
Mr. Liu: Then put it on at nine and it will be ready at one.
**Question**
At what time will the battery pack be ready?
- A. At one in the morning.
- B. At four in the morning.
- C. At nine at night.
- D. At eleven at night.
**Answer** A

## A30-14 楼下便利店的关店时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhou, when do you close?
Mr. Zhou: Eleven, every night except Sunday, when I close at six.
Girl: I need milk at ten.
Mr. Zhou: Come before ten, because at ten I put things away.
Girl: Today is Sunday.
Mr. Zhou: Then come before six, not at ten.
**Question**
At what time should the girl come today?
- A. At ten at night.
- B. At eleven at night.
- C. Before ten at night.
- D. Before six in the evening.
**Answer** D

## A30-15 冰箱的除霜
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, my fridge is making a noise again.
Mr. Gu: How old is it?
Boy: Nine years. The ice box is thick.
Mr. Gu: Then defrost it. Unplug it, leave the door open, and put towels in.
Boy: How long does it take?
Mr. Gu: Four hours, and not in the sun. A sunny window makes it worse.
**Question**
How long will defrosting take?
- A. Two hours.
- B. Nine hours.
- C. Four hours.
- D. One hour.
**Answer** C
