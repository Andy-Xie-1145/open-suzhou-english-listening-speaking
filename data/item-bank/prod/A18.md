> A18 安全与求助（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A18-01 校车坐过站

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Fang, I fell asleep and the school bus went past my stop.
Ms. Fang: Don't worry. The bus turns round at the next stop.
Boy: Shall I walk back from there?
Ms. Fang: No. Get off there and wait. It comes back at half past four.

**Question**
What should the boy do when the bus goes past his stop?
- A. Get off at the next stop and wait for the bus back.
- B. Get off at the next stop and walk back to his stop.
- C. Stay on the bus and ride to the school gate.
- D. Ask the driver to turn back at once.

**Answer** A

## A18-02 灭火器先拔保险销

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Fang, the fire box is by the stairs. Can we use the extinguisher?
Mr. Fang: Yes, but pull out the safety pin first, not the handle.
Boy: Where do I send the water?
Mr. Fang: Aim at the base of the fire. Never use water on a cooking-oil fire.

**Question**
What should the boy do first with the extinguisher?
- A. Send the water at the top of the fire.
- B. Pull the handle down.
- C. Pull out the safety pin.
- D. Use water on the cooking-oil fire.

**Answer** C

## A18-03 坐后排要系安全带

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Ma, Uncle Li drives us to the park. May I sit in the back?
Ms. Ma: Yes, but the seat belt must be on. Sit in the back, not Uncle Li's lap.
Girl: The road is short. Is it still necessary?
Ms. Ma: Yes. Buckle up before the car starts, every time.

**Question**
Where will the girl sit in Uncle Li's car?
- A. In Uncle Li's lap.
- B. In the back seat with the seat belt on.
- C. In the front seat next to Uncle Li.
- D. In the back seat without the seat belt.

**Answer** B

## A18-04 闻到燃气味先关阀门

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Du, I smell gas in the kitchen.
Mr. Du: Close the gas valve first. Do not turn on a light and do not make a phone call here.
Boy: Shall I open the window?
Mr. Du: Not yet. Go downstairs and tell the manager. Call from outside.

**Question**
What should the boy do first?
- A. Close the gas valve.
- B. Open the window in the kitchen.
- C. Turn on the light to look for the leak.
- D. Call his mother from the kitchen.

**Answer** A

## A18-05 打雷下雨躲到礼堂

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Yu, a storm is coming. Shall we wait under the big tree?
Ms. Yu: No. The tree is the worst place. Go to the hall with your friends.
Girl: What about the bike shed?
Ms. Yu: No, it is full of water. Stay in the hall until the rain stops.

**Question**
Where should the girls wait during the storm?
- A. Under the big tree.
- B. On the playground.
- C. In the bike shed.
- D. In the school hall.

**Answer** D

## A18-06 见人落水不要自己下水

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Bai, a boy fell into the river!
Ms. Bai: Shout for help. Throw him a pole or a ball. Do not jump in yourself.
Boy: I can swim well.
Ms. Bai: Not this time. Call 110 from the bank and wait for the boat.

**Question**
What should the boy do instead of jumping in?
- A. Swim across the river to him.
- B. Throw the boy a pole or a ball.
- C. Call 110 from his own home.
- D. Take a boat out on the river.

**Answer** B

## A18-07 走丢了在服务台等

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhou, I can't find my mother. She went to the shoe shop on the second floor.
Mr. Zhou: Stay at the service desk. Dad is waiting by the car.
Girl: Can I go upstairs and look for her?
Mr. Zhou: No. Stay here. She will come back to the desk.

**Question**
Where should the girl wait for her mother?
- A. At her father's car.
- B. At the shoe shop.
- C. At the service desk.
- D. In the staff room.

**Answer** C

## A18-08 陌生人的糖不能收

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Cui, a man at the gate gave me a sweet.
Mr. Cui: Throw it away. Take nothing from a stranger.
Boy: He knows my teacher.
Mr. Cui: Still no. Go and stand with your class at the gate.

**Question**
What should the boy do with the sweet?
- A. Throw it away.
- B. Eat it, because the man knows his teacher.
- C. Give it to his teacher.
- D. Take it home for his sister.

**Answer** A

## A18-09 电梯里靠右站

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Liang, everyone pushes in when the lift arrives.
Mr. Liang: Yes. Let the people inside come out first.
Girl: Then I enter last, don't I?
Mr. Liang: Right. Stand on the right side and keep the door clear.

**Question**
Where should the girl stand in the lift?
- A. In the middle of the lift.
- B. Right in front of the door.
- C. On the left side.
- D. On the right side.

**Answer** D

## A18-10 药要放在高处

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Yan, my little brother says the pills in the box are sweets.
Ms. Yan: Keep all medicine in a high cupboard. Never count a pill as a sweet.
Girl: What should I do if he takes one?
Ms. Yan: Take him to the doctor at once and take the box with you.

**Question**
Where should the medicine be kept?
- A. On the dining table.
- B. In a high cupboard.
- C. On the floor.
- D. In a drawer by the bed.

**Answer** B

## A18-11 割破手指先用自来水冲

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Fu, I cut my finger on the tin.
Ms. Fu: Wash it under the tap first, not with your mouth.
Boy: Does it need a plaster?
Ms. Fu: A clean one, yes. Ask the school nurse, not your friend.

**Question**
What should the boy do first with the cut on his finger?
- A. Wash it under the tap.
- B. Put his finger in his mouth.
- C. Leave it unwashed.
- D. Ask his friend for a plaster.

**Answer** A

## A18-12 红灯要停在斑马线

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gao, the lights are red but no car is coming.
Mr. Gao: Wait. Red means stop, even for a minute. Use the zebra crossing.
Boy: May I cross behind the bus?
Mr. Gao: No. Wait at the crossing, not behind a bus.

**Question**
Where should the boy wait before crossing the road?
- A. Behind the bus.
- B. In the middle of the road.
- C. At the zebra crossing.
- D. On the pavement by the bus stop.

**Answer** C

## A18-13 大风天把阳台东西收进来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Ge, the wind is very strong. Shall we open the window?
Ms. Ge: No. Keep this one closed, and close the balcony window.
Girl: What about my clothes on the balcony?
Ms. Ge: Take them inside and bring the flower pot in as well.

**Question**
What will the girl do on the balcony?
- A. Leave her clothes on the balcony.
- B. Open the balcony window.
- C. Put the flower pot on the roof.
- D. Take the clothes and the flower pot inside.

**Answer** D

## A18-14 地面没干走另一部楼梯

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Du, the floor by the stairs is wet.
Mr. Du: Yes, someone mopped it. Walk round, not across.
Boy: Is it still wet?
Mr. Du: The sign says dry at four. Before that, use the other stairs.

**Question**
What should the boy do before four o'clock?
- A. Walk across the wet floor.
- B. Use the other stairs.
- C. Mop the floor again himself.
- D. Stand by the wet floor and wait.

**Answer** B

## A18-15 火警下楼走楼梯

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, the alarm is ringing. Shall we take the lift down?
Mr. Pei: No. Use the stairs. The lift may stop at any floor.
Girl: Where do we leave our bags?
Mr. Pei: Take them with you. Gather at the sports ground, not the hall.

**Question**
Where will the girls gather after leaving the building?
- A. In the school hall.
- B. On the second floor.
- C. On the sports ground.
- D. At the lift door.

**Answer** C
