> A31 邻里往来与日常安排（15 题）
> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本批对话原创编写，未取自任何正式试题。

## A31-01 楼上漏水
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhou, the ceiling in my bedroom is wet.
Mr. Zhou: That is from upstairs. Knock on their door first, politely.
Girl: What if they say it is not them?
Mr. Zhou: Then put a bowl under the wet place tonight.
Girl: And if the bowl fills?
Mr. Zhou: Take it to the manager on Saturday.
**Question**
What does Mr. Zhou tell the girl to do first?
- A. Knock on the upstairs door and ask politely.
- B. Put a bowl under the wet place tonight.
- C. Take the bowl to the manager on Saturday.
- D. Move out at once.
**Answer** A

## A31-02 金鱼缸的水温
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Han, how warm should the water be for my goldfish?
Ms. Han: Between twenty-two and twenty-six degrees.
Boy: Our heater is set at twenty-eight.
Ms. Han: Then it is too warm, and they will not eat.
Boy: Should I change it every day?
Ms. Han: Once a week. Twice is too much for them.
**Question**
About how warm should the water be?
- A. About twenty degrees.
- B. About twenty-eight degrees.
- C. About twenty-four degrees.
- D. About thirty degrees.
**Answer** C

## A31-03 阳台种葱的间距
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Tang, how far apart should I plant the spring onions?
Ms. Tang: Fifteen centimetres, and no closer.
Boy: My box is sixty centimetres long.
Ms. Tang: Then four rows, not six. Six would be ten centimetres apart.
Boy: So I save a row of onions.
Ms. Tang: And the ones you keep will be thicker.
**Question**
How many rows can the boy plant in the box?
- A. Six rows.
- B. Four rows.
- C. Three rows.
- D. Five rows.
**Answer** B

## A31-04 猫咪的驱虫药
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Song, how much deworming medicine does my cat need?
Mr. Song: One tablet for a cat under four kilos. Yours is five.
Girl: Then two tablets?
Mr. Song: No. Two is too much. Give one and ask again in a month.
Girl: And not with food?
Mr. Song: With food, or she will spit it out.
**Question**
How many tablets will the girl give her cat now?
- A. None.
- B. Two tablets.
- C. Three tablets.
- D. One tablet.
**Answer** D

## A31-05 小区门口修路的绕行
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhao, the road outside our gate is dug up.
Mr. Zhao: Yes, three weeks. Walk out of the east gate instead. It is four minutes longer.
Girl: Is the east gate open then?
Mr. Zhao: From six to ten every day. Your bus comes at half past seven, and the stop is closer.
**Question**
Why is the east gate a good way for the girl?
- A. It is open at half past seven and the stop is closer.
- B. It is four minutes shorter.
- C. It is open all day.
- D. It is on the way to the bus stop.
**Answer** A

## A31-06 给同学讲题
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qiao, my classmate does not understand fractions.
Ms. Qiao: Then do not explain. Give him three easy ones and wait.
Boy: He will say he still cannot.
Ms. Qiao: Then give him five, not thirty, and mark the ones he gets right.
Boy: Mark them in his book?
Ms. Qiao: On a separate paper.
**Question**
Where should the boy mark the questions his classmate gets right?
- A. In his own book.
- B. On a separate paper.
- C. On the blackboard.
- D. In the teacher's desk.
**Answer** B

## A31-07 邻居家的噪音时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Xu, the family next door drill at seven every morning.
Mr. Xu: Before eight it is quiet by law. After eight they may drill.
Girl: I study at seven, so what do I do?
Mr. Xu: Tell them before eight, not after eight. If they drill after eight too, go to the manager on Saturday.
**Question**
What does Mr. Xu advise the girl to do first?
- A. Drill at the same time.
- B. Move house.
- C. Speak to them before eight.
- D. Go to the manager on Saturday.
**Answer** C

## A31-08 向邻居借梯子
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Kong, may I borrow your ladder for an hour?
Mr. Kong: Yes, but you must be nineteen, or somebody must hold it.
Girl: My brother will hold it.
Mr. Kong: Then take it before six; after six the light is bad.
Girl: I only need ten minutes.
Mr. Kong: Ten minutes is fine.
**Question**
When may the girl take the ladder?
- A. At any time.
- B. After six.
- C. Only at weekends.
- D. Before six.
**Answer** D

## A31-09 买酱油的容量
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tao, what size bottle of soy sauce shall I buy?
Mr. Tao: A family of three takes a small bottle in about two months.
Girl: Ours is four people.
Mr. Tao: Then a small bottle is not enough. Take the middle one.
Girl: And the large one?
Mr. Tao: About a year. It is for a restaurant.
**Question**
Which bottle should the girl take?
- A. The middle one.
- B. The small one.
- C. The large one.
- D. Two small ones.
**Answer** A

## A31-10 超市的会员卡积分
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Dai, I have four hundred points on my card.
Ms. Dai: Four hundred is not enough for the rice. That is four hundred eighty.
Boy: And the soap?
Ms. Dai: The soap is three hundred. You would have a hundred left.
Boy: Points do not expire?
Ms. Dai: They expire after two years.
**Question**
How many points will the boy have left after he uses them for the soap?
- A. Three hundred points.
- B. One hundred points.
- C. Four hundred points.
- D. Four hundred eighty points.
**Answer** B

## A31-11 办事窗口的午休
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wan, the office says nine to five, but the line is out of the door.
Ms. Wan: They stop taking numbers at half past twelve and open again at one.
Boy: I have number forty-two.
Ms. Wan: Then you are after lunch. Go and eat first, then come back at half past twelve.
**Question**
What does Ms. Wan tell the boy to do?
- A. Keep his place in the queue.
- B. Leave and come tomorrow.
- C. Go and eat first, then come back.
- D. Go to another office.
**Answer** C

## A31-12 换季衣服的收纳
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Yan, how should I store my winter coats?
Ms. Yan: Clean them first. A coat stored with dirt will smell in August.
Boy: Then hang them?
Ms. Yan: Fold them. Hanging a wool coat for months stretches the shoulders.
Boy: How many will fit in the top box?
Ms. Yan: Three. Four will not shut.
**Question**
How many coats will fit in the top box?
- A. Four.
- B. Two.
- C. Five.
- D. Three.
**Answer** D

## A31-13 公共厕所的保洁时间
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Peng, why is the toilet locked at noon?
Mr. Peng: It is cleaned at noon. It opens again at one.
Girl: But the school closes at twelve.
Mr. Peng: Use the one at the square. That one stays open all day.
Girl: Is it far?
Mr. Peng: Six minutes' walk.
**Question**
Which toilet can the girl use at noon?
- A. The one by the school.
- B. The one at the square.
- C. The one at the park.
- D. The one inside the station.
**Answer** B

## A31-14 调自行车坐垫的高度
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Zou, the seat on my bike is too high.
Ms. Zou: When you sit, your leg should be straight at the bottom.
Boy: It is bent.
Ms. Zou: Then take the seat down about five centimetres. Not ten, or your feet will not reach.
Boy: How do I measure five?
Ms. Zou: One finger is about a centimetre.
**Question**
By how much should the boy lower the seat?
- A. About one centimetre.
- B. About ten centimetres.
- C. About half a centimetre.
- D. About five centimetres.
**Answer** D

## A31-15 楼道堆物的清理
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, the stairs are full of old furniture.
Mr. Yu: It comes down on the first Saturday of the month.
Girl: My mother's sofa is there.
Mr. Yu: Book the van on Friday. It is free.
Girl: How early does the van come?
Mr. Yu: At seven. It leaves at eleven, full or not.
**Question**
At what time will the van leave?
- A. At seven.
- B. At nine.
- C. At eleven.
- D. At four.
**Answer** C
