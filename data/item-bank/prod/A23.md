> A23 课堂学习与校内规则（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A23-01 错题要抄进错题本

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Han, how do I correct my mistakes in the maths book?
Mr. Han: Copy each one in the wrong-answer book and mark it in red.
Girl: Do I have to copy the whole question?
Mr. Han: No, just the question and the right steps. One line is enough.

**Question**
What should the girl do with her mistakes?
- A. Copy them in the wrong-answer book and mark them in red.
- B. Tear the page out and start the book again.
- C. Write them in a new notebook in pencil.
- D. Say nothing and wait for the test.

**Answer** A

## A23-02 用过的粉笔放托盘

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Gao, the new box of chalk has only three sticks left.
Mr. Gao: Put the used sticks in the tray under the board, not in the bin or the new box.
Girl: Do the short ones work too?
Mr. Gao: They do. Anyone can take one from the tray at eight.

**Question**
Where should the girl put the used sticks of chalk?
- A. Back in the new box.
- B. In her pencil case.
- C. In the tray under the board.
- D. In the dustbin.

**Answer** C

## A23-03 跟不上只记关键词

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, the teacher talks too fast for me.
Mr. Pei: Write only the key words, not every sentence.
Girl: Then how do I write it up afterwards?
Mr. Pei: Use those words at night, with the book open.

**Question**
What should the girl write down in class?
- A. Every sentence the teacher says.
- B. Only the key words.
- C. Nothing at all.
- D. A full copy of the textbook.

**Answer** B

## A23-04 饮水机在三楼

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Niu, where can I get some hot water?
Mr. Niu: The water dispenser is on the third floor, not on this floor.
Boy: Can I bring my own cup?
Mr. Niu: Yes, a plastic cup. Not a glass one.

**Question**
Where is the water dispenser?
- A. On the first floor.
- B. On the second floor.
- C. On this floor.
- D. On the third floor.

**Answer** D

## A23-05 实验室的药品不能尝

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bao, can I taste this white powder?
Mr. Bao: No. Nothing in the lab tray is for eating, ever.
Girl: Even if it smells like sugar?
Mr. Bao: Especially then. Read the label first and tell me the name.

**Question**
What should the girl do with the white powder?
- A. Read the label and tell Mr. Bao the name.
- B. Taste a little to see if it is sugar.
- C. Put it back in the tray and say nothing.
- D. Take it home in her bag.

**Answer** A

## A23-06 投影仪提前十分钟开

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, the projector shows nothing.
Mr. Gu: Switch it on ten minutes before the lesson, not at the bell.
Boy: Do I need to call anyone?
Mr. Gu: No, if it still fails then tell me. Don't open the box yourself.

**Question**
When should the boy switch on the projector?
- A. At the bell.
- B. Ten minutes before the lesson.
- C. After the lesson ends.
- D. Only after it fails.

**Answer** B

## A23-07 作业交到班级邮箱

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Wu, how do we hand in the writing homework?
Ms. Wu: Send it to the class email before eight. Paper is not accepted.
Girl: What if the line is busy at eight?
Ms. Wu: Then send it early, not at eight. The box closes at eight.

**Question**
How should the girl hand in the writing homework?
- A. On paper, at eight.
- B. By email, after eight.
- C. By email, before eight.
- D. Tell the teacher in class.

**Answer** C

## A23-08 复习先做数学卷

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Jia, how should I start my revision?
Mr. Jia: Do the maths paper first. It is the one you are worst at.
Boy: How many papers should I do a day?
Mr. Jia: Two, not five. Two papers and a review of the errors.

**Question**
How many papers should the boy do a day?
- A. Five.
- B. One.
- C. Ten.
- D. Two.

**Answer** D

## A23-09 忘带课本和同桌合看

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tong, I left my Chinese book at home.
Mr. Tong: Borrow one from a classmate. Two people can share a book.
Girl: Can I just sit and do nothing?
Mr. Tong: No. Follow the reading on the board, then write it up at home.

**Question**
What should the girl do in class without her book?
- A. Share a classmate's book and follow the reading on the board.
- B. Sit and do nothing until the bell.
- C. Go home and fetch the book.
- D. Ask the teacher to stop the lesson.

**Answer** A

## A23-10 围棋比赛每局二十分钟

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, when is the go tournament?
Mr. Bai: At two in the afternoon, in the reading room.
Girl: How long do we have for each game?
Mr. Bai: Twenty minutes. Put your watch on the table, not your phone.

**Question**
How long does each game last?
- A. Ten minutes.
- B. Twenty minutes.
- C. One hour.
- D. Two hours.

**Answer** B

## A23-11 集会按班号站队

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Kong, how do we line up for the school meeting?
Ms. Kong: By class number, one to forty. Not by height.
Boy: What if my class number is twelve?
Ms. Kong: Then you stand in the middle. Leave the front row to the first class.

**Question**
How should the students line up?
- A. By height.
- B. By name.
- C. In any order they like.
- D. By class number.

**Answer** D

## A23-12 抄完才能擦黑板

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. An, the lesson is over. May I wipe the board?
Mr. An: Wait until the last student has copied it.
Girl: What if I am in a hurry for lunch?
Mr. An: Then ask the class monitor. Don't wipe it on your own.

**Question**
What should the girl do before wiping the board?
- A. Wipe it at once, because the lesson is over.
- B. Ask the teacher to wipe it for her.
- C. Wait until the last student has copied it.
- D. Wipe it before the next lesson starts.

**Answer** C

## A23-13 作业本封面写名字

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Qiu, my work book has no name on it.
Ms. Qiu: Write your name and class on the front, not inside.
Girl: What about the date?
Ms. Qiu: Every day, in the top corner. Then hand it in on Friday.

**Question**
Where should the girl write her name?
- A. On the front of the work book.
- B. Inside the back cover.
- C. On her own desk.
- D. On the teacher's desk.

**Answer** A

## A23-14 书按大小摆桌面

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Zhi, my books are all over the desk.
Mr. Zhi: Put the big ones on the left and the small ones on the right.
Boy: Do I need to do it every morning?
Mr. Zhi: Once a week is enough. Not every morning.

**Question**
How should the boy put the books on his desk?
- A. All in one pile in the middle.
- B. Small ones on the left and big ones on the right.
- C. Big ones on the left and small ones on the right.
- D. Stack them in any order he likes.

**Answer** C

## A23-15 预备铃要马上收书

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, why does the bell ring twice?
Mr. Yu: The first bell is the prepare bell. Put your books away at once.
Girl: Do we go back to the dorm after the first bell?
Mr. Yu: No, you stay in the classroom. Only the second bell ends the class.

**Question**
What happens at the first bell?
- A. Everyone leaves the classroom.
- B. The students put their books away.
- C. The class ends.
- D. The teacher goes out.

**Answer** B
