## E9-01 朋友与友谊
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你最珍视的朋友，说说你们之间的约定，以及这段友谊对你意味着什么。
**参考作答**：My best friend is Zhang Wei, and we have been friends since we were in Grade Seven. He sits behind me in class, and we often walk home together. Our agreement is simple: we meet at the school gate every Friday and read for half an hour in the library. We promise not to use our phones while we are reading. Sometimes we argue about small things, but we always say sorry on the same day. He is better at maths than I am, so he helps me with my homework. I am better at drawing, so I help him with his sketch book. Last year we entered a school competition together, and we did not win, but we did not stop talking to each other. I value this friendship because it makes me calm before an exam. I hope we will still be together when we are older.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：朋友的姓名与认识多久、座位与放学同行、约定的内容与频率、约定中双方都要遵守的一条
- 信息完整性：闹别扭与当天和好、互相帮忙的两个方面、参加比赛的结果、友谊对自己的意义
- 发音准确性：agreement / promise / competition 的重音；Zhang Wei 的音节划分
- 表达流利度：so / and / but 的承接层次
- 语法正确性：since 引导的时间状语；be better at 的比较结构；will be 的时态

## E9-02 时间安排与学习效率
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你平时怎样安排一天的学习时间，说说哪一段时间效率最高。
**参考作答**：I get up at six twenty and start my day with a short run in the yard. I do my hardest homework from six forty to seven twenty, before the school bus comes, because my mind is clearest then. School begins at eight, so I actually revise on the bus. After school I have a club meeting on Friday, and on other days I practise my guitar for half an hour. My mother cooks dinner at six, and we eat together instead of watching television. I read for twenty minutes before I sleep, although my phone is usually beside me. The evening is long, and I often feel tired at nine. Still, the early morning is the best time for me. Last term I moved my mathematics practice to seven in the morning, and my scores went up by ten points.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：起床时间与晨间活动、效率最高时段及其理由、上学途中的安排、放学后的安排
- 信息完整性：晚饭与家庭时间、睡前阅读、白天的长短感受、把练习挪到早晨之后成绩的变化
- 发音准确性：revise / mathematics / scores 的重音；twenty minutes 的连读
- 表达流利度：because / so / still 的先后；although 的让步
- 语法正确性：过去时与现在时的切换；instead of 的用法；最高级 clearest

## E9-03 校园规则
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍学校里的规则，说说你最看重哪条规则。
**参考作答**：Our school has about six rules that every student must follow. We must wear the school uniform from Monday to Friday, and we are not allowed to wear jeans. We cannot use our phones in class, although we may keep them in our bags. Mobile phones must be handed to the teacher before a test. I think the rule about phones is the most useful one, because a phone on a desk takes our attention away. Last year our class broke this rule twice in one week, and the teacher was angry. After that our class made a rule of our own: we put every phone in a box at the door. The school also has a rule about running in the stairs, and nobody is allowed to run there. I agree with that one as well, because people get hurt in staircases.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：规则的大致条数、校服与牛仔裤、手机在课堂与考试时的规定、本人最看重哪条及其理由
- 信息完整性：班级违规的次数与老师反应、班级自定的新规矩、楼梯规则、本人对另一条规则的态度与理由
- 发音准确性：uniform / staircases / attention 的重音；angry 的鼻音
- 表达流利度：also / after that / as well 的承接
- 语法正确性：被动语态 are not allowed / is allowed；最高级 the most useful

## E9-04 出行与交通工具
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你上学使用的交通方式，说说这种出行方式给你带来的变化。
**参考作答**：I go to school by bike every day, and the ride takes about fifteen minutes. My father bought me an old bike two years ago, and he fixed the brakes himself. In the past I took a bus, and the queue at the stop made me late nearly every morning. Last winter I started to ride in heavy rain, which was cold but also healthy. Now I leave home ten minutes earlier, and I am never late for the first class. Riding has changed my body as well, because I feel stronger in my legs. It has also made me more careful when I cross a big road. My father says that I should wear a bright jacket at night, and now I always carry a small light on my bag. I hope I can keep riding when I go to high school next year.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：交通方式与单程时长、车辆来历、以前采用的方式及其带来的问题
- 信息完整性：雨天的感受、现在的时间安排与结果、身体方面的变化、路上安全方面的变化、家人建议与执行、明年的打算
- 发音准确性：brakes / healthy / bike 的重音；because 的连读
- 表达流利度：also / and / but 的顺序
- 语法正确性：现在完成时 has changed / has made；比较级 stronger

## E9-05 音乐与艺术
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你喜欢的那种艺术形式，说说它怎样影响了你。
**参考作答**：The art form I like best is black-and-white photography. My father showed me how to use his old camera when I was in Grade Eight. Photography is different from painting, because a camera does not let you add anything afterwards. I keep a small notebook with the ideas for every shoot. Last spring I photographed my grandmother in her kitchen, and she laughed because she had not seen herself in twenty years. That photograph now hangs above my desk. Photography has made me more careful about light, and I notice shadows in the street now. It has also taught me to be patient, since a good picture often needs thirty minutes. My mother says I look at the world more slowly since I started. I hope to bring a real camera on our next trip.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：艺术形式的名称、开始接触的时间与契机、它与别的艺术形式的区别、拍摄前的准备
- 信息完整性：一次具体的拍摄与家人的反应、照片的去向、它对观察力的影响、对性情的影响、家人评价、今后的打算
- 发音准确性：photography / camera / patient 的音节与重音；grandmother 为三个音节
- 表达流利度：because / since / also 的层次
- 语法正确性：现在完成时 has made / has taught；比较级 more careful

## E9-06 健康习惯
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你保持健康时坚持的习惯，说说坚持之后你身上出现了什么变化。
**参考作答**：The habit I keep is drinking warm water when I get up. I started it last September because I often had a sore throat in the morning. My grandfather told me that warm water is better for us than cold water in winter. Now I drink two glasses before breakfast, and then I do twenty deep breaths by the window. This habit is small, but it has changed my mornings. I used to skip breakfast, and now I eat an apple first. After three months I hardly catch a cold. My mother says my skin is better than last year, and I believe her. My classmate Li Na began the same habit in October, and she also feels fine now. I hope I can keep it for many years, because health is more useful than marks.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：习惯的具体内容、开始的时间与原因、长辈给出的建议、每天的具体做法
- 信息完整性：这个习惯带来的作息变化、三个月后的身体变化、家人的评价、同学受到的影响、今后的打算与理由
- 发音准确性：throat / habit / health 的重音；because 的连读
- 表达流利度：but / and / because 的承接
- 语法正确性：used to do 表示过去的习惯；比较级 better 与 more useful

## E9-07 科技与生活
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍改变了你生活的科技产品，说说它给你带来的好处与麻烦。
**参考作答**：The product that changed my life most is my phone, which I got for my birthday two years ago. Before that I used a small keypad phone for calls only. Now I keep my timetable, my dictionary and my notes in it, so I do not need a paper notebook. The biggest advantage is that I can check a word in two seconds. Another advantage is that my parents can call me when I walk home. However, there is a problem as well. When I look at short videos, I lose twenty minutes without noticing. My father told me to keep the phone in another room when I study, and now I do that every evening. In this way the phone helps me and hurts me at the same time. I think a person should choose how to use it, not the other way round.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：产品名称、获得时间与来源、从前的替代品、现在用它做些什么、最大的好处
- 信息完整性：另一个好处、带来的麻烦与具体表现、家长的建议与自己的执行、总体看法
- 发音准确性：product / dictionary / advantage 的重音；however 的弱读
- 表达流利度：so / however / as well 的转折层次
- 语法正确性：定语从句 which I got；介词 without 后接动名词 noticing 作宾语

## E9-08 动物与宠物
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你喜欢的动物，说说你从它身上学到什么。
**参考作答**：The animal I like best is the dog, and my family has kept one for six years. His name is Doudou, and he is a brown dog with white feet. Doudou waits at the gate every afternoon, so he knows the sound of my steps. My father chose him from a litter next door, and my mother feeds him when she is at home. He never runs into the road, because my father trained him when he was young. Dogs show their feelings more openly than people do, and Doudou is never unhappy for long. From him I learned that a calm animal is easier to live with. I also learned that animals need a fixed routine, so I keep my own study time every evening. Our neighbour has a cat, and the two of them meet at the gate every weekend. Next year I hope to learn how to train a puppy, because Doudou is getting old.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：动物种类与饲养年限、名字与外观、它表现出的一个具体习惯
- 信息完整性：家人与它的关系、从它身上学到的一点、它对作息的影响、与邻居动物的往来、今后的打算与理由
- 发音准确性：neighbour / puppy / brown 的音与重音
- 表达流利度：so / because / also 的层次
- 语法正确性：比较级 more openly；现在完成时 has kept 的时态一致性

## E9-09 理财与消费观
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你学会的花钱方式，说说它怎样帮你攒下了钱。
**参考作答**：The way I learned to spend money is to put my pocket money into three jars. My mother gave me an old coffee jar, and I bought two cheap ones at a shop near my home. One jar is for books, one is for snacks with my friends, and the last one is for things I want but do not need. At the end of each month I count the money in the last jar. When I want a game, I have to wait until that jar is full enough. This method is slow, but it works better than writing down every coin. In the past I spent most of my money in the first two weeks. Now I keep about forty yuan for a whole month. My father says I have become careful, and he now gives me a little more each month. I hope to buy a good dictionary with the money in the books jar next term.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：花钱办法的具体内容、容器从哪来、三个罐子各自的用途、每月如何核对
- 信息完整性：想买东西时的处理办法、该办法与过去做法的对比、现在每月能留下的金额、家人的评价与随之的变化、今后的打算
- 发音准确性：pocket / method / dictionary 的重音
- 表达流利度：but / and 的转折，以及 in the past 与 now 的时间对比
- 语法正确性：比较级 better than；情态动词 have to

## E9-10 家乡的变化
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你的家乡，说说近些年它发生了哪些变化。
**参考作答**：My hometown is a small town near Suzhou, and about three thousand people live there. Ten years ago the main street had only a shop and a post office. Today the same street has a supermarket, a bookshop and two coffee places. The biggest change is the bus line, because it now comes every ten minutes instead of twice an hour. Another change is the park beside the river, which was a piece of waste ground before. Old houses were pulled down, and the new ones are taller, so the town looks quite different now. My grandparents miss the old market, but my mother likes the new supermarket. The town is still small, yet it is much easier to live in than before. I think the changes are mostly good, although traffic is heavier in the afternoon.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：家乡的地理位置与规模、十年前的样子、现在街道上的变化
- 信息完整性：交通方面的最大变化与具体数字、河边公园的变化、房屋的变化、长辈与母亲的不同看法、总体评价与不足之处
- 发音准确性：supermarket / grandparents / traffic 的重音
- 表达流利度：yet / but / although 的让步与转折
- 语法正确性：过去被动 were pulled down；比较级 taller

## E9-11 挫折与坚持
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍一件你原来做不好的事，说说你后来怎么改进的。
**参考作答**：The thing I could not do well was public speaking. In Grade Seven I was asked to read a poem to the whole class, and my hands shook so much that I dropped the paper. I sat down without finishing and said nothing. A year later I joined the debate club, because my English teacher said that talking is a skill like riding a bike. The first month was hard, and I spoke in short sentences only. I practised reading aloud for ten minutes every evening and recorded myself on my phone. In the spring I took part in a school speech contest and won third prize. That was not a big prize, but it meant more than the first prize in Grade Six. The change did not come from talent, and it came from practice. I still get nervous before I speak, but now I keep talking anyway.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：原来做不好的事是什么、一次具体的失败经历与当时的反应、改变起点与其中的原因
- 信息完整性：最初的困难、每天练习的具体做法与工具、后来的一次比赛与名次、这件事除成绩之外的意义、对自己的总结、今后的状态
- 发音准确性：public / nervous / practice 的重音；recorded 的音节
- 表达流利度：because / but / anyway 的顺序
- 语法正确性：过去时叙述保持一致；so much 的程度表达

## E9-12 兴趣与取舍
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你在课外班与兴趣之间作的取舍。
**参考作答**：Last year I had four classes outside school: maths, English, swimming and calligraphy. Swimming was the only one I chose for myself, and it was also the one I liked least. The other three were chosen by my parents, and they all helped me at school. I gave up swimming in March because it took the whole Saturday morning. Now I go to a chess club on Wednesday evening, which costs less and does not stop my homework. My mother worried that I would lose the sport, but I have begun to run in the park instead. Making a choice is not easy, because every hobby takes time. I try to keep two free afternoons for myself, so I never feel that school has taken everything. I hope next term I can return to the swimming pool once a week.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：原先课外班的数量与名称、哪个是自己选的、哪些是家长安排的及其原因
- 信息完整性：放弃的具体项目与时间上的理由、现在的替代安排、家长的担心与自己的应对、对时间分配的想法、今后的打算
- 发音准确性：calligraphy / hobby / Wednesday 的重音与音节
- 表达流利度：because / but / so 的层次
- 语法正确性：过去时与现在时的切换；instead 与 also 的用法

## E9-13 网络安全
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你在网上保护个人信息时遵守的做法。
**参考作答**：The rule I follow online is simple: I never put my real name on a public page. When I joined a fan forum last year, I used a nickname instead. I also never send photos of my school uniform with the school name on it. My cousin once posted such a photo, and a stranger found his class. That frightened us both, so we deleted it on the same evening. I check the padlock at the top of every page before I type anything, and I close the window when I use a public computer at school. My passwords are never the same twice, and I keep them in a notebook at home. These habits are not difficult, although I forget them when I am tired. I think online safety is a skill, and skills improve with practice.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：自己定的规矩的具体内容、加论坛时采取的做法、坚决不做的事
- 信息完整性：家人踩过的坑与处理方式、每次输入前的检查、密码的做法、对这些习惯难度的判断与总体看法
- 发音准确性：nickname / passwords / stranger 的重音；online 的连读
- 表达流利度：so / and / also 的层次
- 语法正确性：when 引导的时间状语从句；never 的位置
