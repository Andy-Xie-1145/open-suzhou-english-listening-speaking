> A25 看展与观演（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A25-01 美术馆周三才开门

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, is the art museum open today?
Mr. Guo: No, today is Monday. We open from Wednesday to Sunday, ten to five.
Girl: Can I come on Tuesday?
Mr. Guo: No. Tuesday is for schools only. Wednesday is the next open day.

**Question**
When can the girl visit the museum?
- A. On Wednesday.
- B. On Monday afternoon.
- C. On Tuesday morning.
- D. On Sunday evening.

**Answer** A

## A25-02 画室里不能拍照

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Shen, may I take a photo of this painting?
Mr. Shen: No. No photos in the painting room. Sketching is fine.
Boy: Can I photograph the stone animals outside?
Mr. Shen: Yes, outside is fine. The rule is only about the paintings.

**Question**
What may the boy do in the painting room?
- A. Take a photo of the painting.
- B. Walk through without stopping.
- C. Sketch only the stone animals.
- D. Sketch the painting in a notebook.

**Answer** D

## A25-03 电影要坐第五排中间

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pan, which seats are best for the new film?
Mr. Pan: The middle of the fifth row, not the first or the last.
Girl: The last row is cheaper, isn't it?
Mr. Pan: It is, but you cannot see the screen there. Book the middle seats.

**Question**
Which seats should the girl book?
- A. The first row.
- B. The middle of the fifth row.
- C. The last row, because it is cheaper.
- D. Any seat she can get.

**Answer** B

## A25-04 迟到要等中场入场

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yu, I am ten minutes late for the play.
Mr. Yu: Wait outside and come in at the interval, not during the first act.
Boy: Can I stand at the back of the hall?
Mr. Yu: No. Outside is where you wait. The usher will not move on.

**Question**
When may the boy go to his seat?
- A. During the first act.
- B. Right after the play ends.
- C. At the interval.
- D. Ten minutes later.

**Answer** C

## A25-05 动物园不能自己喂

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, may I feed the monkeys my bread?
Mr. Bai: No. You must not feed any animal here. The keeper does it.
Girl: Even the ducks by the pond?
Mr. Bai: Even those. Only the keeper may feed them. He takes your bread to them.

**Question**
What may the girl do with her bread?
- A. Give it to the keeper for the animals.
- B. Throw it to the monkeys herself.
- C. Give it to the ducks by the pond.
- D. Drop it on the ground for the birds.

**Answer** A

## A25-06 讲解要领十二号耳机

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, how do I hear the guide's talk?
Mr. Gu: Take a headphone at the desk, number twelve. Not a loud speaker.
Boy: Do I have to give it back?
Mr. Gu: Yes, when the talk ends. Keep it for the whole hour.

**Question**
How will the boy hear the guide's talk?
- A. From a loudspeaker in the hall.
- B. From a headphone he takes at the desk.
- C. By standing next to the guide.
- D. By reading the wall label.

**Answer** B

## A25-07 博物馆的地图免费拿

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Kuang, can I see the museum map?
Mr. Kuang: Take one from the desk by the stairs. It is free, not a shop.
Girl: Is the big paper one on sale?
Mr. Kuang: That one is, but you do not need it. The free one shows every floor.

**Question**
How can the girl get a museum map?
- A. Buy the big paper one at the shop.
- B. Borrow one from the next group.
- C. Copy it from the wall label.
- D. Take the free one from the desk by the stairs.

**Answer** D

## A25-08 展览学生票十元

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Zhai, how much is the exhibition?
Ms. Zhai: Twenty yuan. Students pay ten, not fifteen.
Boy: Can I pay half on the second floor?
Ms. Zhai: Half is for students only, and only at the desk on the first floor.

**Question**
How much will the boy pay?
- A. Twenty yuan.
- B. Fifteen yuan.
- C. Ten yuan.
- D. Five yuan.

**Answer** C

## A25-09 狮子要上午九点看

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, when is the best time to see the lions?
Mr. Pei: In the morning. At noon the stone is hot and they sleep.
Girl: But the zoo opens at nine in the morning.
Mr. Pei: Then be there at nine, not at two. Two is the worst hour.

**Question**
When should the girl go to the lion house?
- A. At nine in the morning.
- B. At two in the afternoon.
- C. At noon.
- D. At seven at night.

**Answer** A

## A25-10 剧院要把包存起来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hu, may I take my bag into the theatre?
Mr. Hu: No. Leave it at the cloakroom. Only the programme goes in.
Girl: Is the cloakroom free?
Mr. Hu: Yes. Take the ticket with you and keep it all evening.

**Question**
What should the girl do with her bag?
- A. Take it into the theatre and sit near the door.
- B. Leave it at the cloakroom.
- C. Put it under her seat.
- D. Give it to the usher at the door.

**Answer** B

## A25-11 电影票要有座位号

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Guo, are these two tickets any good?
Mr. Guo: Not unless the seat numbers are on them. Ask for numbered seats.
Boy: Can we sit anywhere if we pay more?
Mr. Guo: No. The hall is numbered and the numbers on the ticket must match.

**Question**
What should the boy ask for at the ticket desk?
- A. Two tickets without seat numbers.
- B. Tickets for any seat, at a higher price.
- C. Tickets with the film name only.
- D. Tickets with seat numbers.

**Answer** D

## A25-12 画和画框都不能摸

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tong, is this painting real or a photo of one?
Mr. Tong: Real, and that is one of our best works. You must not touch it.
Girl: Can I touch the frame at least?
Mr. Tong: No. Neither the paper nor the frame. Read the label beside it.

**Question**
What may the girl do with the painting?
- A. Touch the frame.
- B. Put her bag against the wall below it.
- C. Read the label beside it.
- D. Sketch it by tracing it on the painting.

**Answer** C

## A25-13 自行车停在门口车架

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ge, can I bring my bike into the museum hall?
Mr. Ge: No. Leave it at the rack by the main gate.
Boy: Will it still be there after two hours?
Mr. Ge: It will. The rack is watched all day. Bring your lock.

**Question**
Where should the boy leave his bike?
- A. At the rack by the main gate.
- B. In the entrance hall.
- C. In the bicycle room on the second floor.
- D. At the rack in the tea room.

**Answer** A

## A25-14 园里的水都不能喝

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, I am thirsty. Can I drink from the pond?
Mr. Bai: No. Nothing in this park is safe to drink. There is a tap by the gate.
Girl: Is bottled water sold here?
Mr. Bai: Yes, at the shop near the exit. Five yuan a bottle.

**Question**
Where should the girl get water?
- A. From the pond in the park.
- B. From a stream beside the path.
- C. From the lake near the bridge.
- D. From the shop near the exit.

**Answer** D

## A25-15 电影要等灯灭再说话

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Wei, the lights are still on. Can I start talking?
Mr. Wei: Wait until the lights go out. Then talk, but not loudly.
Boy: Why are there three short lights before the film?
Mr. Wei: That is the signal. Sit down and wait for the pictures to move.

**Question**
When should the boy start talking?
- A. As soon as he sits down.
- B. After the lights go out.
- C. When the third light comes on.
- D. Before the film starts, while people are finding seats.

**Answer** B
