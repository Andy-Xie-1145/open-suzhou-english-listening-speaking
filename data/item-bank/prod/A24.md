> A24 球类与比赛规则（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A24-01 篮球走步只能两步

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Wei, I caught the ball and ran to the basket.
Mr. Wei: That is walking, not running. You may take two steps, but stop after them.
Boy: Can I walk again if the ball comes back?
Mr. Wei: No. Pivot, but do not step. Then pass or shoot.

**Question**
How many steps may the boy take after catching the ball?
- A. Three steps.
- B. One step.
- C. Two steps.
- D. Five steps.

**Answer** C

## A24-02 排球不能抱着过网

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Mao, I caught the ball above the net.
Ms. Mao: Then you must hit it over, not carry it. Holding it is a fault.
Girl: Can I hold it for one second?
Ms. Mao: No. Not one second. Hit it over or lose the rally.

**Question**
What must the girl do with the ball above the net?
- A. Hit it over the net.
- B. Hold it for one second.
- C. Throw it under the net.
- D. Drop it and let the other team take it.

**Answer** A

## A24-03 足球只有守门员用手

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Zuo, I used my hand to stop the ball.
Mr. Zuo: Only the goalkeeper may use his hands inside his own area.
Boy: What about catching it with my arm on the field?
Mr. Zuo: That is the same fault. Use your chest, not your arm.

**Question**
Who may use his hands to stop the ball?
- A. Any player inside the penalty area.
- B. The boy, once in the second half.
- C. Nobody on the field.
- D. Only the goalkeeper.

**Answer** D

## A24-04 羽毛球不能过腰发球

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fu, my serve keeps going out.
Mr. Fu: You must hit it below your waist, not above it.
Girl: Can the shuttle touch the net on the serve?
Mr. Fu: It may touch the net. It must not land on the line.

**Question**
Where must the girl hit the shuttle when she serves?
- A. Above her shoulder.
- B. Below her waist.
- C. Above her head.
- D. At the height of her chin.

**Answer** B

## A24-05 乒乓球要抛起来打

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hu, my serve always goes into the net.
Mr. Hu: Throw the ball up and strike it as it falls. Do not push it.
Boy: How high should the throw be?
Mr. Hu: About twenty centimetres, not fifty. A high throw hits your own racket.

**Question**
How should the boy hit the ball when he serves?
- A. Throw it up and strike it as it falls.
- B. Push it gently with his fingers.
- C. Press it down onto the table with his palm.
- D. Hold it and drop it onto his own side.

**Answer** A

## A24-06 网球要落在对方场里

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Dai, my shots keep landing in the net.
Mr. Dai: Hit over the net and inside the other court, not outside it.
Girl: Does the ball have to clear the net by a metre?
Mr. Dai: It has to clear the net only. Height does not matter, direction does.

**Question**
Where must the girl's shot land?
- A. On her own side of the court.
- B. Outside the court.
- C. Inside the other court, over the net.
- D. On the net itself.

**Answer** C

## A24-07 罚球线后才能进线

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, I keep stepping over the line before a free throw.
Mr. Gu: Stay behind the free-throw line until the ball is given to you.
Boy: Can I move onto the line to shoot?
Mr. Gu: No. Both feet stay behind the line. Step in only after you release.

**Question**
When may the boy step onto the free-throw line?
- A. As soon as the ball is given to him.
- B. Only after he releases the ball.
- C. Before every shot, to get a better aim.
- D. Just before the shot, so he can reach the board.

**Answer** B

## A24-08 短跑要用蹲踞式起跑

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Luo, how should I start in the hundred metres?
Ms. Luo: Use a crouch start. One foot in front, the other at your hand.
Girl: Is a standing start better because I am tall?
Ms. Luo: No. Standing start wastes the first half second. Start on "ready".

**Question**
How should the girl start the race?
- A. Standing up straight with her arms down.
- B. On her knees, with both hands behind her.
- C. Walking slowly to the line.
- D. With one foot in front and the other at her hand.

**Answer** D

## A24-09 转身要摸壁再转

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Guo, I always swim past the wall at the end of the length.
Mr. Guo: Touch the wall with your hand, then turn. Do not stop in the middle.
Boy: Can I turn without touching?
Mr. Guo: No. A turn without a touch means you did not finish the length.

**Question**
What should the boy do at the end of the length?
- A. Stop in the middle and rest.
- B. Turn without touching the wall.
- C. Touch the wall with his hand, then turn.
- D. Swim to the stairs and climb out.

**Answer** C

## A24-10 哨响立刻停下来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fu, the whistle went but I was still running.
Mr. Fu: Stop the moment you hear it. Do not finish the lap.
Girl: Does the other team get the ball?
Mr. Fu: They may take the ball. Walking on is a fault, not a strategy.

**Question**
What should the girl do when the whistle goes?
- A. Stop at once.
- B. Finish the lap and then stop.
- C. Slow down but keep going.
- D. Keep running until she passes the line.

**Answer** A

## A24-11 号码要穿在背上

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Xu, where does the number go on my playing shirt?
Mr. Xu: On the back, above the number of the year. Not on the front.
Boy: And on my shorts?
Mr. Xu: Nothing on the shorts. Numbers are for the back only.

**Question**
Where should the boy put the number on his shirt?
- A. On the front.
- B. On the back.
- C. On the front pocket.
- D. On the shorts.

**Answer** B

## A24-12 沙包靠肩带动臂

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Cui, my shot always goes too short.
Mr. Cui: Use the shoulder and the arm together. The elbow must be high.
Girl: Should I use my leg to push?
Mr. Cui: A small step is fine, but the power comes from the shoulder.

**Question**
Where should the power for the shot come from?
- A. From the leg only.
- B. From the wrist only.
- C. From the elbow alone.
- D. From the shoulder and the arm.

**Answer** D

## A24-13 跳高要等裁判信号

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Han, can I jump while the bar is still being moved?
Mr. Han: Wait for the referee's signal, then run up.
Boy: How many attempts do I get?
Mr. Han: Three. If you fail, the next competitor comes.

**Question**
When may the boy start his jump?
- A. After the referee gives the signal.
- B. While the bar is being moved.
- C. Any time, as long as nobody is watching.
- D. Only after he has failed three times.

**Answer** A

## A24-14 实心球单手推

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Gu, how do I throw the shot put?
Mr. Gu: Hold it at your neck and push with one hand. Not two.
Girl: Can I throw it underarm like a basketball?
Mr. Gu: No. The ball must stay above your shoulder until you let it go.

**Question**
How should the girl hold the shot put?
- A. With two hands at her waist.
- B. Under her arm.
- C. At her neck, and push with one hand.
- D. Over her head with two hands.

**Answer** C

## A24-15 篮球场上五个人

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Han, how many of our players are on the court?
Mr. Han: Five from our side and five from theirs.
Girl: Can six play if one is tired?
Mr. Han: No. Six players on the court is a fault, and the game stops.

**Question**
How many players from one team are on the court in a basketball game?
- A. Six.
- B. Five.
- C. Seven.
- D. Three.

**Answer** B
