## B7-01 乐器店的钢琴调音
**Dialogue**
Leo: Mr. Ren, how much does it cost to tune a piano?
Mr. Ren: A full tune of an upright piano is three hundred yuan and takes about two hours. A single note is forty yuan. Most people want the whole thing three times a year.
Leo: Mine was tuned in March. It is now August, so five months.
Mr. Ren: Then you are due. But I can only come on a Thursday, because I am at the school on the other days.
Leo: Is it cheaper to have a few notes done?
Mr. Ren: One note is forty, so three notes are a hundred and twenty and take a quarter of an hour, but the piano will sound wrong everywhere else. I tell people to do the whole thing.
Leo: Then the whole thing. How long must you have the piano?
Mr. Ren: Two hours, and I need the lid open, so please move the stool and the plants first. It is half past one now, so I shall finish at half past three.
Leo: My train home is at four.
**Q1**
How much would Leo pay if he had only three notes tuned instead of the whole piano?
- A. Ninety yuan.
- B. One hundred and twenty yuan.
- C. One hundred and forty yuan.
- D. Three hundred yuan.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
About what time will Mr. Ren finish tuning Leo's piano?
- A. At two o'clock.
- B. At half past two.
- C. At ten past three.
- D. At half past three.
**Answer** D

## B7-02 电瓶车充电桩的充电费用
**Dialogue**
Nina: Mr. Guo, how much does it cost to charge my e-bike here?
Mr. Guo: Two yuan for the first hour and one yuan for every hour or part of an hour after that. The meter starts when you plug in.
Nina: I came at half past six and I am charging now. When shall I come back?
Mr. Guo: The first hour takes you to half past seven. Charge to eight and you pay two yuan plus one yuan, so three yuan in all.
Nina: It is quarter to eight now. If the rain starts at nine, shall I wait?
Mr. Guo: Before half past eight it is three yuan, and half past eight is where the third hour begins, so if you wait for the rain it is four yuan.
Nina: Then I shall stop at eight.
Mr. Guo: Good. The cover over the meter keeps the rain off, but a strong wind blows it open. It is not worth four yuan to save one.
Nina: All right, at eight then.
**Q1**
How much will Nina pay if she stops charging at eight o'clock?
- A. Two yuan.
- B. Four yuan.
- C. Three yuan.
- D. Five yuan.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
How much more will she pay if she waits until the rain begins?
- A. One yuan more.
- B. Two yuan more.
- C. Three yuan more.
- D. Four yuan more.
**Answer** A

## B7-03 社区羽毛球馆的场地费
**Dialogue**
Kate: Ms. He, what does one court cost after school?
Ms. He: Eight yuan an hour for students and six for members. Membership is sixty yuan a term and it is open to anyone over twelve. A court is booked by the hour and cannot be booked for less than one hour.
Kate: We want it from a quarter past four until half past five, and then again from six until seven.
Ms. He: One hour and a quarter, then one hour. At eight yuan an hour that is ten yuan and eight yuan.
Kate: How many hours of play would pay back the membership?
Ms. He: At six yuan an hour, sixty yuan is ten hours. Most families reach that in a term, most young ones do not.
Kate: Our class has eleven names on the list, and we play on Tuesdays.
**Q1**
How much will Kate pay for the two bookings if she does not take out a membership?
- A. Sixteen yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Twenty-four yuan.
- D. Eighteen yuan.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
How many hours of play would pay back the membership?
- A. Ten hours.
- B. Seven and a half hours.
- C. Eight hours.
- D. Six hours.
**Answer** A

## B7-04 社区体检的项目与顺序
**Dialogue**
Emma: I have an appointment for the health check on Tuesday. What shall I do first?
Nurse Lin: Come at seven in the morning, because the blood test must be done on an empty stomach. Then you have a light breakfast in the waiting room, and from half past eight the checks go one after another.
Emma: How long does the whole thing take?
Nurse Lin: The blood test is twenty minutes, the eye test is thirty, and the heart test is thirty. There are ten minutes' wait after each one.
Emma: That is more than two hours?
Nurse Lin: Twenty plus ten, thirty plus ten, thirty plus ten: one hour and fifty minutes from half past eight, so at twenty past ten.
Emma: Then I shall be out before eleven.
Nurse Lin: Yes, and the report is ready on Thursday at four, not before.
**Q1**
What time must Emma arrive, and why?
- A. At half past eight, because the checks start then.
- B. At seven, because the blood test must be done on an empty stomach.
- C. At eight, because the eye test takes thirty minutes.
- D. At half past six, because breakfast ends at seven.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
At about what time will the three checks finish?
- A. At ten o'clock.
- B. At ten past ten.
- C. At twenty past ten.
- D. At half past ten.
**Answer** C

## B7-05 银行换零钱与手续费
**Dialogue**
Leo: Mr. Kong, I have ninety yuan in coins and I would like it in notes.
Mr. Kong: Above fifty yuan a day we charge a flat two yuan. Fifty yuan or less is free.
Leo: So I pay two yuan.
Mr. Kong: The fee is two yuan either way. Do you want the rest in notes?
Leo: I shall take ninety yuan in notes and pay the two yuan on top.
Mr. Kong: Very well. The counter closes at half past four, and it is a quarter past three now. On Friday the counter shuts at noon, so people come on Thursday.
Leo: Today is Thursday.
Mr. Kong: Then you have an hour and fifteen minutes.
Leo: Plenty. Is the small note of five yuan used here?
Mr. Kong: Yes, and one-yuan notes too. Two-yuan notes we stopped taking last year.
**Q1**
How much will Leo pay to change the coins?
- A. Two yuan.
- B. Nothing.
- C. One yuan.
- D. Ninety cents.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
How much time does Leo have before the counter closes?
- A. One hour.
- B. One hour and fifteen minutes.
- C. Twenty minutes.
- D. Fifteen minutes.
**Answer** B

## B7-06 家政阿姨上门做饭的小时费
**Dialogue**
Nina: Ms. Xu, how much do you charge for cooking dinner at my home?
Ms. Xu: Forty yuan an hour, and I do not charge for the bus. Most jobs take an hour and a half and cost sixty yuan.
Nina: Our family is four. Can you come tomorrow?
Ms. Xu: Tomorrow I have another family at four, and the bus to your street takes fifty minutes, so I must leave that home at three. I can start at yours at four and finish at half past five.
Nina: Then dinner is early.
Ms. Xu: You can eat at half past five and keep the rest warm for seven. Setting the table is not charged.
Nina: What about the washing up?
Ms. Xu: Washing up takes thirty-six minutes, so that job is two hours and six minutes and costs eighty-four yuan.
**Q1**
How much will Nina's family pay for tomorrow's cooking job?
- A. Eighty yuan.
- B. Forty yuan.
- C. Ninety yuan.
- D. Sixty yuan.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
How much more would the family pay if the washing up were included?
- A. Twenty yuan.
- B. Forty yuan.
- C. Twenty-four yuan.
- D. Sixty yuan.
**Answer** C

## B7-07 宠物狗的洗澡卡
**Dialogue**
Jack: Ms. Qiao, what does it cost to wash my dog?
Ms. Qiao: A single bath is sixty yuan. A card of five baths is two hundred and fifty, and it is good for twelve months from the day you buy it. A dog over thirty kilos pays ten yuan more for each bath.
Jack: Mine is twenty-two kilos, so no extra. How long is the card?
Ms. Qiao: Twelve months. One card may be used by two people in your family, but not by two dogs on the same day.
Jack: My sister has a small dog. May she use it?
Ms. Qiao: A dog under ten kilos may share your card. Yours is twenty-two kilos and hers is seven, so the card covers both of you, one dog a day.
Jack: When is the shop quiet?
Ms. Qiao: After four o'clock on Tuesdays.
**Q1**
How much does one bath cost if Jack pays for each bath separately?
- A. Sixty yuan.
- B. Seventy yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Two hundred and fifty yuan.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
How much does Jack save if he uses all five baths on the card?
- A. One hundred yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Sixty yuan.
**Answer** C

## B7-08 照相馆拍证件照
**Dialogue**
Emma: Mr. Sun, I need a passport photo. How much is it?
Mr. Sun: Four prints are twelve yuan. If you need six, it is sixteen. All of them are three point five by four point five centimetres, and we cut them on white.
Emma: Six, please. How long does it take?
Mr. Sun: We take the picture at once and the prints are ready in twenty minutes. We open from nine to seven, and we shut between twelve and one for lunch.
Emma: It is half past ten now. Then I can wait here?
Mr. Sun: You can, but your job goes in the morning queue, so the six prints will be ready at half past twelve, not at eleven.
Emma: Can I have them at three instead?
Mr. Sun: You can, but the afternoon queue is forty minutes long, so it would be twenty to four.
**Q1**
How much will Emma pay for six prints?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Sixteen yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Fourteen yuan.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
If Emma comes back at three in the afternoon, about what time will she get the prints?
- A. At half past three.
- B. At half past twelve.
- C. At ten past three.
- D. At twenty to four.
**Answer** D
