> A26 工厂与职业参观（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A26-01 发电厂要戴安全帽

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, may we look at the machines without a hat?
Mr. Guo: No. Wear a safety hat here, not a cap.
Girl: Do we need one indoors too?
Mr. Guo: Especially indoors. Overhead cables come down to this height.

**Question**
What must the girl wear in the power station?
- A. A cloth cap.
- B. Nothing at all.
- C. A safety hat.
- D. A rain hat.

**Answer** C

## A26-02 面包厂要换工作服

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Xin, can I wear my own shoes in the bakery?
Ms. Xin: No. Put on the white work coat and the cap we give you.
Boy: Do I have to give my cap back?
Ms. Xin: Yes, at the gate. Outdoor clothes stay outside the room.

**Question**
What must the boy put on at the gate?
- A. A grey work coat and his own cap.
- B. A cloth cap and his outdoor jacket.
- C. Nothing; he keeps his own clothes.
- D. A white work coat and the cap they give him.

**Answer** D

## A26-03 养鸡场不能拍手

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Dou, the hens all ran away when I clapped.
Mr. Dou: Never clap or shout here. Walk slowly instead.
Girl: Can I call them with food?
Mr. Dou: No. Only the keeper feeds them. Watch from the rail.

**Question**
How should the girl behave in the hen house?
- A. Walk slowly and never clap or shout.
- B. Clap loudly to call the hens to her.
- C. Feed the hens herself from her bag.
- D. Run between the cages to look at them.

**Answer** A

## A26-04 鱼塘边不能跳水

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ku, may I jump into the fish pond to look at the carp?
Mr. Ku: No. The water is deep and cold. Stay behind the rail.
Boy: Can I put my feet in?
Mr. Ku: No. Not your feet. The keeper feeds the fish at four.

**Question**
What may the boy do at the fish pond?
- A. Jump into the pond to look at the carp.
- B. Stay behind the rail and watch.
- C. Put his feet into the water.
- D. Feed the carp at four.

**Answer** B

## A26-05 车间一次进八个人

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Dai, how many of us can go into the workshop at once?
Mr. Dai: Eight. The room takes eight, no more.
Boy: There are eleven. Do three of us wait outside?
Mr. Dai: Yes. The other eight go in with me.

**Question**
How many visitors can go into the workshop at once?
- A. Eight visitors.
- B. Three visitors.
- C. Ten visitors.
- D. Twelve visitors.

**Answer** A

## A26-06 气象站不能放风筝

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, may we fly a kite on the field behind the station?
Mr. Gu: No. Never fly near the station. There are wires up there.
Boy: How far away is safe?
Mr. Gu: A kilometre. Two if the wind is strong.

**Question**
How far from the weather station may the boy fly a kite?
- A. Ten metres.
- B. Two hundred metres.
- C. A kilometre.
- D. Five hundred metres.

**Answer** C

## A26-07 法院旁听要安静

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hao, may we talk during the trial?
Mr. Hao: No talking, no photographs, and put your phone on silent.
Girl: Do we have to stand when the judge comes in?
Mr. Hao: Yes, everyone stands. Sit only after the judge sits down.

**Question**
What must the girl do when the judge comes in?
- A. Sit down at once.
- B. Talk quietly to her friend.
- C. Take a photograph of the judge.
- D. Stand up.

**Answer** D

## A26-08 警察局参观要提前三天

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yu, can we visit the police station tomorrow?
Mr. Yu: Not without a form. Ask for it three days before.
Boy: Can my class teacher send one tomorrow?
Mr. Yu: No. Three days, not one. The group is at ten.

**Question**
How far ahead must the boy ask for the form?
- A. One day.
- B. Three days.
- C. Ten days.
- D. One hour.

**Answer** B

## A26-09 铁厂要戴护目镜

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Quan, why must I put these glasses on before we go in?
Mr. Quan: Sparks fly from the welders. The glasses stop them.
Girl: Can I take them off inside?
Mr. Quan: Not while the welders work. Keep them on until they stop.

**Question**
Why should the girl wear the glasses in the iron works?
- A. To stop sparks from the welders.
- B. To help her see the dark room.
- C. To keep her eyes cool.
- D. To make her look older.

**Answer** A

## A26-10 仓库参观要跟导游

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hui, may I go and look at the boxes by myself?
Mr. Hui: No. Stay with the guide. Do not touch the shelves.
Boy: Can I take a photo of the labels?
Mr. Hui: No. Labels are for the staff. Put the camera away.

**Question**
What must the boy do in the warehouse?
- A. Go and look at the boxes by himself.
- B. Touch the shelves to see what is on them.
- C. Stay with the guide and keep his hands off the shelves.
- D. Take a photo of the labels on the boxes.

**Answer** C

## A26-11 拖拉机只能司机坐

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, may I sit on the tractor for a minute?
Mr. Bai: No. Only the driver sits there, and only while it is still.
Girl: Can I touch the steering wheel after it stops?
Mr. Bai: Even then no. Ask the driver before you touch anything.

**Question**
Who may sit on the tractor?
- A. The girl, for one minute.
- B. Only the driver.
- C. Anyone in a group of two.
- D. Anyone, once it has stopped.

**Answer** B

## A26-12 家具厂要开高窗

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Niu, the air in here smells strong.
Mr. Niu: Open the high window. The glue smell goes out that way.
Girl: Is the glue dangerous?
Mr. Niu: It is not, but the smell is. Open the window, not the door.

**Question**
What should the girl do about the strong smell?
- A. Open the high window.
- B. Close the high window and keep the door shut.
- C. Put her coat over her nose and stay.
- D. Open the small window at floor level.

**Answer** A

## A26-13 低处的果子由果农摘

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhi, may I pull the low apples myself?
Mr. Zhi: No. Those are not ours. The farmer uses a pole for them.
Girl: May I pick up the ones on the ground?
Mr. Zhi: Only into the farmer's basket. Otherwise they go back on the tree.

**Question**
How may the girl get the low apples?
- A. By pulling them down with her own hands.
- B. By standing on a chair and reaching up.
- C. By shaking the tree until they fall.
- D. Not at all; the farmer picks them with a pole.

**Answer** D

## A26-14 玻璃厂末班车五点

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Kang, may I touch the glass that comes out of the furnace?
Mr. Kang: No. It is still hot enough to burn you. Look from the rail.
Boy: When does the shop open?
Mr. Kang: At nine. The last truck leaves at five, not six.

**Question**
When does the last truck leave the glass works?
- A. At six.
- B. At five.
- C. At eight.
- D. At midnight.

**Answer** B

## A26-15 染布房只能摸干布

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Xie, may I touch the cloth in that vat?
Mr. Xie: No. The dye is still wet and it stains for weeks.
Girl: Can I hold the dry cloth on the rack?
Mr. Xie: Yes, the dry one only. And wash your hands after.

**Question**
Which cloth may the girl touch?
- A. The cloth in the dye vat.
- B. Any cloth, with gloves on.
- C. The dry cloth on the rack.
- D. Neither the wet nor the dry cloth.

**Answer** C
