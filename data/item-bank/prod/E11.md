## E11-01 志愿服务与社区
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你参加过哪些志愿服务或社区活动，说说它们给你带来了什么。
**参考作答**：Last summer I joined a volunteer team in my neighbourhood. Every Saturday morning we cleaned the small park near my flat, and two old men always thanked us at the gate. Our team gave away free books at the gate of a primary school last term. I also visited a home for the elderly once a month and talked with the old people there. I was a little afraid at first, because I did not know what to say to them. They asked me many questions about my school, and I learned that listening is more useful than talking. We collected old books for a village school in the countryside. Collecting them took two weekends. This year I want to teach English to children on Sunday afternoons.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：核查作答是否同时给出【服务的固定频次】与【一次具体经历的细节】；仅罗列活动名称而无频次或细节者，不给满分
- 发音准确性：volunteer / neighbourhood / elderly 的音节数；flat 作单音节词
- 表达流利度：every Saturday / once a month / also 的时间状语层次；because 引导原因
- 语法正确性：动名词 Collecting them took two weekends 作主语；learned that 引导宾语从句

## E11-02 网络与手机
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，说说你平时怎样使用网络和手机，谈谈它给你带来的好处与麻烦。
**参考作答**：I use my phone almost every day, mostly to search for words and to talk to my classmates. I also watch short videos when I want to relax after school. At weekends I sometimes do my homework on a tablet instead of paper. My mother says I spend too much time on it, and she is right. Last term I could not sleep well because I stayed online late. Since then I put my phone in another room after ten o'clock. Now I sleep better and finish my homework faster. Still, my phone helps me a lot when I forget a word in class.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：日常用手机查单词、与同学聊天、放松时看短视频、周末改用平板写作业、母亲的提醒、上学期因熬夜作息紊乱、十点后手机放另一房间、睡得好且作业更快、课堂忘词时手机有用
- 发音准确性：search / forget / videos 的音节与词尾辅音
- 表达流利度：mostly / also / still 的补充与转折层次；Since then 的承接
- 语法正确性：could not sleep 与 stayed online 的时态对比；to search / to talk 的不定式表目的

## E11-03 运动与健康
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你保持健康的方式，说说运动给你带来了什么变化。
**参考作答**：I keep myself healthy in three simple ways. I run for twenty minutes in the park every other morning. I sleep nine hours on weekdays and I hardly drink any sugary drink. My mother says walking is the easiest sport to keep. Before I started running, I often felt tired in class. Now I feel more awake in the morning and my legs are stronger. My father says exercise helps him sleep well too. Our class also takes part in a short test every year, and I always pass it easily.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：隔日晨跑二十分钟、工作日睡九小时、少喝含糖饮料、母亲认为散步最容易坚持、以前上课易困、现在更清醒且腿更有力、父亲说运动助眠、全班体检顺利通过
- 发音准确性：healthy / exercise / sugary 的音节；stronger 的比较级读法
- 表达流利度：Before 与 Now 构成对比；also 的补充
- 语法正确性：hardly any 的用法；feel more awake 中的感官动词搭配

## E11-04 阅读与书
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你喜欢的阅读方式，也说说让你印象最深的内容。
**参考作答**：Reading is the quietest hobby I have. I usually read for half an hour before I sleep. I keep a small notebook beside my bed, and I write down the date every time. When I meet a good sentence, I copy it into the notebook. My favourite shop is a second-hand bookshop near my home. Last winter I read a book about a boy who walked from his village to the city to find his teacher. I read it in three days and I could not stop thinking about it. My Chinese teacher once told me that a reader is not a person who reads many books, but one who is often changed by them.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：睡前读半小时、床头放小笔记本并写日期、摘抄好句子、家附近二手书店、去年冬天读的书（男孩走路上学找老师）、三天读完、难以忘怀、老师说读者不是读得多而是被书改变
- 发音准确性：reading / notebook / village 的重音位置；quietest 的最高级
- 表达流利度：When 引导的时间从句；not... but one 的对照
- 语法正确性：定语从句 who walked 与 who reads 的正确使用

## E11-05 出行与交通
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你最近的一次出行，说明你选择的交通方式和沿途的见闻。
**参考作答**：Last National Day my family went to a small town near Lake Tai by train. We left Suzhou at seven in the morning and arrived two hours later. My father bought the tickets three days before the trip. The train was not crowded, so we found seats easily. On the way I could see rice fields and a lot of tall trees through the window. When we got there, an old boatman took us around the lake for an hour. I had never seen such clear water before. The trip taught me that travelling slowly is more relaxing than travelling fast.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：目的地（太湖边小镇）、乘火车、出发与到达时刻、提前三天购票、车上不拥挤、窗外的稻田与高树、老船夫载我们环湖一小时、从未见过这么清的水、慢行比快行更放松的体会
- 发音准确性：arrive / through / crowded 的音节与词尾辅音
- 表达流利度：so 引出结果；When 引导的时间从句
- 语法正确性：一般过去时与现在完成时的对比（arrived 与 had never seen）

## E11-06 环保行动
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你为保护环境做过的事，也谈谈你对垃圾分类的看法。
**参考作答**：I do three small things every week for the environment. I put bottles and paper into different bins at home. My father helps me carry them downstairs on Sunday. I also walk to school instead of taking his car when the weather is fine. My mother says every bag of rubbish we sort saves a little more energy. At first I thought sorting rubbish was boring and useless. My teacher showed us how much glass could be recycled if it went into the wrong bin. Since then I never throw rubbish on the ground.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：每周做三件小事、家中分类投放、周日父亲帮忙、天气好时步行上学、母亲说每袋分类垃圾都省一点能量、原以为分类无聊无用、老师讲玻璃回收量、从那以后不再乱扔
- 发音准确性：environment / recycle / glass 的重音位置
- 表达流利度：instead of 构成的替代；I thought 与 My teacher showed 的转折递进
- 语法正确性：when 引导的时间状语从句；could be recycled 的被动语态

## E11-07 友谊与同学
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你最好的同学，说明你们是怎样成为朋友的。
**参考作答**：My best friend is called Chen Hao, and we have known each other since Grade Seven. At first we sat at different desks and hardly talked. He is taller than me and he plays basketball very well. One day I broke my pencil and he lent me his without saying a word. After that we began to talk about football every day. Our birthdays are only two weeks apart, so we often give each other cards. Now we sit together and we help each other with homework. I think a real friend is the person who stays with you when something goes wrong.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：姓名陈浩、七年级相识、起初不同桌且少说话、他个子高且篮球好、铅笔事件借出铅笔、此后每天聊足球、生日只差两周互送卡片、如今同桌并互帮作业、真朋友是出事时仍在身边的人
- 发音准确性：Chen Hao 的音节划分；hardly / lent 的读音
- 表达流利度：At first、After that、Now 构成时间推进；and 的并列
- 语法正确性：定语从句 who stays；when 引导的时间状语从句

## E11-08 家乡变化
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，说说你的家乡最近几年发生了哪些变化，谈谈你的感受。
**参考作答**：My hometown is a small city near Suzhou, and it has changed a lot in recent years. Five years ago the road near my home was narrow and full of dust. Now it is wide and there are green trees on both sides. A new library was built behind my school last year. Before it opened, many students had nowhere to study quietly. A new bus line also opened near our block last spring. My grandmother says the old city will disappear in a few years. I love the old streets, but I also want a better city for the people who live there.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：家乡位置、几年前路窄多尘、现在路宽且两旁有树、学校后新建图书馆、开放前学生无处安静自习、新增公交线路、奶奶说老城将消失、既爱老街又盼居住者有更好的城市
- 发音准确性：hometown / library / disappear 的重音位置
- 表达流利度：Five years ago 与 Now 的对比；but 的转折
- 语法正确性：there are 的 there be 结构；was built 的被动语态

## E11-09 传统文化
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你感兴趣的中国传统文化，谈谈它为什么值得保留。
**参考作答**：I am interested in Chinese paper cutting, and my grandmother can do it very well. She learned it when she was young, fifty years ago. Every Spring Festival she puts red paper animals on the windows of our flat. The only tools she needs are a pair of scissors and one sheet of paper. Children in our building always stop to watch her hands. Paper cutting has no words at all, and yet it can tell a whole story. My school once held a small show about it. Other people may think it is old-fashioned, but I believe it belongs to everyone.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：中国剪纸、奶奶会剪、五十年前学会、春节贴窗花、只需剪刀与一张纸、楼里的孩子停下来看、剪纸不用文字却能讲故事、学校办过小型展示、有人认为过时而我认为它属于所有人
- 发音准确性：cutting / Spring Festival / children 的音节划分
- 表达流利度：and yet 构成的让步；but I believe 的转折
- 语法正确性：并列句 and my grandmother can do it very well 的主谓一致；it belongs to everyone 的主谓一致

## E11-10 科技与生活
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，说说你最常用的科技工具，谈谈它改变了你生活的哪一点。
**参考作答**：The tool I use most is my phone, and I would be lost without it. I pay for things, take photos, and check my timetable with it every day. My father often asks me to look up a bus time for him. I also check the weather on it before I go out. The change I like most is that I no longer need to carry a paper dictionary. When I started middle school, that book was very heavy in my bag. Now I can open it in two seconds. Technology is useful, but it is not magic.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：最常用工具是手机、支付与拍照与看课表、替父亲查公交时刻、出门前查天气、最喜欢的变化是不再带纸质词典、初中时词典很沉、两秒即可打开、有用但不是魔法
- 发音准确性：technology / dictionary / seconds 的重音与音节
- 表达流利度：Technology is useful but it is not magic 的收尾对照；every day 的频率副词
- 语法正确性：定语从句 The tool I use most is my phone；and I would be lost without it 的并列谓语

## E11-11 时间管理与计划
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你安排学习时间的方式，谈谈计划对你有没有帮助。
**参考作答**：I make a short plan for every evening before dinner. I write my homework on a small card and put it on my desk. The hardest subject always comes first, because I am still fresh at that time. My desk is beside the window, so the card is easy to see. When I finish a subject I put the card back in my bag. If I finish it early, I review my mistakes for half an hour. A plan is not a promise, so I also leave ten minutes for a break. Last month my marks went up, and I finally stopped feeling nervous before an exam.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：每晚睡前做短计划、写在小卡片上放桌上、最难的科目先做（趁清醒）、书桌靠窗卡片显眼、做完一科把卡片收回包里、早完成则复习错题半小时、计划不是承诺要留十分钟休息、上月成绩提高、不再考前紧张
- 发音准确性：plan / promise / nervous 的音节划分
- 表达流利度：because 引导的原因；so 引出结果
- 语法正确性：条件状语从句 If I finish it early 的主谓一致；review 作及物动词

## E11-12 压力与心情
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你遇到压力时的反应，谈谈什么办法对你最有效。
**参考作答**：Before an exam I sometimes feel so nervous that I cannot sleep. I try to tell myself that one paper does not decide everything. Last year I made myself a list of everything I was afraid of, and reading it made the problem smaller. My best friend told me to talk to our teacher, which felt hard at first. I also tried running slowly for twenty minutes, and it worked better than I expected. Now I write three lines about my feelings before every test. My mother says I look calmer now. Doing that really helps me sleep well the night before.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：考前紧张到失眠、劝自己一次考试不定乾坤、去年把害怕的事列成清单且读过就好了、朋友建议跟老师谈（起初觉得难）、慢跑二十分钟（比预想有效）、现在考试前写三行感受、母亲说我现在更镇静、借此睡得好
- 发音准确性：nervous / afraid / expected 的重音位置
- 表达流利度：so...that 构成结果状语；also 的补充
- 语法正确性：afraid of 的介词搭配；make myself a list 中的反身代词

## E11-13 未来与理想
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你将来想做什么，谈谈现在的你为此做了哪些准备。
**参考作答**：In the future I want to be a doctor, because I care about people when they are ill. My grandmother was ill last winter, and the way the doctors spoke to us was calm and kind. So far I have only studied hard at school, and that is not enough. Every weekend I read one book about medicine, which is difficult but useful. I also join school science talks to learn how a real hospital works. My classmates think doctors have long working hours, and they are right. My teacher says medicine needs patience as well as science. I know this road is long, and I am ready to walk it.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：核查作答是否同时给出【目标本身】与【已采取的具体行动】；只表达愿望而无行动者，不给满分
- 发音准确性：future / medicine / hospital 的重音位置
- 表达流利度：because 引导的原因；which is difficult but useful 的对照
- 语法正确性：want to be 表将来；So far 与现在完成时连用
