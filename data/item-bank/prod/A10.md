> A10 校园生活与公共服务（15 题）

> 来源：并行子任务产出｜共 15 份 / 15 题｜体裁：一段对话一道题的四选一

> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。

## A10-01 垃圾分类扔哪只桶
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. He, where do I put this empty paper box?
Ms. He: In the blue bin. It is clean paper.
Boy: What about the banana skin and the used tissues?
Ms. He: They go in the green bin, not the red one. The red bin is only for batteries.
**Question**
Where will the boy put the banana skin and the used tissues?
- A. In the blue bin.
- B. In the red bin.
- C. In the green bin.
- D. In the empty paper box.
**Answer** C

## A10-02 春游要租几辆车
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Tang, grades seven and eight will go on the spring trip. How many coaches?
Ms. Tang: 380 students altogether, and one coach takes 45.
Boy: My friend says seven coaches.
Ms. Tang: Seven seats 315. Eight seats 360 and leaves twenty out. Ten is too many. Book nine.
**Question**
How many coaches will the class book for the spring trip?
- A. Seven.
- B. Eight.
- C. Ten.
- D. Nine.
**Answer** D

## A10-03 书法课要带什么
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fang, what shall I bring for the calligraphy class tomorrow?
Mr. Fang: Bring a brush and a felt mat. Not a pencil.
Girl: Do I need an ink stone and an ink bottle?
Mr. Fang: Yes, and a small cup for water. The school has no paper, so buy eight sheets on the way.
**Question**
What shall the girl buy on her way to school?
- A. Eight sheets of paper.
- B. A brush and an ink stone.
- C. An ink stone and an ink bottle.
- D. A pencil and a felt mat.
**Answer** A

## A10-04 绿豆芽第七天多高
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Gu, our sprouts are seven days old. How tall are they now?
Ms. Gu: They came out on day three. On day seven they are five centimetres high.
Boy: My partner says ten centimetres. He keeps the jar in the sun.
Ms. Gu: Too much sun dries them. Move it away from the window.
**Question**
How tall are the bean sprouts on the seventh day?
- A. Ten centimetres.
- B. Three centimetres.
- C. Five centimetres.
- D. Seven centimetres.
**Answer** C

## A10-05 朗读比赛奖品是什么
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, what do the winners of the reading contest get?
Mr. Pei: First prize is a bookshop ticket worth fifty yuan, not a schoolbag.
Girl: My cousin came second. What does she get?
Mr. Pei: Two science books and a blue notebook. The ticket only goes to first place.
**Question**
What will the girl's cousin get for second place?
- A. A bookshop ticket worth fifty yuan.
- B. Two science books and a blue notebook.
- C. A new schoolbag.
- D. Only a blue notebook.
**Answer** B

## A10-06 台风天停课怎么上课
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Dai, a typhoon may come tomorrow. Will we go to school?
Ms. Dai: No. All lessons move online. Log in at nine with your student card.
Boy: Then the football match on the playground is cancelled, too?
Ms. Dai: Yes. No match and no club. Stay in your own room at home.
**Question**
What will the boy do on the typhoon day?
- A. Play the football match on the playground.
- B. Join the club meeting in the school hall.
- C. Go to school at nine and hand in his student card.
- D. Watch the lessons online in his own room at home.
**Answer** D

## A10-07 停水什么时候来
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hao, our building has no water again. When will it come back?
Mr. Hao: The workers shut the main off at seven this morning.
Girl: My mother wanted to wash the bowls before we left for school at eight.
Mr. Hao: Water returns at one in the afternoon and stays till eight tonight.
**Question**
When will the water come back?
- A. At seven in the morning.
- B. At eight in the morning.
- C. At one in the afternoon.
- D. At eight in the evening.
**Answer** C

## A10-08 大扫除第三组做什么
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Ren, our class is cleaning this afternoon. What does group three do?
Ms. Ren: Group one washes the windows, group two sweeps the floor.
Boy: My friend says group three cleans the blackboard.
Ms. Ren: No. Group three carries the desks out and carries them back at five.
**Question**
What will group three do this afternoon?
- A. Wash the windows.
- B. Carry the desks out of the classroom.
- C. Sweep the floor.
- D. Clean the blackboard.
**Answer** B

## A10-09 全班一共捐了多少钱
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tao, our class is sending money to a school in the mountains.
Mr. Tao: How much did you collect?
Girl: Forty-two students gave two yuan each. My mother added fifty yuan.
Mr. Tao: Two times forty-two is eighty-four. With fifty, that is one hundred and thirty-four yuan.
**Question**
How much money will the class send to the school in the mountains?
- A. One hundred and thirty-four yuan.
- B. Eighty-four yuan.
- C. Fifty yuan.
- D. Forty-two yuan.
**Answer** A

## A10-10 花圃里种什么花
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Wei, our class garden is by the playground. Shall we plant roses?
Mr. Wei: Roses need too much care. Plant mint and marigolds instead.
Boy: My sister says roses are prettier than marigolds.
Mr. Wei: They are, but a class garden needs plants that grow fast and need little water.
**Question**
Which plants will they put in the class garden?
- A. Roses.
- B. Roses and mint.
- C. Only marigolds.
- D. Mint and marigolds.
**Answer** D

## A10-11 全班合影女生站哪里
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, the class photo is being taken on the steps at nine.
Mr. Yu: The tall boys sit in the back row, not the front.
Girl: Where do the girls stand?
Mr. Yu: In the front row, beside me. The monitor stands on my left.
**Question**
Where will the girls stand for the class photo?
- A. In the back row.
- B. In the front row, beside Mr. Yu.
- C. On the steps behind Mr. Yu.
- D. With the class monitor.
**Answer** B

## A10-12 干洗大衣多少钱
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Long, my winter coat is dirty. Should I wash it at home?
Mr. Long: Too thick for our machine. Send it to the dry cleaner's.
Boy: My mother says it takes two days and costs ten yuan.
Mr. Long: Two days is too short; it takes four. The price is twenty yuan, not ten.
**Question**
How much will the boy's coat cost at the dry cleaner's?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Four yuan.
- C. Two yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** D

## A10-13 地震演习怎么做
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Cui, our class will have a drill next Monday morning.
Ms. Cui: Yes. When the bell rings, get under your desk and hold its leg.
Girl: Then we all run out to the playground?
Ms. Cui: No. Stay under the desk till the second bell. Do not stand in the hall.
**Question**
What should the girl do when the bell rings?
- A. Get under the desk and hold its leg.
- B. Run out to the playground with the other classes.
- C. Stand in the hall with her teacher.
- D. Hold her desk and carry it to the playground.
**Answer** A

## A10-14 端午节吃什么
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yan, what does your family eat at the Dragon Boat Festival?
Mr. Yan: We make zongzi on the fifth day.
Boy: My mother buys mooncakes for it. My aunt makes dumplings.
Mr. Yan: Dumplings are for Spring Festival and mooncakes for September. For the fifth day, zongzi.
**Question**
What will Mr. Yan's family eat on the fifth day of the fifth month?
- A. Mooncakes.
- B. Zongzi.
- C. Dumplings.
- D. Mooncakes and dumplings.
**Answer** B

## A10-15 家长开放日家长做什么
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Kan, parents' open day is Wednesday. What will they do?
Ms. Kan: They come to room 203 at eight.
Boy: My mother wants to see the PE lesson on the playground.
Ms. Kan: The PE lesson is on Thursday. The hall show is on Friday. On Wednesday parents sit at the back and hear Chinese.
**Question**
What will the parents do on the open day?
- A. Watch a PE lesson on the playground.
- B. Go to the school hall for the show.
- C. Hear a Chinese lesson in room 203.
- D. Watch the PE lesson on Thursday.
**Answer** C
