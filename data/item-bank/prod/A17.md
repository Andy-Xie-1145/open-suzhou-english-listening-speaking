> A17 生活安排与数字（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A17-01 迟到要罚站多久

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. He, I got to school at seven fifty-two. Class begins at eight.
Ms. He: Yes, you are eight minutes late.
Boy: How long must I stand outside?
Ms. He: One minute for every minute you are late, so eight minutes by the door.
Boy: All right. Eight minutes.

**Question**
How long must the boy stand outside the classroom?
- A. Four minutes.
- B. Twenty minutes.
- C. Eight minutes.
- D. Twelve minutes.

**Answer** C

## A17-02 早读几点开始

**Dialogue**
Ms. Zhou: Our English reading starts at eight. The bell rings at eight.
Boy: But the first class begins at eight forty.
Ms. Zhou: Right. Reading comes first, so leave home at seven twenty.
Boy: Seven twenty, then eight for reading. Thank you, Ms. Zhou.

**Question**
When should the boy leave home for English reading?
- A. At seven twenty.
- B. At seven forty.
- C. At eight forty.
- D. At eight o'clock.

**Answer** A

## A17-03 午休后几点回教室

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ding, what time does lunch end?
Mr. Ding: Lunch is from eleven forty to twelve twenty.
Girl: And after that?
Mr. Ding: Twenty minutes' quiet time, so we go back at twelve forty.
Girl: Twelve twenty to twelve forty. That is twenty minutes.

**Question**
When will the students go back to class after lunch?
- A. At eleven forty.
- B. At twelve twenty.
- C. At twelve fifty.
- D. At twelve forty.

**Answer** D

## A17-04 二十四支铅笔怎么分

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Fan, we have twenty-four new pencils for the art class.
Ms. Fan: There are six of you. Share them equally.
Boy: Twenty-four for six. So each of us gets four, right?
Ms. Fan: Exactly. Not five, and keep the two long rulers in the box.

**Question**
How many pencils will each student get?
- A. Six pencils.
- B. Four pencils.
- C. Five pencils.
- D. Twenty-four pencils.

**Answer** B

## A17-05 离开教室要关什么

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ye, our classroom is dark after the last class. Who turns off the computer?
Mr. Ye: The last student out does it. Switch off the computer, the screen and the lights.
Girl: What about the fan in summer?
Mr. Ye: The fan too, not only the lights.
Girl: Computer, screen, lights and fan. Four things.

**Question**
What should the last student switch off before leaving?
- A. The computer, the screen and the fan.
- B. The lights and the screen only.
- C. The computer, the screen, the lights and the fan.
- D. The lights only.

**Answer** C

## A17-06 煮鸡蛋要几分钟

**Dialogue**
Girl: Grandma, the water is boiling. How long do we cook the eggs?
Grandma: Put the eggs in now, and count eight minutes on the clock.
Girl: Eight minutes, not ten? My mother usually says ten.
Grandma: Ten is too long. Take them out at eight and put them in cold water.

**Question**
How long should the girl cook the eggs?
- A. Ten minutes.
- B. Six minutes.
- C. Twelve minutes.
- D. Eight minutes.

**Answer** D

## A17-07 洗衣机要转多久

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mum, these clothes are dirty. How long does the machine take?
Mum: It washes for thirty-five minutes, then rinses twice, five minutes each.
Boy: So thirty-five plus five plus five?
Mum: That's forty-five minutes in all. Hang them out right after.

**Question**
How long does the washing machine take altogether?
- A. Forty-five minutes.
- B. Thirty-five minutes.
- C. Ten minutes.
- D. Fifty minutes.

**Answer** A

## A17-08 仙人球多久浇一次水

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Bai, I bought a small cactus for my desk. How often should I water it?
Mr. Bai: In winter, once every twenty days. Not every day.
Boy: Then in summer it needs more, doesn't it?
Mr. Bai: Yes, every ten days, and never twice on the same day.

**Question**
How often should the boy water the cactus in winter?
- A. Every day.
- B. Every twenty days.
- C. Every ten days.
- D. Every five days.

**Answer** B

## A17-09 指甲多久剪一次

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Qi, my fingernails are getting long. How often should I cut them?
Ms. Qi: Once a week. Boys often cut them every two weeks.
Girl: Every two weeks seems too long for me.
Ms. Qi: Then cut yours on Sunday night. Short ones are easier to keep clean.

**Question**
How often should the girl cut her fingernails?
- A. Every day.
- B. Every two weeks.
- C. Once a week.
- D. Every two days.

**Answer** C

## A17-10 电梯坏了走几层

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ren, the lift is not working today. How many floors do I walk?
Mr. Ren: You live on the seventh, not the tenth. Seven floors, ten minutes.
Boy: Ten minutes? With two bags of books?
Mr. Ren: Take them one at a time. Do not wait for the lift at noon.

**Question**
How many floors does the boy need to walk?
- A. Five.
- B. Seven.
- C. Ten.
- D. Twelve.

**Answer** B

## A17-11 配眼镜要等几天

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Han, I need new glasses. How long do they take to make?
Ms. Han: Four days. You choose the frames today and come back on Thursday.
Girl: Not tomorrow? My old ones broke on Tuesday.
Ms. Han: You can still see with them for four days. Do not buy new frames.

**Question**
When can the girl pick up her new glasses?
- A. On Tuesday.
- B. On the next day.
- C. In four hours.
- D. On Thursday.

**Answer** D

## A17-12 星期几还钱

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Xu, my cousin lent me twenty yuan last week. What should I do?
Mr. Xu: Pay him back on Friday, not today.
Boy: Should I also buy him a gift?
Mr. Xu: No need. Pay the money first, and keep the change in your wallet.

**Question**
When should the boy pay his cousin back?
- A. Today.
- B. On Wednesday.
- C. On Friday.
- D. On Saturday.

**Answer** C

## A17-13 楼道灯坏了找谁说

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Guo, the light on our stair is out. Who do I tell?
Ms. Guo: Call the office on the first floor, not the shop next door.
Boy: The office closes at five, so I cannot go after class.
Ms. Guo: Then tell your landlord on your way home, not at the office.

**Question**
Who should the boy tell about the broken light today?
- A. His landlord.
- B. The shop next door.
- C. The office on the first floor.
- D. The office teacher at school.

**Answer** A

## A17-14 肉要放多久才能切

**Dialogue**
Boy: Dad, I took the meat out of the fridge. Can I cut it now?
Dad: No. Leave it on the table for forty minutes.
Boy: Forty minutes? My mother says half an hour.
Dad: Half an hour is for thin meat. Ours is thick, so wait forty minutes.

**Question**
How long must the boy wait before cutting the meat?
- A. Thirty minutes.
- B. Ten minutes.
- C. Forty minutes.
- D. An hour.

**Answer** C

## A17-15 洗手要洗多久

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Song, how long should I wash my hands before lunch?
Ms. Song: Twenty seconds with soap, not ten, and rub the backs of your hands.
Girl: Twenty seconds? That sounds shorter than I thought.
Ms. Song: Count to twenty out loud. Then dry them with your own towel.

**Question**
How long should the girl wash her hands with soap?
- A. Ten seconds.
- B. One minute.
- C. Half a minute.
- D. Twenty seconds.

**Answer** D
