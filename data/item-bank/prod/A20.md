> A20 家务与居家安排（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A20-01 订两桶桶装水

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Liang, our water bottle is empty.
Ms. Liang: Call 6321 and order two. A full one costs eighteen yuan.
Boy: Do we pay the man who brings it?
Ms. Liang: Yes, pay him at the door. Don't send me a message.

**Question**
How much will the boy pay for two bottles of water?
- A. Eighteen yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Six yuan.
- D. Thirty-six yuan.

**Answer** D

## A20-02 换灯泡要一百瓦

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. He, the bulb in the hall has gone.
Mr. He: Buy a hundred-watt bulb, not a forty-watt one.
Girl: The old bulb says sixty watts. Why change it?
Mr. He: The room is dark now. A hundred watts is right. Fit it after you cut the power.

**Question**
Which bulb should the girl buy?
- A. A forty-watt bulb.
- B. A hundred-watt bulb.
- C. A sixty-watt bulb.
- D. A pair of forty-watt bulbs.

**Answer** B

## A20-03 春节前贴春联

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wu, when shall we put up the couplets?
Mr. Wu: On New Year's Eve, before sunset. Not on the first morning.
Girl: Should we use glue or nails?
Mr. Wu: Glue, on the old paper. Take the old one down first.

**Question**
When will they put up the new couplets?
- A. On the first morning of the year.
- B. On New Year's Eve after sunset.
- C. On New Year's Eve before sunset.
- D. On New Year's Eve after dinner.

**Answer** C

## A20-04 厨房油污用洗洁精

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Zhou, there is oil on the kitchen wall.
Mr. Zhou: Use washing-up liquid and warm water. Not the dry powder.
Boy: How long must I wait after?
Mr. Zhou: Two minutes, then wipe it. The powder leaves white marks.

**Question**
What should the boy use to clean the oily wall?
- A. Washing-up liquid.
- B. The dry cleaning powder.
- C. Hot water only.
- D. Warm water only.

**Answer** A

## A20-05 订煤气要留电话号码

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Gong, our gas bottle is nearly empty.
Ms. Gong: Ring the shop and leave your phone number.
Girl: Should I tell him which floor we live on?
Ms. Gong: Yes, the fourth floor. He comes in the morning, not at night.

**Question**
When will the gas man come?
- A. At nine at night.
- B. At noon on Sunday.
- C. After dinner.
- D. In the morning.

**Answer** D

## A20-06 客人来了擦桌子

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Dai, my uncle is coming for dinner.
Mr. Dai: Wipe the table first. Cover it with the blue cloth, not the white one.
Boy: Shall I set out the big bowls?
Mr. Dai: Four people, so four small bowls. Put the big bowl away.

**Question**
How many small bowls should the boy set out?
- A. Three.
- B. Six.
- C. Four.
- D. One big bowl.

**Answer** C

## A20-07 车位被占找保安

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tong, another car is in my parking place.
Mr. Tong: Take the number of the car. Go to the office, not to the street.
Girl: Can I move the car myself?
Mr. Tong: No. The security man will move it. Don't call the police.

**Question**
Who will move the other car out of the parking place?
- A. The girl herself.
- B. The security man.
- C. Mr. Tong.
- D. The police.

**Answer** B

## A20-08 窗帘布要两倍宽

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yuan, how do I choose curtains for my room?
Mr. Yuan: Measure the window first. A wide one needs a double rail.
Girl: My window is 120 wide and 150 high.
Mr. Yuan: Then the cloth must be 240 wide, not 120. That is twice the width.

**Question**
How wide should the cloth be?
- A. 240 cm.
- B. 120 cm.
- C. 150 cm.
- D. 360 cm.

**Answer** A

## A20-09 换床单要三床

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Tao, how many sheets do I need for the two beds?
Ms. Tao: Two for the beds, one for your mother's bed. Three in all.
Girl: Do I wash the old ones first?
Ms. Tao: Wash them at the weekend. Not tonight.

**Question**
How many sheets will the girl wash at the weekend?
- A. Two.
- B. One.
- C. Five.
- D. Three.

**Answer** D

## A20-10 腌咸菜放五十克盐

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hu, how much salt do we need for one kilo of vegetables?
Mr. Hu: Fifty grams. Not eighty, or the food turns black.
Boy: Do we weigh it?
Mr. Hu: Yes, with the kitchen scale. Two hundred grams is too much.

**Question**
How much salt will the boy use for one kilo of vegetables?
- A. Fifty grams.
- B. Eighty grams.
- C. Two hundred grams.
- D. Twenty grams.

**Answer** A

## A20-11 电饭煲预约六点半

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Fu, can the rice cooker start by itself?
Mr. Fu: Yes. Set it for six thirty, and dinner is ready at seven.
Boy: I have to leave at seven ten for football practice.
Mr. Fu: Then leave the cooker on, not off. It keeps warm for two hours.

**Question**
What time should the boy set the rice cooker?
- A. At five thirty.
- B. At six o'clock.
- C. At six thirty.
- D. At seven ten.

**Answer** C

## A20-12 擦玻璃要用两块布

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Cui, the window in my room is very dirty.
Mr. Cui: Use two cloths, one wet and one dry. Not one cloth for both.
Girl: Is there a special order?
Mr. Cui: Wash the top first, then the bottom. Never use your sleeve.

**Question**
How many cloths does the girl need to clean the window?
- A. One.
- B. Two.
- C. Three.
- D. Four.

**Answer** B

## A20-13 电视没图像先拔插头

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Wei, the TV has no picture today.
Mr. Wei: Unplug it and plug it in again. Don't press the buttons.
Boy: Should I call the repair man?
Mr. Wei: Try that first. Call him only if it still fails after ten minutes.

**Question**
When should the boy call the repair man?
- A. At once, before trying anything.
- B. Never.
- C. After one minute.
- D. After ten minutes if it still fails.

**Answer** D

## A20-14 地毯要用吸尘器

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, our carpet is full of dust.
Mr. Guo: Vacuum it twice, not once. Move the chairs first.
Girl: Can I clean it with a wet cloth?
Mr. Guo: No, that is for the floor. Use the vacuum, not water.

**Question**
How should the girl clean the carpet?
- A. With a vacuum cleaner, twice.
- B. With a wet cloth, once.
- C. With a broom, once.
- D. With water and a brush.

**Answer** A

## A20-15 修鞋摊搬到市场东门

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hao, my boots are broken. Is the shoe stall open today?
Mr. Hao: No. He moved to the east gate of the market. He opens at nine.
Girl: Can you mend them here?
Mr. Hao: I can't. Bring them back tomorrow and I will show you the way.

**Question**
Where is the shoe stall now?
- A. At the west gate of the market.
- B. In front of our building.
- C. At the east gate of the market.
- D. Behind the school.

**Answer** C
