## A16-01 盆栽换个大点的盆
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Hu, my plant doesn't look well. The pot is too small.
Ms. Hu: Move it into a bigger pot tomorrow, but a medium one.
Girl: How much soil? A full bag is ten yuan.
Ms. Hu: Half a bag is enough. Put it by the window, not in the hall.
**Question**
What will the girl do with the plant tomorrow?
- A. Move it into a large pot.
- B. Move it into a medium pot.
- C. Leave it in the small pot by the window.
- D. Move it to a big pot in the hall.
**Answer** B

## A16-02 遥控器要几节电池
**Dialogue**
Boy: Grandma, the remote control does not work. How many batteries?
Grandma: Two, not one. Change them every year.
Boy: My cousin changes them every month.
Grandma: That is too often. Put them back before you sleep.
**Question**
How many batteries does the remote control need?
- A. Four.
- B. Three.
- C. Two.
- D. One.
**Answer** C

## A16-03 洗衣粉要放多少
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fang, my father is washing. How much powder shall I put in?
Mr. Fang: For these five kilos of clothes, use half a bag.
Girl: The bag holds two kilos. My friend pours in a whole bag.
Mr. Fang: Don't. Soak the shirts for ten minutes, then wash them by hand.
**Question**
How much washing powder will the girl put in?
- A. Half a bag.
- B. A whole bag.
- C. A quarter of a bag.
- D. Two bags.
**Answer** A

## A16-04 羊毛衫要选冷水档
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Guo, my shirt is wool. Which program shall I choose?
Ms. Guo: Not the thirty-degree cotton one. Wool needs the cold program.
Boy: How long does the cold program take?
Ms. Guo: Fifty minutes. The quick one is only fifteen, and that is not enough for wool.
Boy: Then I shall dry it flat, not in the machine.
**Question**
Which program does Ms. Guo tell the boy to use?
- A. The thirty-degree cotton program.
- B. The quick fifteen-minute program.
- C. The cold program for wool.
- D. The spin-only program.
**Answer** C

## A16-05 买鱼要不要代杀
**Dialogue**
Boy: Aunt Xue, I'm buying a fish at the market. Should she kill it?
Aunt Xue: Yes, she does that for free. Ask her to clean it as well.
Boy: I need it only for tonight. My mother comes home late.
Aunt Xue: Then buy a big one, about one kilo, not the small ones.
**Question**
Which fish will the boy buy?
- A. A small fish.
- B. Two small fish.
- C. A big fish of two kilos.
- D. A big fish of about one kilo.
**Answer** D

## A16-06 带饭要热几分钟
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Du, I bring a lunch box to school. How long should I heat it?
Mr. Du: Five minutes in the microwave, not three. Turn it over midway.
Girl: My friend heats hers for ten minutes. Her rice gets very dry.
Mr. Du: Let her add a little water and take the box back on Friday.
**Question**
How long should the girl heat her lunch?
- A. Three minutes.
- B. Five minutes.
- C. Ten minutes.
- D. Twenty minutes.
**Answer** B

## A16-07 拍毕业照提前多久到
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, our class photo is on Saturday. When shall we get to the park?
Mr. Bai: Be there at eight in the morning, not at nine.
Girl: The shop says nine. It is twenty-five minutes' walk from here.
Mr. Bai: Then leave home at half past seven. Bring your red scarf.
**Question**
When shall the class get to the park on Saturday?
- A. At nine in the morning.
- B. At half past seven in the morning.
- C. At eight in the morning.
- D. At half past eight in the morning.
**Answer** C

## A16-08 一分钟跳绳要练多少个
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qi, our class has a rope-skipping test. How many shall I do in a minute?
Ms. Qi: Aim at a hundred and twenty, not a hundred and fifty.
Boy: I can only jump eighty now.
Ms. Qi: Do it at seven in the morning, before breakfast. Ten minutes a day is enough.
**Question**
How many jumps should the boy aim at in a minute?
- A. One hundred and twenty.
- B. One hundred and fifty.
- C. Eighty.
- D. Two hundred.
**Answer** A

## A16-09 停电了在家吃面包
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Pei, our building has no electricity tonight. Can we cook dinner?
Ms. Pei: No. The repair men come at seven, so the lights are off until nine.
Boy: Shall we eat at the restaurant near the gate?
Ms. Pei: No. Eat the bread and eggs at home. I have two loaves here.
**Question**
What will they eat tonight?
- A. Dinner at the restaurant near the gate.
- B. Nothing at all, because there is no electricity.
- C. Bread and eggs at Ms. Pei's home.
- D. Bread and eggs at the restaurant near the gate.
**Answer** C

## A16-10 网断了先用手机流量
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yan, my home net is down again. My homework is on the computer.
Mr. Yan: Call the company before seven. It opens at eight, not at nine.
Girl: The line was busy last time. A man comes at ten tomorrow.
Mr. Yan: Use your phone's data today. Don't change the router yourself.
**Question**
What will the girl do with her homework today?
- A. Use the data on her phone.
- B. Change the router herself.
- C. Wait at home until the man comes.
- D. Call the company at nine tomorrow.
**Answer** A

## A16-11 图书角还缺几本书
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Lu, our class reading corner has only twelve books. Can we donate more?
Ms. Lu: Yes. We need thirty in all, so bring eighteen from home.
Boy: My sister has ten. I have read them all twice already.
Ms. Lu: Give her old ones, not the new storybooks. Put them on the shelf today.
**Question**
How many books should the boy bring from home?
- A. Ten books.
- B. Twelve books.
- C. Thirty books.
- D. Eighteen books.
**Answer** D

## A16-12 衬衫的扣子掉了
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Shao, a button came off my white shirt. Where shall I take it?
Ms. Shao: A small shop near the station can do it for five yuan.
Girl: I have no time before six. Can I do it myself?
Ms. Shao: Yes, use white thread. Don't use the black one in your mother's box.
**Question**
What will the girl do about the button?
- A. Take the shirt to a shop near the station.
- B. Sew it on herself with white thread.
- C. Sew it on with the black thread in her mother's box.
- D. Buy a new white shirt at the shop.
**Answer** B

## A16-13 晒被子要晒多久
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Yu, my mother wants to dry the quilt on the roof.
Ms. Yu: Put it up at ten in the morning and take it in at one.
Boy: It rained at three last week, so my friend's quilt got wet.
Ms. Yu: That's right. Dry it on the sunny side, not the north side.
**Question**
When should the boy take the quilt in?
- A. At one in the afternoon.
- B. At four in the afternoon.
- C. At three in the afternoon.
- D. At ten in the morning.
**Answer** A

## A16-14 楼道里的纸箱谁搬走
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hou, three big boxes have stood in our hallway for two days.
Mr. Hou: Don't move them yourself. Ask Mr. Li on the fifth floor.
Girl: He says they are not his. A moving man brought them on Sunday.
Mr. Hou: Then call the manager's number on the wall.
**Question**
Whom should the girl call about the boxes?
- A. Mr. Li on the fifth floor.
- B. The moving man who brought them.
- C. The manager of the building.
- D. Nobody; leave the boxes where they are.
**Answer** C

## A16-15 单词一天记多少个
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Kan, I want to learn more English words. How many a day?
Mr. Kan: Fifteen new ones, not thirty. Ten of them before breakfast.
Girl: I write all thirty in my notebook. I never go over them again.
Mr. Kan: Go over the old ten first. Review them on Sunday, not on Friday.
**Question**
How many new words should the girl learn a day?
- A. Ten words.
- B. Fifteen words.
- C. Twenty words.
- D. Thirty words.
**Answer** B
