> A14 校园与家务（15 题）
> 仿写练习素材，非真题。本型为「一个对话配一道四选一题」，无官方题型依据，材料与题目均为仿写。

## A14-01 换到窗边第三排
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Deng, may I change my seat? The sun comes in here after lunch.
Ms. Deng: The front row is full. Take the seat by the window in row three, not the one near the door.
Girl: Is Mr. Hu's desk free?
Ms. Deng: No, Mr. Hu moves there on Monday. Come to me before class on Tuesday.
**Question**
Which seat will the girl take?
- A. The seat in the front row.
- B. The seat near the door.
- C. The seat by the window in row three.
- D. Mr. Hu's desk.
**Answer** C

## A14-02 零花钱分成三份
**Dialogue**
Boy: Dad, I have thirty yuan this week. May I buy a toy?
Dad: Put ten yuan in a box for books, ten yuan in a box for the school trip, and keep the rest.
Boy: Then the toy must be very small.
Dad: That's right. Keep it in the box.
**Question**
How much money will the boy have for the toy?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Thirty yuan.
- D. Five yuan.
**Answer** A

## A14-03 水龙头请人上门修
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Fang, the tap in our kitchen drips all night. Can you come and fix it?
Mr. Fang: I'm free on Tuesday afternoon, not on Saturday morning.
Boy: My parents are at work on Tuesday. Can you come on Thursday?
Mr. Fang: On Thursday I can come at nine. Bring a new washer, not a new tap.
**Question**
When will Mr. Fang come to the boy's home?
- A. On Thursday in the afternoon.
- B. On Thursday at nine in the morning.
- C. On Tuesday afternoon.
- D. On Saturday morning.
**Answer** B

## A14-04 停电只上半天的课
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, there's no electricity in my building tonight. Will we have class tomorrow?
Ms. Hu: Yes, but only in the morning. The power will be off from one o'clock to six.
Boy: Then my class in the afternoon is off too, isn't it?
Ms. Hu: That's right. Go home at twelve, not at three.
**Question**
When will the boy go home tomorrow?
- A. At one in the afternoon.
- B. At three in the afternoon.
- C. At six in the evening.
- D. At twelve in the morning.
**Answer** D

## A14-05 科学小组看月亮
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ma, our science club will watch the moon tonight. When shall we meet?
Mr. Ma: Not at eight. The moon comes up at half past seven.
Girl: The gate closes at nine. Shall we meet inside the lab?
Mr. Ma: No. Meet under the big tree by the east gate at a quarter to nine.
**Question**
At what time will the science club meet?
- A. At half past seven.
- B. At a quarter to nine.
- C. At eight o'clock.
- D. At nine o'clock.
**Answer** B

## A14-06 墙报要做两米宽
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ding, how big should our wall newspaper be?
Mr. Ding: Two metres wide and one metre high. My class used four sheets last year.
Boy: I drew it on four small sheets. Can we just use four again?
Mr. Ding: No, use eight this year. Join them and leave a white edge two fingers wide.
**Question**
How wide will the wall newspaper be?
- A. Two metres.
- B. One metre.
- C. Four metres.
- D. Eight metres.
**Answer** A

## A14-07 周记写一百字
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, I never write my diary. How long should each entry be?
Mr. Guo: A hundred words, not thirty. Write it on Friday evening, not on Sunday night.
Girl: Sunday is my busiest day. What about a hundred and fifty words?
Mr. Guo: No. Keep to a hundred, not a hundred and fifty. Ten lines will do.
**Question**
How many words should each diary entry have?
- A. Thirty words.
- B. A hundred and fifty words.
- C. A hundred words.
- D. A hundred and twenty words.
**Answer** C

## A14-08 消防演习站到东墙前
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qian, will our class have a fire drill tomorrow morning?
Ms. Qian: Yes, at ten, not at nine. When the bell rings, walk down the stairs and do not run.
Boy: Should I take my schoolbag with me?
Ms. Qian: No. Everyone will stand on the playground in front of the east wall, not in the hall.
**Question**
Where will the students stand during the fire drill?
- A. In the school hall.
- B. On the playground in front of the west wall.
- C. On the playground in front of the east gate.
- D. On the playground in front of the east wall.
**Answer** D

## A14-09 拼图还差三块
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yuan, this puzzle has ninety-six pieces. I can only find ninety.
Mr. Yuan: Look under the sofa. My son played with it there on Saturday.
Girl: Under the sofa I find three more pieces. How many are still missing?
Mr. Yuan: Three. Ninety on the table, three under the sofa, ninety-six in all.
**Question**
How many pieces are still missing?
- A. Six.
- B. Three.
- C. Ninety.
- D. Ninety-three.
**Answer** B

## A14-10 去草丛里找蚂蚁
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Song, we want to watch ants today. Where should we look?
Ms. Song: Not on the playground. Look in the grass behind the library, not near the car park.
Boy: My friend says ants come out in the rain. Is that true?
Ms. Song: No, they come out when the ground is dry. Look from the path.
**Question**
Where will the boy look for the ants?
- A. In the grass behind the library.
- B. On the playground.
- C. Near the car park.
- D. In the grass behind the office building.
**Answer** A

## A14-11 晾衣服前先拧干
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mom, I washed my T-shirt. Can I hang it out now?
Mom: Wring the water out first, not after. Hang it in the sun, not under the balcony.
Girl: It'll take long to dry. Shall I use the dryer?
Mom: No, that makes the shirt smaller. Hang it out before eight, and bring it in before six.
**Question**
When should the girl bring the shirt in?
- A. Before eight in the morning.
- B. At six in the morning.
- C. Before eight in the evening.
- D. Before six in the evening.
**Answer** D

## A14-12 书法比赛用毛笔
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Wei, the writing competition is next week. What should I bring?
Mr. Wei: A brush and ink, not a pen or a pencil. Practice twenty minutes a day.
Boy: May I use my mother's brush? Mine is too old.
Mr. Wei: No, borrow one from the art room. Write your name on the handle.
**Question**
What will the boy use to write at the competition?
- A. A pen from home.
- B. His mother's brush.
- C. A brush from the art room.
- D. A pencil.
**Answer** C

## A14-13 历史故事三人来讲
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Fang, our history class will tell stories on Friday. How many do you need?
Ms. Fang: Three, not four. Each of you has five minutes, not ten.
Girl: I want to tell the one about the Yellow River. May I be first?
Ms. Fang: Yes, you go first. The other two are Li Bo and Chen Jie.
**Question**
Who will tell a story first on Friday?
- A. Li Bo.
- B. The girl.
- C. Chen Jie.
- D. Ms. Fang.
**Answer** B

## A14-14 借自行车要押证件
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, can I borrow a bicycle from the school? What do I need?
Ms. Hu: Your student card. Not a passport. The lock is already on it, so bring none.
Boy: Do I have to give the card back at once?
Ms. Hu: Yes, at five, when you bring the bike back.
**Question**
What must the boy leave at the office?
- A. His student card.
- B. His passport.
- C. A lock.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** A

## A14-15 罚抄单词二十遍
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. He, I forgot to hand in my homework again. How many times must I copy the word?
Ms. He: Twenty, not ten. Copy it in your notebook, not in the textbook.
Boy: Twenty times? My notebook is nearly full.
Ms. He: Then take a new one. Write it before dinner, not after.
**Question**
How many times must the boy copy the word?
- A. Ten times.
- B. Twice.
- C. Twenty times.
- D. Twelve times.
**Answer** C
