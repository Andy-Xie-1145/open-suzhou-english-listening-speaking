## A13-01 物业费下周五交

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Dai, has our building's service fee been paid?
Ms. Dai: Not yet. It is due next Friday, not this Friday.
Boy: How much is it for our family?
Ms. Dai: Two hundred yuan a month. Give it to me, not to the office.
Boy: Two hundred for March, then. Thanks, Ms. Dai.

**Question**
When is the service fee due?
- A. This Friday.
- B. Next Monday.
- C. This Saturday.
- D. Next Friday.

**Answer** D

## A13-02 楼上半夜的钢琴声

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fan, I can't sleep. The boy upstairs plays the piano until midnight.
Mr. Fan: His mother says he only practises for ten minutes.
Girl: Ten minutes? I hear it for an hour. What should I do?
Mr. Fan: Write down the hours for a week, then let the office call her.

**Question**
What does Mr. Fan suggest the girl do?
- A. Write down the hours for a month, then let the office call her.
- B. Write down the hours for a week, then call the mother herself.
- C. Write down the hours for a week, then let the office call her.
- D. Write down the hours for a month, then let the boy call her.

**Answer** C

## A13-03 数学题不会做

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, question five is hard. I've been on it for half an hour.
Ms. Hu: Reading it again won't help. Copy it into your notebook and ask me at four.
Boy: Our math lesson is at four. Could I ask you at three fifteen?
Ms. Hu: Yes, but bring the notebook, not the test paper.

**Question**
When will the boy ask Ms. Hu for help?
- A. At four.
- B. At three fifteen.
- C. At half past three.
- D. At half past four.

**Answer** B

## A13-04 美术课要带什么材料

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, what do we need for tomorrow's art lesson?
Mr. Yu: Two brushes, a bottle of black ink and some old newspapers.
Girl: How many sheets of paper should I bring?
Mr. Yu: Only small ones. Leave the big roll at home.
Girl: Two brushes and one bottle. Understood, Mr. Yu.

**Question**
What will the girl take to the art lesson tomorrow?
- A. Two brushes, one bottle of ink and small sheets.
- B. One brush, one bottle of ink and small sheets.
- C. Two bottles of ink, one brush and small sheets.
- D. Two brushes, one bottle of ink and the big roll.

**Answer** A

## A13-05 水表读数对不上

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Jin, my water bill is forty yuan, but my neighbour's is only twenty.
Ms. Jin: Read the meter again. His number is one thousand two hundred.
Boy: Mine says one thousand. Should I tell someone?
Ms. Jin: Yes, ring the office on Monday and say both numbers.

**Question**
What should the boy do about the bill?
- A. Ring the office on Tuesday and say both numbers.
- B. Ring the office on Monday and say one number.
- C. Ring the office on Monday and say both numbers.
- D. Ring Ms. Jin on Monday and say both numbers.

**Answer** C

## A13-06 秋游改到下周

**Dialogue**

Ms. Qiao: Not now. The bus is being repaired, so we go next Tuesday.
Boy: Next Tuesday? Then we miss our geography lesson.
Ms. Qiao: Mr. Deng will move that lesson to Friday morning.

**Question**
Which day will the class go on the trip now?
- A. Thursday.
- B. Friday.
- C. Monday.
- D. Next Tuesday.

**Answer** D

## A13-07 牛奶盒投哪个桶

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wei, where do I put the banana skin?
Mr. Wei: In the green bin, not the blue one. The blue one is for paper.
Girl: And my empty milk carton?
Mr. Wei: That one goes to the yellow bin by the north gate.

**Question**
Where should the girl put her empty milk carton?
- A. In the yellow bin by the north gate.
- B. In the yellow bin by the south gate.
- C. In the blue bin for paper.
- D. In the green bin by the north gate.

**Answer** A

## A13-08 洗衣机滤网多久洗

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Luo, my washing machine leaves water in the door.
Ms. Luo: Clean the little filter under it every month.
Girl: Every month? My mother never does it.
Ms. Luo: Then write it on the fridge door and start now.

**Question**
How often should the girl clean the filter?
- A. Every day.
- B. Every month.
- C. Only when the door has water in it.
- D. Once a year.

## A13-10 班费交多少

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wu, class money is twenty yuan this term, isn't it?
Mr. Wu: Yes, but only twelve. The other eight comes from the book fund.
Girl: Twelve yuan to Ms. Tang, then.
Mr. Wu: Yes, and bring coins, not a note.

**Question**
How much must the girl bring this term?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Eight yuan.
- D. Thirty yuan.

**Answer** A

Ms. Fan: Red, not green. And I'll paint the big letters.

## A13-12 修路期间怎么上学

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gao, my home is far from school. Should I ride a bike?
Mr. Gao: Not yet. The road by the bridge is under repair.
Boy: How long will that last? Riding takes me five minutes.
Mr. Gao: Two weeks. Walk for now, and bring a raincoat on Monday.

**Question**
What will the boy do during the repair?
- A. Ride a bike on the road by the bridge.
- B. Ask his father to drive him.
- C. Walk to school and bring a raincoat on Monday.
- D. Stay at home until the repair is over.

**Answer** C

## A13-15 新衬衫怎么洗

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ren, my new shirt says "wash at thirty". May I use hot water?
Mr. Ren: No, thirty is the warmest it can be. Use cold water at home.
Boy: Then I shall wash it by hand, not in the machine.
Mr. Ren: Right, and dry it flat, not on a hanger.

**Question**
How should the boy wash his new shirt?
- A. By hand with cold water.
- B. In the machine with hot water.
- C. In the machine with cold water.
- D. By hand with water at thirty degrees.

**Answer** A
