> A27 日常健康与学习习惯（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A27-01 耳机音量只开一半

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, my ears hurt after I use my earphones.
Mr. Guo: Turn the volume down. Half of the maximum, not all of it.
Girl: How long may I use them a day?
Mr. Guo: One hour at a time. Take them out after that.

**Question**
What volume should the girl set?
- A. As loud as possible.
- B. A little below the maximum.
- C. The maximum.
- D. Half of the maximum.

**Answer** D

## A27-02 本子要对着鼻子放

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Ding, I have to twist my neck to see my notebook.
Ms. Ding: Turn the book so it points at your nose, not at your left hand.
Boy: What about the light?
Ms. Ding: It comes from the left, so your right hand does not cast a shadow.

**Question**
How should the boy place his notebook?
- A. To the left of his body.
- B. Straight in front of him, pointing at his nose.
- C. Far to the right on the desk.
- D. Flat against his chest.

**Answer** B

## A27-03 课桌高度到腰

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fu, my desk is too high for me.
Mr. Fu: Lower it until the top is level with your waist.
Girl: Then where do my knees go?
Mr. Fu: Under it, bent at right angles. Not against the desk edge.

**Question**
How high should the top of the desk be?
- A. Level with the girl's waist.
- B. Level with her shoulders.
- C. Level with her knees.
- D. Level with her chest.

**Answer** A

## A27-04 打喷嚏用手肘捂住

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Zong, I sneezed right in front of my friend.
Mr. Zong: Cover your mouth and nose with your elbow, not your hand.
Boy: Why not the hand?
Mr. Zong: The hand then touches the desk and the next person. The elbow does not.

**Question**
What should the boy use to cover a sneeze?
- A. His hand.
- B. His collar.
- C. His elbow.
- D. Nothing at all.

**Answer** C

## A27-05 跑完要小口喝水

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Kong, I drank a whole bottle after the race.
Ms. Kong: Take small sips instead. A big drink makes you dizzy.
Girl: Should I drink at all right away?
Ms. Kong: Wait five minutes. Then water, not sweet drinks.

**Question**
How should the girl drink after the race?
- A. A whole bottle at once.
- B. Small sips, five minutes after finishing.
- C. One big glass of sweet juice.
- D. Nothing at all for an hour.

**Answer** B

## A27-06 文具盒只留三支笔

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Xin, my pencil case is full of broken bits.
Mr. Xin: Empty it once a week. Keep only three pencils and one pen.
Boy: What about the ruler and the eraser?
Mr. Xin: The ruler stays, the eraser stays. Everything else goes out.

**Question**
What should the boy keep in his pencil case?
- A. Everything he has, including the broken bits.
- B. Only the ruler and the eraser.
- C. All his old pens and pencils.
- D. Three pencils and one pen, plus the ruler and eraser.

**Answer** D

## A27-07 水果一天吃两个

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Liang, how many fruits should I eat a day?
Mr. Liang: Two. One apple and one orange is enough.
Girl: Can I have a banana as well?
Mr. Liang: Then skip the orange, not the apple. Two in all.

**Question**
How many fruits should the girl eat in a day?
- A. One.
- B. Three.
- C. Two.
- D. Five.

**Answer** C

## A27-08 袜子要每天换一双

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Yun, my socks are always grey after one day.
Ms. Yun: Change them every day. Cotton socks, not nylon.
Girl: How many pairs do I need for a week?
Ms. Yun: Seven, one for each day. Wash them at forty degrees.

**Question**
How often should the girl change her socks?
- A. Every day.
- B. Every three days.
- C. Once a week.
- D. Only when they begin to smell.

**Answer** A

## A27-09 头发要早晚各梳一次

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, my hair gets tangled after two days.
Mr. Gu: Comb it every morning and every night, not once a week.
Boy: Should I wash it every day?
Mr. Gu: No, twice a week is enough. Combing matters more than washing.

**Question**
How often should the boy comb his hair?
- A. Once a week.
- B. Only when it is tangled.
- C. After every haircut.
- D. Every morning and every night.

**Answer** D

## A27-10 耳朵进水要侧头跳

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Pei, water went into my ear in the shower.
Mr. Pei: Turn your head to that side and jump, not the other way.
Girl: Should I put cotton in the ear?
Mr. Pei: No. Cotton pushes the water further in. Pull the ear up gently.

**Question**
What should the girl do to get the water out?
- A. Turn her head to that side and jump.
- B. Turn her head the other way and jump.
- C. Put cotton wool deep in the ear.
- D. Lie down with that ear on the pillow.

**Answer** A

## A27-11 流鼻血要低头

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yu, my nose is bleeding again.
Mr. Yu: Sit down and lean forward. Do not tilt your head back.
Boy: Can I put cold water on my forehead?
Mr. Yu: No. Hold the soft part of the nose for ten minutes, not the bridge.

**Question**
What should the boy do when his nose bleeds?
- A. Tilt his head back and lie down.
- B. Lean forward and hold the soft part of his nose.
- C. Press the bridge of his nose hard.
- D. Put cold water on his forehead.

**Answer** B

## A27-12 误吞东西要说出来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fei, I swallowed a small stone by mistake.
Mr. Fei: Tell an adult at once. Do not keep it a secret.
Girl: Will it hurt if I do not say anything?
Mr. Fei: It may. Hiding it is what makes it dangerous, not the stone.

**Question**
What should the girl do after swallowing the stone?
- A. Tell an adult at once.
- B. Keep it a secret until it hurts.
- C. Wait three days and see.
- D. Drink a lot of cold water and say nothing.

**Answer** A

## A27-13 走路不要看手机

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gao, I nearly fell because I was reading a message.
Mr. Gao: Put the phone away when you walk. Watch the steps, not the screen.
Boy: Can I look at it at the crossing?
Mr. Gao: Not there either. Stop at the kerb before you look at anything.

**Question**
When should the boy look at his phone?
- A. While he is walking to school.
- B. At the crossing, before the light changes.
- C. After he has stopped at the kerb.
- D. While he is climbing the stairs.

**Answer** C

## A27-14 躺着看书要坐起来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Wei, I read in bed every night because the lamp is there.
Mr. Wei: Sit at the desk for reading. In bed you read only ten minutes.
Girl: Is it the light or the position?
Mr. Wei: Both. The lamp belongs on the desk, not by your head.

**Question**
Where should the girl read?
- A. In bed, for as long as she likes.
- B. Lying on the sofa under a blanket.
- C. Standing at the window.
- D. At her desk, at the lamp.

**Answer** D

## A27-15 细嚼慢咽二十分钟才饱

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhu, I always feel hungry an hour after lunch.
Mr. Zhu: Chew each bite twenty times. You eat far too fast.
Girl: Does chewing really change how full I feel?
Mr. Zhu: Yes. The stomach tells the brain it is full after about twenty minutes.

**Question**
How long must the girl wait before she feels full?
- A. About one minute.
- B. About twenty minutes.
- C. About five minutes.
- D. Only after the second meal.

**Answer** B
