> A19 校园日常与办事（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A19-01 食堂没带饭卡

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Lu, I left my meal card at home. Can I pay with cash?
Ms. Lu: No, we can't take cash. Go to the office and ask for a temp card.
Boy: Will the office be open at lunch?
Ms. Lu: Yes, it is open from eleven to one. Pay me back this afternoon.

**Question**
How should the boy pay for his lunch today?
- A. Pay with cash at the door.
- B. Get a temp card from the office.
- C. Go home to fetch his meal card.
- D. Ask the office worker for money instead.

**Answer** B

## A19-02 图书馆的书要放回推车

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ren, I read a book from shelf 12. Where do I put it back?
Mr. Ren: Return it to the trolley, not shelf 12. The number on the spine is 12-B.
Girl: So 12-B goes on the trolley until the librarian checks it.
Mr. Ren: Right. Books on the trolley are counted on Friday.

**Question**
Where should the girl put the book back?
- A. On shelf 12.
- B. On the librarian's desk.
- C. In her own bag.
- D. On the trolley.

**Answer** D

## A19-03 照片留三张今天交

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Lu, how many photos do we bring tomorrow?
Ms. Lu: Four one-inch photos in all. One goes on the form, and three come today.
Boy: Does my mother have to bring them tomorrow?
Ms. Lu: No, she should send them today. The office closes at four.

**Question**
How many photos will the boy's mother send today?
- A. Three.
- B. One.
- C. Four.
- D. Two.

**Answer** A

## A19-04 旧校服交到班主任手里

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Guo, when do we get our new school clothes?
Mr. Guo: On Monday, after the last class. Not on Tuesday.
Girl: Do I bring the old ones back?
Mr. Guo: Yes, hand them to me in the hall, not to the office.

**Question**
What should the girl do with her old school clothes?
- A. Give them to the office on Tuesday.
- B. Take them home again.
- C. Hand them to Mr. Guo in the hall.
- D. Throw them in the bin.

**Answer** C

## A19-05 合唱比赛穿白衬衫黑裤子

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, what do we wear for the singing show?
Ms. Hu: A white shirt and black trousers. Not blue jeans.
Boy: My sister has a blue shirt at home.
Ms. Hu: Don't wear jeans. The show is on Friday morning in the hall.

**Question**
What will the boy wear for the singing show?
- A. Blue jeans and a white shirt.
- B. A white shirt and black trousers.
- C. A blue shirt and jeans.
- D. His sister's blue shirt and jeans.

**Answer** B

## A19-06 借教室要老师签字

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Qian, can our class use room 14 on Thursday afternoon?
Ms. Qian: Ask the office one day before. You cannot write on the form today.
Girl: Who has to sign the form?
Ms. Qian: Your head teacher, not the class monitor or me. The office only takes the paper.

**Question**
Who has to sign the form for room 14?
- A. The class monitor.
- B. Ms. Qian.
- C. A worker in the office.
- D. Her head teacher.

**Answer** D

## A19-07 下车要提前按铃

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hu, how do we get off the bus?
Mr. Hu: Press the bell before the stop. Not after the doors open.
Boy: What if the bus is full at the front?
Mr. Hu: Walk to the back door, not the middle. Stand near the door.

**Question**
When should the boy press the bell?
- A. After the doors open.
- B. After the bus stops.
- C. When the bus is full.
- D. Before the bus stops.

**Answer** D

## A19-08 鞋湿了塞报纸晾干

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. He, my shoes are wet after the rain.
Ms. He: Take them off and dry them first. Do not wear wet socks.
Girl: Can I dry them on the radiator?
Ms. He: No, not there. Put paper in them and leave them by the door.

**Question**
How should the girl dry her shoes?
- A. Put paper in them and leave them by the door.
- B. Dry them on the radiator.
- C. Wear them while they are wet.
- D. Leave them in the sun on the balcony.

**Answer** A

## A19-09 阅览室九点关门

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Niu, when do the lights go out in our building?
Ms. Niu: At ten. The gate is locked at ten fifteen.
Boy: Can I study in the reading room then?
Ms. Niu: No, only until nine. The reading room closes at nine.

**Question**
Until what time can the boy study in the reading room?
- A. Until ten.
- B. Until ten fifteen.
- C. Until nine.
- D. Until the gate is locked.

**Answer** C

## A19-10 图书馆不能吃东西

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zou, may I eat a sandwich in the library?
Mr. Zou: No. Food is not allowed here. Use the reading room upstairs.
Girl: Can I drink water at my desk?
Mr. Zou: A bottle with a cap is fine, but no juice boxes.

**Question**
What may the girl do at her desk in the library?
- A. Eat a sandwich.
- B. Drink water from a bottle with a cap.
- C. Drink a juice box.
- D. Open a bag of chips.

**Answer** B

## A19-11 袖子破了找秦老师缝

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ma, my sleeve is open at the seam.
Mr. Ma: Take it to Ms. Qian on the first floor. She mends clothes after four.
Boy: Is it free for students?
Mr. Ma: Yes, free. Bring the old uniform, not the new one.

**Question**
Who will mend the boy's sleeve?
- A. Ms. Qian on the first floor.
- B. Mr. Ma in his office.
- C. The class teacher on the third floor.
- D. His mother.

**Answer** A

## A19-12 超市排队站在队尾

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Wu, there is only one till open.
Ms. Wu: Wait in line. Go to the end of the line, not the front.
Girl: Can I use my phone and pay?
Ms. Wu: Not for this shop. Pay at the till, not online.

**Question**
Where should the girl wait to pay?
- A. At the front of the line.
- B. At the till with her phone.
- C. Outside the shop on her phone.
- D. At the end of the line.

**Answer** D

## A19-13 换零钱只能去银行

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ni, I need coins for the class sale.
Mr. Ni: The shop won't change a fifty. Go to the bank counter before four.
Boy: Can I change it at the school office?
Mr. Ni: No. Only the bank has coins for us. Come back by five.

**Question**
Where can the boy get coins?
- A. At the shop.
- B. At the school office.
- C. At the bank counter.
- D. In the classroom.

**Answer** C

## A19-14 买药要医生写处方

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Ren, can I buy these cough tablets here?
Ms. Ren: Not without a doctor's note. You need a prescription.
Girl: My father is a doctor. Will that do?
Ms. Ren: His work card is not enough. He must write the prescription, then she comes back at four.

**Question**
What must the girl's father do before she buys the tablets?
- A. Give the shop his work card.
- B. Write down the prescription.
- C. Come to the shop at four with her.
- D. Take the tablets home first.

**Answer** B

## A19-15 上课铃响老师没来

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Mu, the bell has rung and Ms. Bai is not here.
Mr. Mu: Wait five minutes. Then knock on the office door.
Girl: Shall we go back to our classroom?
Mr. Mu: Stay in the corridor, not in the classroom or the playground. Sit on the bench.

**Question**
What should the girls do first?
- A. Wait in the corridor for five minutes.
- B. Go back to the classroom and study.
- C. Knock on the office door at once.
- D. Sit on the bench in the playground.

**Answer** A
