## E10-01 社区与邻里
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你所在社区的邻里关系，说说大家平时怎样互相帮忙。
**参考作答**：I live in a small community in Suzhou, where most of the buildings are no more than six floors high. The neighbours on our floor often talk to each other on the stairs, and they know almost every family on the floor. Old Mrs. Xu at number 12 keeps a small garden, and she gives tomatoes to anyone who knocks at her door. When my mother was ill last winter, three different families brought soup to our flat in the same week. One of them was the young couple upstairs, who had just moved in and hardly knew anyone. In summer we often share cold drinks on the steps after dinner. Last year our building collected rubbish together, because a new recycling point was opened at the corner. I think a good community depends on small acts rather than on big plans, and ours has plenty of them.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：社区与楼层概况、邻里熟识的程度、门牌 12 号的徐太太在院里种小菜园并把番茄送给上门的人
- 信息完整性：母亲生病那周有三户人家送汤、刚搬来不久的新邻居也参与、夏天晚饭后在台阶上分冷饮、全楼一起垃圾分类、以小事而非大计划立社区的总体看法
- 发音准确性：community / neighbours / rubbish 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / because / I think 的承接层次
- 语法正确性：When 引导的时间状语从句；because 引导的原因状语从句

## E10-02 天气与季节
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你最喜欢的季节，说说那个季节给你带来了什么。
**参考作答**：My favourite season is autumn, because the weather in my city is neither too hot nor too cold in those months. The trees along my street turn yellow in late October, and the fallen leaves cover the pavement like a carpet. October and November are also the best months for walking, because the wind is cool and gentle. I usually take photographs of the same corner on every sunny weekend. In winter my hands hurt in the morning, and I have to wear three pairs of socks. Spring brings too many showers for me, since I often forget to carry an umbrella. Summer is the season I like least, because the rooms in my flat become hard to sleep in. Even so, the autumn holidays are when I feel happiest all year.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：最喜欢秋天及理由——气温不冷不热、秋天假期最开心；对比冬天要穿三双袜、春天阵雨多要带伞、夏天房间热得难睡
- 信息完整性：十月末树叶转黄、落叶铺满人行道、十月与十一月最适合散步、晴天周末给同一个街角拍照
- 发音准确性：autumn / neither / umbrella 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：because / since / Even so 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：neither ... nor 的并列结构；because 与 since 引导的原因状语从句

## E10-03 健康与作息
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你的日常作息，说说健康习惯是怎么形成的。
**参考作答**：I usually get up at half past six, and my first task is to drink a glass of warm water. I go to bed at about eleven, because I need enough sleep to keep my head clear during lessons. When the school day is over, I do the hard work first and leave the easy homework for later. On weekdays I eat breakfast at home, but at weekends I sometimes sleep late and skip it altogether. My mother used to nag me about exactly the same things for years, and I only started listening after I felt dizzy in class one afternoon. Now I do twenty jumping jacks before I sit down to study. I also try to stay away from the phone for the first hour after I wake up. Sleep, water and movement are the three things I would keep if I could change nothing else.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：六点半起床与第一件事、十一时左右就寝及其理由、先做难题留简单作业、工作日与周末的早餐差别
- 信息完整性：母亲多年唠叨与一次上课头晕后的转变、现在先做二十个开合跳、醒后第一小时不看手机、睡眠与水与运动三条总结
- 发音准确性：get up / dizzy / wake up 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / because / Now 的承接层次
- 语法正确性：used to + 动词原形表过去习惯；would + 动词原形表虚拟

## E10-04 金钱与消费
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你买东西时的习惯，说说家里的钱主要花在哪里。
**参考作答**：Before I buy anything, I always write it on a list, and I put the list in my pocket when I go out. I rarely buy the first thing I see in the shop window, because a decision made in five seconds is usually a bad one. When a pair of shoes is reduced by half, I ask myself whether I really need a second pair. My family's biggest expense is my school fees, which take a large part of my father's income every year. The second biggest is food, and my mother buys most of it at the market near the station. I keep most of my own pocket money in a box at home, and I have never used a card for a small purchase. My uncle once told me that the people who spend carefully are not mean, they are simply clear about what matters. I have started to keep a small record, and it turns out that I save more than I thought.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：买前先列清单并随身带、橱窗前不冲动、降价时问自己是否真需要第二双
- 信息完整性：学费是家里最大开支、食物是第二大开支、零钱存放在家且小笔支出不用卡、舅舅说花钱仔细不是吝啬而是清楚什么重要、记账后发现存得比想的多
- 发音准确性：reduced / pocket / expense 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / because / it turns out 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：whether 引导宾语从句；the people who 引导定语从句

## E10-05 宠物与动物
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你和动物相处的经历，说说动物给你的生活带来了什么。
**参考作答**：We had a dog when I was nine, and he was a thin brown one that my father found beside the road. He followed my bicycle to school every day for six years, and he was always waiting at the gate when I came back. He died in the autumn of my first year in middle school, and our street was strangely quiet for months afterwards. Since then we have kept a small bowl of food on the wall for the cats behind the buildings. They appear only after dark, and none of them has a name that we know. Last winter a cat with one ear stopped coming, and we could only guess where it had gone. I believe animals measure time better than we do, because they do not count the years at all. A street with animals in it feels different from one without them, and I cannot explain exactly how.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：九岁养的狗是父亲在路边捡到的棕色瘦狗、六年来每天跟车上学、后来固定在墙上给楼后的猫留食
- 信息完整性：猫只在天黑后出现、冬天断耳猫不再出现、狗去世后街道安静了几个月、动物对时间的感觉与人不同、有动物的街道感觉与没有的不同
- 发音准确性：brown / strangely / measure 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / because / I believe 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：since 引导的时间状语从句；a cat with one ear 中 with 复合结构

## E10-06 交通与出行
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你平时怎样上下学，说说现在的出行方式给你带来了什么。
**参考作答**：I go to school by bike, and the ride takes about fifteen minutes from my flat to the school gate. My father used to take me by car, but he stopped when I was in Grade Seven, because he said the streets are worse in the morning. There used to be a bus stop at the end of our road, and it was taken away two winters ago. Now I share the bike ride with my neighbour, whose children go to the same school. The new route takes a little longer, but the air on the way is much cleaner. In the rain we both wait under the awning of a shop until it eases. Traffic in our district is much lighter than it was five years ago, although the ring road is still slow at eight o'clock. My knees have never hurt, and I think that has more to do with the bicycle than with anything else.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：骑车上学约十五分钟、父亲七年级时停开车（理由是早上更堵）、两年前路末的公交站被撤、现在与邻居家的孩子同行
- 信息完整性：新路线稍长但空气更干净、雨天在店铺雨棚下等雨小、本区路况比五年前轻但环路八点仍慢、膝盖从未疼过
- 发音准确性：bicycle / traffic / awning 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：but / because / although 的让步与转折层次
- 语法正确性：used to + 动词原形表过去习惯；比较级 lighter than 与 worse in

## E10-07 穿着与风格
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你平时怎样挑选衣服，说说你在穿着上的原则。
**参考作答**：I own very few clothes, and most of them were bought in the same shop near my home. My sister calls my style plain, and she is not wrong about that. I do not follow fashion because the things I like rarely change from one year to the next. What matters to me is whether a piece of clothes lasts, not whether it looks special this week. My mother taught me to check the label before anything, and I have thrown away three jackets since she said it. Uniforms make the mornings easier, since nobody at school notices that two girls wore the same blue shirt. I once bought a white pair of shoes for a school trip, and they were ruined by the second afternoon. Now I keep one dark pair for everything, and I have stopped worrying about what other people are wearing.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：衣服不多且多在同一家店买、母亲教的看标签要求、为学校旅行买白鞋第二天下午就坏
- 信息完整性：不追潮流、看重耐穿而不看重这一周是否特别、校服省去早晨的烦恼、现在只留一双深色鞋、姐姐评其风格朴素
- 发音准确性：uniforms / label / ruined 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：because / since / Now 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：What matters ... is ... 的主语从句；whether 引导的宾语从句

## E10-08 音乐与艺术
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你接触过的艺术形式，说说你最喜欢的是什么。
**参考作答**：The first music I noticed was in my grandmother's house, where an old radio sat on a high shelf and was never switched off. I still remember a song that she hummed while she cooked, and I have never found out what it was called. My cousin taught me to play the erhu when I was twelve, and I was terrible at it for a full year. Watercolours came later, at a club that met on Friday afternoons, and they taught me to look at ordinary things more slowly. Painting has one advantage over music: a bad picture is still worth showing. Music has one advantage over painting: it can follow you along the street for free. Music is what I like most of the three, because it follows me down the street for free and it never asks for an exam result. My grandmother could not read the radio dial, and she never needed to.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：奶奶家高柜上的收音机与奶奶哼的那首歌、十二岁学二胡及一年的生疏、周五下午的水彩俱乐部
- 信息完整性：绘画与音乐各有的优势对照、在三者中明确选音乐及其理由——免费跟着走、不过问考试成绩、奶奶不会读收音机刻度也不需要
- 发音准确性：radio / erhu / watercolours 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：where / when / and 的叙述层次
- 语法正确性：what it was called 的宾语从句；because 引导的原因状语从句

## E10-09 科技与手机
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈手机在你生活里的位置，说说它带来方便的同时带来了什么。
**参考作答**：My phone is the first thing I touch when I wake up, and the last thing I look at before I sleep, which my mother does not enjoy hearing. It replaces almost everything else in my bag: a dictionary, a map, a notebook, a ticket and a wallet. When I travel, the paper tickets it used to be are the only thing I still carry. I know where the bus goes without waiting for a sign, and I know the way home in three towns I have never studied. The cost is harder to measure, because I do not lose an hour a day deliberately; it simply disappears. Last term I stopped taking it into the dormitory on Sunday nights, and I read two books that I would otherwise have scrolled past. My phone has never once let me get lost, and that is not a small thing. I would not give it up, but I would like to be a person who can put it face down for an hour.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：醒时第一件、睡前最后一件、包里其他物品被它取代、旅行时只保留纸质票
- 信息完整性：不用等站牌就知道车去哪、在三个没学过的城镇认得回家的路、代价难以衡量、周日不带手机读了两本书、希望能整小时把手机屏幕朝下放
- 发音准确性：dictionary / measure / deliberately 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / because / but 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：which 引导非限定定语从句；would have + 过去分词 作定语表过去未实现

## E10-10 规则与公共秩序
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈公共场合需要守的规矩，说说规矩为什么必要。
**参考作答**：On the bus, everybody queues even when there are only three people waiting, and nobody pushes, which would be unthinkable in some other cities. In the library we keep our voices low enough that the person next to us can hear themselves think. When someone drops something, the rule is simple: you stop, you pick it up, and you carry it back to them yourself. Most public rules were written by somebody who had a problem once, and I have found out that this is almost always true. My own school banned phones from the dining hall last year, and the first week was full of complaints. By the second week the hall was so quiet that we could hear the clock. Somebody has to decide these things, and it is easier to decide them once than to argue about them every day. A rule is not there to stop you from doing anything; it is there so that the people around you are not paying for your convenience.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：公交车即使只有三人也排队且不挤、图书馆把声音压到旁边人能听见自己思考、捡东西要停下、捡起并亲自送回
- 信息完整性：公共规矩多是吃过亏的人写的、本校去年禁食堂手机第一周抱怨第二周安静得能听见钟声、规矩不为阻止你而为让周围人不替你付代价
- 发音准确性：queues / rules / convenience 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / when / so that 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：enough that 引导结果状语从句；so that 引导目的状语从句

## E10-11 友谊与合作
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈和朋友合作做的事情，说说合作教会了你什么。
**参考作答**：Our class spent a whole term building a model bridge out of paper, and the four of us divided the work in a way that nobody had planned. I drew the shape, my friend cut the pieces, and two others held everything together with glue they were not supposed to use. On the last afternoon the bridge held more weight than the teacher had expected, and we all knew exactly which part of it would fail first. What I learned was less about bridges than about patience. When a plan is agreed quickly, somebody's idea disappears quietly, and you only notice when the work is finished. My friend and I still argue about small things, and we have never once walked away from a job half done. We still meet the four of us once a year, and we rebuild something small each time. Cooperation is slower than doing something alone, and afterwards it belongs to more than one person.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：整学期用纸造桥、四人分工、用了不许用的胶水、最后一下午的承重与对薄弱处的预判
- 信息完整性：学到的主要是耐心而不是造桥、快速定计划会悄悄抹掉某个人的想法、仍为小事争吵但从不半途而废、合作更慢且成果归属多人
- 发音准确性：cooperation / patience / glue 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：less ... than / when / and 的推进层次
- 语法正确性：less about ... than about ... 的比较结构；when 引导的时间状语从句

## E10-12 失败与挫折
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈一次你没有做成的事情，说说你从那次结果里得到了什么。
**参考作答**：Last year I entered an English speech competition, and I finished fourth out of eleven, which was not the result I had repeated in my head for weeks. I had memorised my speech word for word, and when the lights went on I forgot the second paragraph entirely. Afterwards three classmates told me it had been a good speech, and I did not believe any of them. What helped was writing it down afterwards, line by line, and seeing that my pauses were the problem rather than my English. This term I entered again, and I used short notes instead of a full script. I came third, and the difference between the two years was about six pages of paper. I showed that speech to my cousin afterwards, and she said it was better than I thought. Failing once is cheap if you read the result properly, and expensive only if you decide it was about your worth.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：去年英语演讲比赛第四名且与自己反复设想的不同、逐字背诵上台忘第二段、三位同学说好但自己不信
- 信息完整性：本学期改用短提纲重赛得第三名、两次相差约六页纸、事后写下来发现问题在停顿而不在英语、结果贵不贵取决于是否当成对自我价值的判决
- 发音准确性：competition / entirely / line 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：and / which 的叙述层次
- 语法正确性：which 引导非限定定语从句；rather than 的对比

## E10-13 未来与变化
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，谈谈你希望将来的生活有什么变化，说说你打算为此做什么。
**参考作答**：Ten years from now I hope to live in a smaller flat than this one, but not far from where my parents will be living. I would like to work in a place where I can see the sky from my desk, and I am aware that this narrows my choices. The change I care about most has nothing to do with money, although money makes it easier. I want to be able to switch off my phone at the end of a working day and not feel that something is being missed. My father did that for years, and he has never once told me it was easy. The first step is small: I am learning to cook, because feeding myself is a skill nobody can take away when the price of a restaurant goes up. My mother thinks the plan is reasonable, which is more than I expected. Plans like this are easy to write down and hard to keep, so I have told my sister to ask me about it every month.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：十年后想住比现在小但离父母不远的房子、工作能从桌面看见天空且知道这样会收窄选择、下班时能关机
- 信息完整性：最在意的改变与钱无关但钱会让它容易、父亲做了多年却从没说容易、第一步小——学做饭这项技能没人能拿走、计划写下来容易而坚持难、请姐姐每月问一次进度
- 发音准确性：restaurant / narrows / choices 的音节与重音
- 表达流利度：but / although / so 的让步与推进
- 语法正确性：I would like + 动词原形；smaller ... than 的比较级
