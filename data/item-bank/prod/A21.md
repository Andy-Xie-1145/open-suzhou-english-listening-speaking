> A21 校园手续与生活算账（15 题）

> 全部材料为仿写素材，非真题；本型（一个对话配一道四选一题）无官方试题结构依据，故在文件头注明。

## A21-01 打印一页两毛钱

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Long, I want to print my report.
Mr. Long: Twenty cents a page. For twelve pages that is two yuan forty.
Boy: Can I print it in colour?
Mr. Long: No, black and white only. Colour is two yuan a page.

**Question**
How much will the boy pay for twelve black-and-white pages?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Two yuan.
- C. Two yuan forty.
- D. One yuan twenty.

**Answer** C

## A21-02 放大照片三天取

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, can you enlarge these photos today?
Mr. Yu: No. It takes three days. Come on Friday, not on Thursday.
Girl: How much is one big print?
Mr. Yu: Six yuan each. The small ones are two yuan.

**Question**
When should the girl come to collect the big prints?
- A. On Thursday.
- B. On Friday.
- C. On Saturday.
- D. Today.

**Answer** B

## A21-03 打车八公里二十元

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hao, how much is the taxi from here to the station?
Mr. Hao: Ten yuan for the first three kilometres, then two yuan a kilometre.
Girl: It's about eight kilometres, isn't it?
Mr. Hao: Yes. So ten plus five times two, twenty yuan.

**Question**
How much will the girl pay for the taxi?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Sixteen yuan.
- C. Twenty-six yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.

**Answer** D

## A21-04 话费要去营业厅充

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Xia, I have no money on my phone.
Mr. Xia: The school shop can't do that. Go to the service hall.
Girl: Can I pay by card at the hall?
Mr. Xia: Yes, or in cash. The hall closes at six.

**Question**
Where can the girl top up her phone?
- A. At the service hall.
- B. At the post office.
- C. At the library.
- D. At the school shop.

**Answer** A

## A21-05 加油要加五十升

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gu, my tank holds fifty litres and it is empty now.
Mr. Gu: Then fill it up. Fifty litres, not thirty.
Boy: Is it seven yuan a litre?
Mr. Gu: Seven yuan twenty. Fifty litres is three hundred sixty yuan.

**Question**
How much will the boy pay for the fuel?
- A. Two hundred ten yuan.
- B. Two hundred sixteen yuan.
- C. Three hundred sixty yuan.
- D. Three hundred fifty yuan.

**Answer** C

## A21-06 洗车要等一小时

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Yu, how long do I have to wait for the car?
Mr. Yu: About an hour. The queue has six cars in it.
Girl: Can I wait in the car park?
Mr. Yu: Wait in the coffee shop, not in the car park.

**Question**
Where should the girl wait?
- A. In the car park.
- B. In the coffee shop.
- C. At the gate.
- D. At the bus stop.

**Answer** B

## A21-07 课间操要做五分钟

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qi, how long is the break-time exercise?
Ms. Qi: Five minutes, not ten. We do it on the ground.
Boy: What music do we use?
Ms. Qi: The bell rings when we start, not when we stop.

**Question**
How long will the boy do the break-time exercise?
- A. Five minutes.
- B. Three minutes.
- C. Half an hour.
- D. Ten minutes.

**Answer** A

## A21-08 卫生评比由班长记分

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ren, who writes down the scores for the clean-class check?
Mr. Ren: The class monitor, not the cleaning boy.
Girl: What happens if we lose three points?
Mr. Ren: We clean again on Friday. Nobody gets punished.

**Question**
Who writes down the scores?
- A. The cleaning boy.
- B. Mr. Ren.
- C. The head teacher.
- D. The class monitor.

**Answer** D

## A21-09 社团报名周五截止

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Xiao, can I still join the chess club?
Ms. Xiao: Yes, but the form must reach me by Friday at four.
Boy: Is Monday too late?
Ms. Xiao: Too late. The list goes to the office on Friday evening.

**Question**
When is the last day to join the club?
- A. Monday.
- B. Friday.
- C. Tuesday.
- D. Saturday.

**Answer** B

## A21-10 学费周一交

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fan, when is the school fee due?
Mr. Fan: On Monday. The office takes money from eight to eleven.
Girl: My mother comes on Sunday. Is that all right?
Mr. Fan: No, she must come on Monday. Sunday the office is shut.

**Question**
When must the girl's mother come?
- A. On Sunday.
- B. On Saturday.
- C. On Monday.
- D. On Tuesday.

**Answer** C

## A21-11 放学要排队出校门

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Han, can I run out of the gate when the bell rings?
Mr. Han: No. Line up by class, then walk out quietly.
Boy: Can my sister wait at the gate for me?
Mr. Han: She must wait outside the rail, not inside the gate.

**Question**
Where should the boy's sister wait?
- A. Inside the school gate.
- B. At the classroom door.
- C. On the road.
- D. Outside the rail.

**Answer** D

## A21-12 宿舍洗澡要等多久

**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Qu, how long is the wait for the shower?
Ms. Qu: Fifteen minutes at eight. At ten there is no queue.
Girl: Should I bring my own towel?
Ms. Qu: Yes. The house ones are for the sports class only.

**Question**
How long will the girl wait if she showers at ten?
- A. No waiting at all.
- B. Ten minutes.
- C. Half an hour.
- D. Fifteen minutes.

**Answer** A

## A21-13 冬窗上会结霜

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Ji, my window is white in the morning.
Mr. Ji: That is frost on the inside. Wipe it after the room gets warm.
Boy: Can I use hot water straight away?
Mr. Ji: No. The glass will break. Use warm water, not hot.

**Question**
What should the boy wipe the frost with?
- A. Hot water.
- B. Cold water.
- C. Warm water.
- D. Vinegar.

**Answer** C

## A21-14 拔河比赛怎么站位

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Deng, how shall our class stand for the tug of war?
Mr. Deng: Put the strongest boy at the back and the lightest at the front.
Girl: Do we all hold the rope at the same time?
Mr. Deng: Yes, and pull together on the word, not before it.

**Question**
Who should stand at the back of the team?
- A. The lightest person.
- B. The strongest boy.
- C. The shortest person.
- D. The team leader.

**Answer** B

## A21-15 借来的书要夹书签

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zou, I can't finish this book before Monday.
Mr. Zou: Put a paper bookmark in it. Don't fold the page or write on it.
Girl: What if the next reader has no paper?
Mr. Zou: He uses his own. Never tear the corner off the page.

**Question**
What should the girl do before she puts the book back?
- A. Write her name on the cover.
- B. Fold the last page.
- C. Tear the corner off the page.
- D. Put a paper bookmark in it.

**Answer** D
