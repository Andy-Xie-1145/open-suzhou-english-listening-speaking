> A32 居家细节与办事（15 题）
> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本批对话原创编写，未取自任何正式试题。

## A32-01 订牛奶的配送时间
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Dai, what time do you deliver the milk?
Ms. Dai: Before seven every morning. I leave the bottles in the box outside the door.
Boy: We are out before seven on weekdays.
Ms. Dai: Then move the box to the side gate. I can leave it there until eight.
**Question**
What does Ms. Dai suggest the boy do?
- A. Move the box to the side gate.
- B. Come to her shop at seven.
- C. Wait for her at the door.
- D. Leave the box where it is.
**Answer** A

## A32-02 在食堂打饭的份量
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. He, how much rice do I get?
Ms. He: One ladle for a small bowl, two for a large one.
Boy: I am having the fish today.
Ms. He: Then start with the vegetables, then the fish, then the rice.
Boy: Can I take the fish first?
Ms. He: No. The vegetables go first.
**Question**
Which does Ms. He tell the boy to take first?
- A. The rice.
- B. The fish.
- C. The vegetables.
- D. The soup.
**Answer** C

## A32-03 给花盆垫排水片
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Kong, my potted plant keeps having water in the bottom.
Mr. Kong: Put a stone or a broken tile over the hole. One tile is enough.
Girl: I have no stones, but I have an old saucer.
Mr. Kong: A saucer holds the water, so no.
**Question**
How many tiles does the girl need for her small pot?
- A. Two.
- B. Three.
- C. Six.
- D. One.
**Answer** D

## A32-04 面包店买多少个吐司
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Cui, how many loaves do two people need?
Ms. Cui: One a day between you.
Boy: That is thirty a month.
Ms. Cui: Twenty-eight, because a month is four weeks.
Boy: So I buy four at a time.
Ms. Cui: Four loaves keeps for three days, not five.
**Question**
About how many loaves does the pair need in a month?
- A. About twenty-eight loaves.
- B. About thirty loaves.
- C. About twenty loaves.
- D. About thirty-two loaves.
**Answer** A

## A32-05 旧书的折价
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Cui, how much will you give me for these three books?
Mr. Cui: A new book is thirty yuan. A second-hand one is a third of that.
Girl: And this one has no cover.
Mr. Cui: Nothing for that one. Ten yuan for each of the other two.
Girl: Then I get twenty yuan.
Mr. Cui: Twenty yuan.
**Question**
How much will the girl get in all?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Thirty yuan.
- C. Twenty yuan.
- D. Zero yuan.
**Answer** C

## A32-06 餐馆点几个菜
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Xie, can we order without seeing the menu?
Mr. Xie: Yes. But say the number of people first. Four people cannot share three dishes.
Girl: We are four. How many dishes then?
Mr. Xie: Four dishes and a soup. Rice is included.
Girl: And extra rice?
Mr. Xie: Extra rice is not free.
**Question**
How many dishes does Mr. Xie suggest?
- A. Three dishes.
- B. Four dishes and a soup.
- C. Five dishes.
- D. Six dishes.
**Answer** B

## A32-07 相框挂墙的高度
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wan, how high should I hang the photo frame?
Ms. Wan: Its centre at the height of your eye. Not its top.
Boy: My eye is one metre fifty.
Ms. Wan: Then the centre is at one metre fifty, and the frame hangs down from there.
**Question**
At what height will the centre of the frame be?
- A. At one metre fifty.
- B. At one metre forty.
- C. At one metre sixty.
- D. At one metre thirty.
**Answer** A

## A32-08 早上设几个闹钟
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qin, I set my alarm for six, but I get up at six twenty.
Ms. Qin: Then set three alarms, five minutes apart.
Boy: Six, six five and six ten?
Ms. Qin: That is right. The last one is the one that works.
Boy: Mine is one alarm at six.
Ms. Qin: Then you are lucky.
**Question**
How many alarms does Ms. Qin suggest the boy set?
- A. One.
- B. Three.
- C. Two.
- D. Six.
**Answer** B

## A32-09 手机充电线的长度
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Liang, which cable do I need for my phone?
Mr. Liang: The white one is one metre. The black one is two metres.
Boy: My desk is one metre from the wall socket.
Mr. Liang: Then take the white one. The black one will be in the way.
**Question**
Which cable does Mr. Liang suggest for the boy?
- A. The black one.
- B. Both of them.
- C. Neither of them.
- D. The white one.
**Answer** D

## A32-10 在展馆看画的时长
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qin, how long do people usually spend in the gallery?
Ms. Qin: About forty minutes. Most of them see ten pictures.
Boy: I want to see every one.
Ms. Qin: There are sixty. Then plan for four hours, not forty minutes.
Boy: Is there a rule about talking?
Ms. Qin: No talking in the first room.
**Question**
How long does Ms. Qin suggest the boy plan for?
- A. About forty minutes.
- B. About four hours.
- C. About one hour.
- D. About six hours.
**Answer** B

## A32-11 楼层的电费怎么分摊
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Qi, the electricity bill for our floor is ninety yuan.
Ms. Qi: Six flats, so fifteen each.
Boy: The top flat was empty for two weeks.
Ms. Qi: Then it pays nothing, and the five of you pay eighteen each.
**Question**
How much does each of the five flats pay?
- A. Fifteen yuan.
- B. Ninety yuan.
- C. Six yuan.
- D. Eighteen yuan.
**Answer** D

## A32-12 书包里书怎么放
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Wu, how do you keep your books upright in a bag?
Ms. Wu: Biggest at the bottom. Everything else leans on it.
Girl: But my bag is too full to stand them up.
Ms. Wu: Then take out the two you never open and carry them.
**Question**
Which book should the girl keep in the bag?
- A. A book she never opens.
- B. The biggest book.
- C. Two books she needs.
- D. The maths book.
**Answer** B

## A32-13 遥控器电池怎么试
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Fu, how long does a remote control battery last?
Mr. Fu: About half a year.
Boy: Mine died last month.
Mr. Fu: Then you leave it on, or the buttons wear.
Boy: Can I test it now?
Mr. Fu: In a light, not in the sun. The meter is on the back.
**Question**
How does Mr. Fu say to test the battery?
- A. In a light.
- B. In the sun.
- C. In water.
- D. On the desk.
**Answer** A

## A32-14 给盆栽浇多少水
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Pei, should I use tap water for my plants?
Ms. Pei: Let it stand overnight, or use yesterday's.
Girl: Why?
Ms. Pei: Hot water from the tap hurts the roots.
Girl: How much for one pot?
Ms. Pei: Two hundred millilitres is enough. More is how people kill their plants.
**Question**
How much water does one pot need?
- A. One hundred millilitres.
- B. Two hundred millilitres.
- C. Half a litre.
- D. A full jug.
**Answer** B

## A32-15 把旧课本送给低年级
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Cui, I have finished Grade Nine. May I give my books away?
Ms. Cui: Yes, and Grade Eight students are here next week.
Boy: My little brother is in Grade Six.
Ms. Cui: He may have two of them, not ten. Maths goes first. Nobody keeps a book they cannot read.
**Question**
Which books does Ms. Cui say should go first?
- A. The science books.
- B. The oldest books.
- C. The maths books.
- D. Ten of them.
**Answer** C
