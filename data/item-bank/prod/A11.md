## A11-01 空调设成二十六度
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Fang, our classroom is too cold. The air conditioner is on.
Ms. Fang: Set it at twenty-six, not at twenty-two.
Girl: Twenty-six. Should we turn it off when we leave?
Ms. Fang: Turn it off at ten, not at nine. Our class ends at nine.
Girl: Twenty-six, and off at ten. All right.
**Question**
At what temperature will the girl set the air conditioner?
- A. At twenty-two degrees.
- B. At twenty-four degrees.
- C. At twenty-six degrees.
- D. At twenty-eight degrees.
**Answer** C

## A11-02 口袋里的纸票和钥匙
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Hu, I'm going to wash my school clothes.
Mr. Hu: Turn out your pockets first.
Boy: Why? There is only a little money in my pocket.
Mr. Hu: There is a paper ticket and a key in the left pocket. Check it, not just the money.
Boy: Then I'll look in both pockets before washing.
**Question**
What is in the left pocket of the boy's school clothes?
- A. A paper ticket and a key.
- B. A pen and a ruler.
- C. A school card.
- D. A lot of money.
**Answer** A

## A11-03 微波炉不用金属盘
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Ren, I want to heat my milk in the microwave.
Mr. Ren: Use a glass bowl, not a metal plate.
Girl: This spoon is metal too. May I put it in?
Mr. Ren: Take the spoon out. Put the bowl in for two minutes, not three.
Girl: A glass bowl for two minutes, with no spoon.
**Question**
What will the girl put in the microwave?
- A. A metal plate for three minutes.
- B. A metal plate for two minutes.
- C. A glass bowl with a metal spoon for two minutes.
- D. A glass bowl without a spoon for two minutes.
**Answer** D

## A11-04 出门前关掉家里的灯
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, our flat is dark. I left for school in a hurry.
Ms. Hu: Which lights are still on?
Boy: The big light in the hall and my desk light.
Ms. Hu: Two lights for eight hours. Turn both off when you leave. Not one of them.
Boy: All the lights next time. I see.
**Question**
How many lights will the boy turn off next time?
- A. One.
- B. Two.
- C. Three.
- D. Eight.
**Answer** B

## A11-05 冰箱里不要塞满
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Tao, I put all our food in the fridge yesterday.
Mr. Tao: Did you fill every shelf?
Girl: Yes, even the door. The motor is very loud.
Mr. Tao: Take out two bottles. Cold air must move.
Girl: How much space should I leave on the shelves?
Mr. Tao: A little, not a full load.
**Question**
What should the girl do to the fridge?
- A. Turn off the motor.
- B. Take two bottles out.
- C. Buy a bigger fridge.
- D. Fill every shelf again.
**Answer** B

## A11-06 洗衣粉只放一勺
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. An, how much washing powder shall I put in?
Mr. An: One spoon for a full load, not two.
Boy: My mother always puts in two spoons.
Mr. An: One is enough. Too much powder leaves marks on the clothes.
Boy: One spoon, then I press the button.
**Question**
How much washing powder will the boy put in?
- A. One spoon for half a load.
- B. Two spoons for a full load.
- C. Two spoons for half a load.
- D. One spoon for a full load.
**Answer** D

## A11-07 插线板最多接两样
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Deng, I want three things on one power strip.
Ms. Deng: How many? A rice cooker, a kettle and a lamp?
Girl: Yes, all at the same time.
Ms. Deng: Two at most, not three. The lamp needs another wall socket.
Girl: Two here, and the lamp goes over there.
**Question**
Where will the lamp be plugged in?
- A. In another wall socket.
- B. In the same power strip.
- C. In a new power strip.
- D. It will not be plugged in.
**Answer** A

## A11-08 洗澡只冲十分钟
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Gao, the shower water is too hot for me today.
Mr. Gao: Turn the tap to the left, not to the right.
Boy: How long shall I stay in?
Mr. Gao: Ten minutes, not twenty. Turn off the tap while you wash your hair.
Boy: Ten minutes, and the tap off for my hair.
**Question**
How long will the boy stay in the shower?
- A. Five minutes.
- B. Twenty minutes.
- C. Ten minutes.
- D. Fifteen minutes.
**Answer** C

## A11-09 洗手时先关水再冲
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Jin, I leave the tap on when I wash my hands.
Ms. Jin: How long does it run then?
Girl: Far too long. My two brothers wash after me.
Ms. Jin: Close the tap while you rub. Open it again to rinse.
Girl: Tap closed while I rub, then open it to rinse.
**Question**
When should the girl close the tap?
- A. While she rubs her hands with soap.
- B. While she rinses her hands.
- C. After she dries her hands.
- D. Only when her brothers wash after her.
**Answer** A

## A11-10 洗菜水拿去浇花
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Yu, I throw the water away after I wash the vegetables.
Mr. Yu: How much water do you use each time?
Boy: About two bowls. I wash the vegetables every day.
Mr. Yu: Keep it in a bucket for the flowers on your balcony.
Boy: Two bowls in the bucket, and I water the plants after dinner.
**Question**
What will the boy do with the water after he washes the vegetables?
- A. Throw it into the sink.
- B. Use it for the flowers on his balcony.
- C. Give it to his neighbours.
- D. Keep it in the fridge.
**Answer** B

## A11-11 看完电视拔掉插头
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Bai, our television is on all evening, even with no picture.
Mr. Bai: That red light means the set is still working.
Girl: We watch until eleven. Then we just put the covers on.
Mr. Bai: Pull out the plug after eleven. Not a new TV.
Girl: The plug goes out at eleven then. Good.
**Question**
What will the girl do after eleven o'clock?
- A. Pull out the plug.
- B. Put the covers on the set.
- C. Buy a new television.
- D. Watch the television until midnight.
**Answer** A

## A11-12 天热先开电风扇
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Mo, it is thirty-five degrees today. Shall we open the air conditioner?
Ms. Mo: Not yet. Open the window and run the fan for an hour.
Boy: Then shall I turn the air conditioner on at two?
Ms. Mo: Yes, at two, and close the windows again.
Boy: Wind first, then the air conditioner at two.
**Question**
When will the boy turn the air conditioner on?
- A. At one o'clock.
- B. Right away.
- C. At two o'clock.
- D. At three o'clock.
**Answer** C

## A11-13 水费单多了四吨
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Luo, our water bill went up. It is four tons more than June.
Mr. Luo: Where did the extra water go?
Girl: We washed the car at home twice, and the tap was on.
Mr. Luo: Each wash takes two tons. Use the car wash, not the yard.
Girl: Then we wash it there next time.
**Question**
Why was the water bill higher than June's?
- A. They drank more water in the hot weather.
- B. A garden tap was broken.
- C. They used more water in the bathroom.
- D. They washed the car at home twice.
**Answer** D

## A11-14 先甩干再拿到阳台晾
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wei, my clothes are wet for a long time on the balcony.
Ms. Wei: Did you press the spin button before you hung them?
Boy: No. I just hung them straight from the machine.
Ms. Wei: Spin for three minutes. Then the balcony needs one hour, not four.
Boy: Spin first, and the balcony needs less time.
**Question**
How long will the clothes dry on the balcony?
- A. One hour.
- B. Three minutes.
- C. Two hours.
- D. Four hours.
**Answer** A

## A11-15 电熨斗别一直放在板上

**Question**
How long may the iron stay on the board?

**Answer** C
