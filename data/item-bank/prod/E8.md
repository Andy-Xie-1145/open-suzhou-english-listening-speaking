## E8-01 个人信息与爱好
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你的个人信息与一项爱好，说清楚它对你意味着什么。
**参考作答**：My name is Li Wei and I am fifteen years old. I study in a middle school in Suzhou, and I am in Grade Nine this year. I am not very tall, but I am good at running. My favourite subject is history, because our teacher tells stories about real people. I play the guitar after dinner almost every evening, and I practise for about half an hour. I also like drawing, although I am not good at it yet. Last year I won a small prize in a school drawing competition. In my free time I help my grandmother water her flowers on the balcony. I think hobbies are important because they make me relaxed after a long day at school.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：姓名年龄、学校年级、身体特点、学科偏好、爱好与坚持方式、另一项爱好、一次成绩、家务参与
- 信息完整性：应覆盖 5 个以上信息点，说满 7 句后仍可补充细节
- 发音准确性：fifteen / hobbies 的音节数；guitar / drawing 的浊音与词尾 -ing
- 表达流利度：because / although / I think 的连接，避免短句堆砌
- 语法正确性：because 与 although 引导的从句主谓一致

## E8-02 校园生活
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你的校园生活，说说你最喜欢其中哪一部分。
**参考作答**：Our school is not very big, but it has a new library and a big playground. Classes begin at eight in the morning and finish at half past four in the afternoon. We have six lessons a day, and the longest break is the one after lunch. My favourite teacher is our maths teacher, because she never hurries us when we make a mistake. Our school has three clubs, and I am in the reading club. We meet every Friday afternoon and talk about one book together. Sometimes we also play basketball on the playground after school. Next term I hope to take part in the school sports meeting. I think school life is busy, but it is also the best time of my life.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：校园设施、作息、课程量、最喜欢的老师与理由、社团与频率
- 信息完整性：课外运动、下学期计划、总体感受
- 发音准确性：playground / maths / afternoon 的重音位置
- 表达流利度：but / and / also 的承接层次
- 语法正确性：not very big 的否定；have / finish / meet 的第三人称单数

## E8-03 家庭
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你的家庭成员，说说你和家人怎样相处。
**参考作答**：I live with my parents and my younger sister in a flat near the city centre. My father is a driver and he often works on weekends, so we usually have dinner together only on weekdays. My mother is a nurse and she works in a hospital, which means she is often tired in the evening. My sister is only nine, so she likes playing with her toys more than studying. On Sundays we often visit my grandparents in a small town near Suzhou. My grandmother always cooks us fish, and my grandfather likes telling stories about his childhood. I help them clean the house every time we visit. I love my family very much, and I hope we can have a longer holiday together next year.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：家庭构成、父母职业与作息、妹妹特点、周日安排、祖辈习惯
- 信息完整性：自己的参与方式、明年愿望
- 发音准确性：nurse / driver / flat 的元音区别；grandmother 为三个音节（grand-moth-er）
- 表达流利度：so / which means / and 的递进层次
- 语法正确性：which means 说明结构；only 的位置

## E8-04 旅行
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍一次你印象深刻的旅行，说说它给你留下了什么。
**参考作答**：Last summer I went to Hangzhou with my parents, and we stayed there for five days. We took a high-speed train, and the journey took about two hours. On the first day we walked around West Lake, and the weather was fine. The next day we took a boat on the lake and saw many small islands. I bought a silk scarf for my aunt as a present. We also visited a tea plantation in the afternoon of that day. I liked the trip because it was the first time I had travelled by train. Next year I hope to go to Xi'an, because I want to see the Terracotta Army. I will certainly take my camera and a notebook with me.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：时间与同行者、停留天数、交通方式与时长、首日行程、次日活动
- 信息完整性：购买的礼物、另一处参观点、喜欢的理由、下年计划
- 发音准确性：Hangzhou / Xi'an / silk 的辅音与音节
- 表达流利度：because 与 next year 的先后使用
- 语法正确性：过去时叙述一致；I had travelled 为过去完成时

## E8-05 运动
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你参加的一项运动，说说它带给你的变化。
**参考作答**：I have played badminton for about three years, and I usually play it with my classmates after school. We go to the sports hall twice a week, and each time we play for about an hour. Badminton is not as easy as it looks, and I still make mistakes with my footwork. Running is my second sport, and I run in the park on Sunday mornings. I am not very fast, but I can keep going for a long time. Our school sports meeting is in October, and I have signed up for the eight-hundred-metre race. Last year I came third, so I hope to do better this year. I believe doing sports is good for both my body and my mind.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：项目与年限、频率与时长、难点所在、第二项运动、目标赛事与以往成绩
- 信息完整性：今年期望、运动的益处
- 发音准确性：badminton / metre / footwork 的重音与连读
- 表达流利度：and 与 so 的承接、but 的转折
- 语法正确性：现在完成时 have played 与一般现在时 I run 的区分

## E8-06 饮食
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你们家的饮食习惯，说说你对健康饮食的看法。
**参考作答**：Our family does not eat much meat, so we have vegetables almost every day. For breakfast I usually have an egg, some bread and a glass of milk. My grandmother lives in the south, and she often cooks rice soup for us when we visit her. I am not fond of hot food, but I have learned to eat it because it is good for the body. Fast food is popular among students, but my parents tell me not to eat it too often. There is a small vegetable market near my home, and we go there twice a week. My mother often says that children need three bowls of vegetables a day, and I am trying to follow that. I think a healthy diet is much more important than an expensive one.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：家庭取向、早餐内容、祖辈与粥、个人偏好与转变、快食与限制、买菜地点、母亲的每日蔬菜建议
- 信息完整性：健康饮食的标准这一收尾观点
- 发音准确性：vegetables / breakfast 的音节与重音；rice 与 milk 的单音节对比
- 表达流利度：两处 but 的转折语气要有区别
- 语法正确性：am not fond of 的否定形式；tell me not to eat it 的不定式否定

## E8-07 节日
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍一个你最喜欢的节日，说说你们家怎样庆祝。
**参考作答**：The Spring Festival is the most important festival in China, and my family always celebrates it together. Before the festival we clean the house and put up red couplets on the door. On New Year's Eve we have a big dinner, and my grandmother makes dumplings. After dinner we watch a programme on television until midnight. Last year my cousin gave me a red envelope, and I saved most of the money for a book. I also like the Mid-Autumn Festival, because we eat mooncakes and sit outside together. This year I hope to learn to make mooncakes with my grandmother. Festivals help our family stay close, and they bring good food as well.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：节日重要性、年前准备、除夕晚餐、守岁、压岁钱用途
- 信息完整性：另一个节日、今年愿望、节日的作用
- 发音准确性：dumplings / envelope 的音节；Festival 与 mooncakes 的重音
- 表达流利度：and 连接的并列叙述要有层次
- 语法正确性：I hope to learn 与 I saved 的时态区分

## E8-08 环境
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你所在城市的一项环境问题，说说人们可以做什么。
**参考作答**：I live in Suzhou, which is a city with many gardens and old streets. The air here is much better than it was ten years ago, because the factories have moved away. People in my neighbourhood take their rubbish to the recycling point instead of putting it in the river. My family walks or takes the bus instead of driving when we go to the city centre. At school we have a poster about saving water, and our class cleans the small river near our gate once a month. There are also small gardens along many streets, and my neighbours water them together at weekends. Some people still throw rubbish into the water, which is a real problem. I think a clean city is not only the duty of the government, but also the duty of every person.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：城市特点、空气改善与原因、社区回收、绿色出行、校园行动、街道小花园与邻居共同浇灌
- 信息完整性：现存问题、责任归属的观点
- 发音准确性：recycling / rubbish / neighbourhood 的重音与词尾 -ing
- 表达流利度：instead of 与 not only 的对比效果
- 语法正确性：which is a real problem 的非限定定语从句

## E8-09 网络与阅读
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍你的阅读习惯，说说网络与纸书的不同。
**参考作答**：I read a lot, but these days most of my reading is done on my phone. I usually read English news after dinner, and it takes me about twenty minutes. Before bed I still like to read a paper book, because it is easier on my eyes. My favourite writer is Lu Xun, and I have read several of his short stories. I also keep a small notebook where I write down new words that I meet. My school library lets us borrow two books at a time, and I almost always take both. Sometimes I search for information on the internet, but I always check it in a dictionary first. I think reading is the cheapest way to travel to another place.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：主要载体、每日时段、纸质书偏好与理由、喜欢的作者、笔记本用途、学校图书馆可借两本
- 信息完整性：上网络的习惯与核对方式、阅读的价值
- 发音准确性：internet / dictionary / writer 的音节；writer 与 notebook 的词尾辅音
- 表达流利度：两处 but 的转折层次
- 语法正确性：is done 的被动结构；where 引导的定语从句

## E8-10 理想与计划
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，说说你长大后的理想，以及你为此做了哪些准备。
**参考作答**：I want to be a teacher when I grow up, although my parents hope that I will study science. My reason is simple: I once had a teacher who was patient with me when I was ill in Grade Seven. In the future I hope to work in a small school in my hometown. To reach that goal, I need to study hard and learn to speak well in front of others. Next term I will join the speech club at our school. I also plan to read one book about teaching every month. My parents say that teaching does not pay well, but I do not mind. If I change my mind later, that is fine too, and I will still be happy.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：理想与家人期待的分歧、原因、理想工作地点、需要的准备、下一步行动
- 信息完整性：每月读书计划、对收入的看法、允许自己改变主意
- 发音准确性：teacher / patient / hometown 的重音
- 表达流利度：although / To reach that goal / also 构成清晰层次
- 语法正确性：hope that 从句与主句时态呼应；I need to 表安排的用法

## E8-11 志愿服务
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍一次志愿服务的经历，说说你从中得到了什么。
**参考作答**：Last Spring Festival I took part in a volunteer activity at the railway station. My job was simple: I helped old people find the right waiting room and carried their bags. The work lasted three days, and I was tired when I came home each evening. Still, I felt useful, because many travellers did not know where to go. Our class has started a small project to collect old books for a village school. We have already sent two boxes, and we are planning to send more before the holiday. Our teacher says that anyone can volunteer, even if you only have an hour a week. I think volunteering is not only about helping others, but also about meeting people whom I would never meet otherwise.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：时间与地点、具体工作、持续时长、身体感受、心理感受、老师说每周一小时也可参与
- 信息完整性：班级新项目、已完成的成果、志愿服务的意义
- 发音准确性：volunteer / railway / village 的音节
- 表达流利度：Still 引出转折，also 引出补充
- 语法正确性：whom 引导的定语从句；not only ... but also

## E8-12 传统文化
**话题题目**：请用英语作一段话，介绍一项中国传统文化，说说它现在有什么变化。
**参考作答**：Our country has many old traditions, and my family keeps some of them. On the fifth day of the fifth lunar month we eat rice dumplings to remember a poet named Qu Yuan. My grandmother used to make them by hand, but now she teaches me, and I am still not very good at it. During the Dragon Boat Festival there are boat races on the river near our town. We also keep the custom of giving red money to children at the Spring Festival. Our class also learned paper cutting from an old teacher last term. Some traditions are changing, but I hope they will not disappear. I think traditions are a way for us to remember where we come from.
**评分要点**：
- 信息完整性（占七成）：家庭保留的传统、端午与屈原、祖母手艺的变化、龙舟比赛、春节压岁、班上向老艺人学剪纸
- 信息完整性：传统面临的变化、个人的态度
- 发音准确性：traditions / dumplings 的音节与复数尾音；Dragon Boat Festival 三词连读
- 表达流利度：but / also / I think 三层推进
- 语法正确性：used to make 与现在时的对照；a poet named Qu Yuan 中 named 作后置定语
