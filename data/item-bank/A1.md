## A1-01 选课咨询
**Dialogue**
Boy: I'd like to take the computer course. Is it still available?
Girl: Sorry, it's full. I hear the art club has a place on Wednesday afternoon.
Boy: That's fine. I like drawing, too. Where do we meet?
Girl: In the art room next to the gym.
**Question**
What will the boy do on Wednesday afternoon?
- A. Take the computer course.
- B. Join the art club.
- C. Ask his teacher for help.
- D. Meet his new classmates.
**Answer** B
