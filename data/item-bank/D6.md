# D6 类 · 记录表与登记表类表格填空（10 份）

> 来源：并行子任务产出｜共 10 份 / 30 空｜每份词库 5 个短语，恰好 2 个为干扰项
> 全部为仿写素材，非真题。本题型（材料 + 表格 + 挖空 + 从词库选短语填入）依据用户记忆生成，**结构未经官方证实**。
> 本批表格列数口径：10 份统一为 **5 列**（失物登记、借阅记录、植物观察、参观登记、气象观察、实验记录、订购登记、作品登记、报名登记、家庭水电记录）。依据：D 类每份只挖 3 空，5 列可在同一张表内同时给出行定位与列口径，且与 D4（时间表类）、D5（分类对应与对比表类）的表形不重合，全批不用 6 列。
> 本批人物与称谓口径：叙述与对话一律用英文名，称呼只用 Mr. 与 Ms.，说话人统一标注为 M: 与 W:，全批一致。
> 本批题号：D6-01 至 D6-10，主题与 D-batch1、D4、D5 均不重复。

## D6-01 失物招领登记簿
**Passage**
Our school keeps its lost-and-found book in the gatehouse, and Mr. Brown, the gatekeeper, writes in it every afternoon. The pages now in use belong to March, and the last entry in them is 12 March. In the date column he always writes the day first and then the month, and he never writes the year. On 8 March he found a blue water bottle in the reading room, and Jack carried it home the same day. On 10 March he picked up a pair of black gloves in the dining hall, and Nina got them back on 12 March. A school badge was found on the stairs between the second and the third floor. Emma, a new student in Class Two, said the badge was hers, so Mr. Brown gave it to her in the gatehouse.
**Table**
| Item | Where it was found | Date in the book | Who took it home | Given back to the owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a blue water bottle | ① | 8 March | Jack | yes |
| a pair of black gloves | ② | 10 March | Nina | yes |
| a school badge | on the stairs | 12 March | ③ | yes |
**Word Bank**
A. in the reading room
B. Emma
C. in the dining hall
D. in the gatehouse
E. Jack
**Answers**
① A　② C　③ B

## D6-02 社区图书角借阅记录
**Dialogue**
M: Our reading corner has a borrowing record now. Ms. Zhou, our volunteer, started the new page on 2 April.
W: What do the five columns mean?
M: The five columns are the reader, the name of the book, the day the book left the corner, the day it should come back, and whether the reader gave it back on time. In both date columns she writes the day and the month, like 8 April, and never the year.
W: So the first line is our class, right? We took nine copies of a story collection on 4 April, and they are due on 11 April. Jack is our monitor, so his name goes in the first column.
M: Right. The second line is Nina. She took a story about a little white horse home on 8 April, and it should have come back on 15 April, but she has not returned it yet.
W: The third line is Leo. He borrowed a science book about the sea on 12 April and gave it back on time on 19 April.
**Table**
| Reader | Name of the book | Day it left the corner | Day it is due back | Back on time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jack | a story collection for the class | 4 April | 11 April | yes |
| Nina | ① | 8 April | ② | no |
| Leo | ③ | 12 April | 19 April | yes |
**Word Bank**
A. 15 April
B. 11 April
C. 12 April
D. a story about a little white horse
E. a science book about the sea
**Answers**
① D　② A　③ E

## D6-04 自然博物馆参观登记单
**Dialogue**
W: Did you see the new visitor book at the Nature Museum? Ms. Gao, who works in the office there, began the new page on 8 June.
M: I did. She keeps five columns: the name of the visitor, the day of the visit, the price of the ticket, the hall the visitor went to, and the group the visitor came with. In the date column she writes the day first and then the month, and she never writes the year. The price column always ends with the word yuan.
W: Then what about our class? We went there on 6 June. Emma went to the hall of wild animals, and I went to the hall of ancient China. Both of us paid 12 yuan, because students pay less than adults.
M: Nina went there on her own the next day. She is a college student now, so she paid 20 yuan, and she stayed in the hall of space all afternoon.
**Table**
| Name | Day of the visit | Price of the ticket | Hall | Group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emma | 6 June | 12 yuan | ① | Class Two |
| Jack | 6 June | 12 yuan | ② | Class Two |
| Nina | 7 June | 20 yuan | ③ | alone |
**Word Bank**
A. the hall of space
B. the hall of ancient China
C. the hall of machines
D. the hall of birds
E. the hall of wild animals
**Answers**
① E　② B　③ A

## D6-05 气象观察记录
**Passage**
Our school writes the weather down on a board at the front of the science building. The student on duty writes one line every morning at eight o'clock, and only the past month is kept on the board. The new page began on 1 April. In the two temperature columns the students write the number and the letter C, and they never use the degree sign. On 5 April it rained in the morning and stopped before noon, so the highest temperature was 23 and the lowest was 11. On 7 April the sky stayed cloudy all day, and the temperature did not go above 18; the lowest was 13. On 10 April the sun came out and it was the hottest day of the month. The highest was 28, and the lowest was 16. The wind was light on 5 April, there was no wind at all on 7 April, and a strong wind came from the north on 10 April.
**Table**
| Day | Weather | Highest (C) | Lowest (C) | Wind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 April | ① | 23 | 11 | light |
| 7 April | cloudy | ② | 13 | none |
| 10 April | sunny and hot | 28 | ③ | strong |
**Word Bank**
A. 23
B. 11
C. rainy in the morning
D. 16
E. 18
**Answers**
① C　② E　③ D

## D6-06 厨房小实验记录单
**Dialogue**
M: Mum and I did three kitchen experiments last weekend, and she helped me write them in her notebook. She began the new page on 16 March, and she always writes the day first and then the month.
W: What was the first one?
M: The first one was about the colour of red cabbage. I put the leaves in hot water and the water turned pink. It took 10 minutes, so I wrote that line myself.
W: And the second one?
M: The second one was about eggs. We dropped a raw egg into a cup of salt water, and the egg stayed at the bottom. It took only 5 minutes. Kate did that one with me.
W: What about the third one?
M: The third one was about baking soda and vinegar. He mixed them in a bowl, and the mixture rose like a cake. It took 8 minutes. Leo did that one on his own, because his sister Nina was out that afternoon.
**Table**
| Experiment | What we used | Minutes | What we saw | Who did the experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the colour of red cabbage | ① | 10 | the water turned pink | I |
| a raw egg in salt water | an egg and salt water | 5 | the egg stayed at the bottom | ② |
| baking soda and vinegar | soda and vinegar | 8 | the mixture rose like a cake | ③ |
**Word Bank**
A. red cabbage leaves in hot water
B. hot water and a green leaf
C. Leo
D. Nina
E. Kate
**Answers**
① A　② E　③ C

This year the shop has four sizes in stock: S, M, L and XL. Our class ordered on the first day. Jack is tall and thin, so he chose size M, and he took two shirts and one pair of shorts. Nina is smaller, so she chose size S, and she took two shirts only. Leo has a large head, so Ms. Liu ordered size L for him, with one shirt and one pair of shorts, and his father came to pay on 9 September.

| Nina | ② | two | 0 | 6 September |
| Leo | L | one | one | ③ |
**Word Bank**

B. S
C. XL
D. M

**Answers**

## D6-09 书法比赛报名登记表
**Dialogue**
M: Ms. Hu at the district office opened the sign-up for the calligraphy competition on 5 November, and the last day was 12 November. Her table has five columns: the name, the group, the event, the day the form arrived, and where the form came from. The event is written out in full, and the day before the month. Grade Nine students can enter only the senior group, and Grade Eight students only the junior group.
W: Who from our school entered?
M: Jack entered the senior group and chose hard-pen writing. He sent his form on the first day, 5 November, through the school office. Nina chose brush writing, but she is only in Grade Eight, so she entered the junior group; her mother sent the form from her office on 9 November.
W: What about Emma?
M: Emma is in Grade Nine, and she chose hard-pen writing and sent her form on the very last day, 12 November, from the community centre where she studies. The office ran an English handwriting event too, but only for students from three other schools.
**Table**
| Name | Group | Event | Day the form arrived | Where the form came from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jack | senior | ① | 5 November | the school office |
| Nina | junior | ② | 9 November | her mother's office |
| Emma | ③ | hard-pen writing | 12 November | the community centre |
**Word Bank**
A. brush writing
B. senior
C. junior
D. English handwriting
E. hard-pen writing
**Answers**
① E　② A　③ B

## D6-10 家庭用电与用水记录
**Passage**
My family writes down the electricity and the water we use, because the two bills change a lot between summer and winter. My mother keeps one book for this, and she started a new page on 1 January. In the electricity column she writes only the number and the letter k, and in the water column only the number and the letter t. Two months ago we used 118 k of electricity and 15 t of water, and the bill was 78 yuan. Last month we used 145 k of electricity, but the water went down to 12 t, because it rained a lot. My mother says the electricity goes up when we run the air conditioner on hot days. The bill last month was 96 yuan. This month so far she has written 102 k of electricity, 18 t of water and a bill of 85 yuan in the book.
**Table**
| Month | Electricity (k) | Water (t) | Bill (yuan) | Who wrote the line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| two months ago | 118 | 15 | 78 | my mother |
| last month | ① | ② | ③ | me |
| this month so far | 102 | 18 | 85 | my mother |
**Word Bank**
A. 118
B. 145
C. 96
D. 12
E. 15
**Answers**
① B　② D　③ C
