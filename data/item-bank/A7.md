> A7 生活场景（15 题）

## A7-01 课间借尺

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Hu, may I use your ruler? Mine is in my bag.
Ms. Hu: Take it. Please give it back before the bell.
Boy: The bell rings at ten. Class Two begins at ten fifteen.
Ms. Hu: All right. You have enough time for the map test.
Boy: Thanks. I'll draw the river on it now.

**Question**
When must the boy give the ruler back?
- A. At ten fifteen.
- B. At ten thirty.
- C. After Class Two.
- D. Before the bell.

**Answer** D

## A7-02 早餐两杯茶

**Dialogue**
Mum: Ben, it's Saturday. Get up, or we'll miss the train.
Ben: What time does it leave?
Mum: At seven twenty. I want eggs and toast, not cereal.
Ben: Shall I make some tea?
Mum: Yes, two cups. One is for your father and one for me.

**Question**
Who will drink tea with the breakfast?
- A. Ben and his father.
- B. Ben's father and Mum.
- C. Mum and Ben.
- D. Ben's father and his sister.

**Answer** B

## A7-03 买十二元的本子

**Dialogue**
Boy: Good afternoon, Mr. Sun. I need a notebook for science.
Mr. Sun: We have four, five, twelve and twenty yuan ones.
Boy: My friend buys the four yuan one for every subject.
Mr. Sun: Science notes are printed on thick paper. Take the twelve yuan one.
Boy: All right. Twelve yuan, please.

**Question**
How much will the boy pay?
- A. Four yuan.
- B. Twenty yuan.
- C. Twelve yuan.
- D. Five yuan.

**Answer** C

## A7-04 米饭要炒的

**Dialogue**
Waiter: Good evening. What would you like to eat?
Girl: I want the chicken and rice, not the beef.
Waiter: How would you like your rice? Fried or boiled?
Girl: Fried, please. Boiled rice is too plain for me.
Waiter: Right. And the chicken — with soup or without?
Girl: Without soup. I'll drink water after.

**Question**
How will the girl have her rice?
- A. Fried.
- B. Boiled.
- C. Fried with soup.
- D. Boiled with soup.

**Answer** A

## A7-05 周六坐公交

**Dialogue**
Boy: Excuse me. How can I get to the museum?
Man: Take bus No. 15 and get off at the third stop.
Boy: Is there a train as well?
Man: Yes. It leaves from the north station, but it does not run on Saturdays.
Boy: Saturday. Then I take the bus.

**Question**
How will the boy go to the museum?
- A. By train today.
- B. By bus.
- C. By bus on Sunday.
- D. By train on Sunday.

**Answer** B

## A7-06 周五上午看牙

**Dialogue**
Boy: Doctor, my tooth hurts, and I love ice cream.
Doctor: I can see a small hole. Come back on Friday morning.
Boy: I have a maths test on Friday afternoon.
Doctor: Take the medicine twice a day. Eat no ice cream until Friday.
Boy: All right. Friday morning, then.

**Question**
When will the boy see the doctor again?
- A. On Friday afternoon.
- B. On Thursday morning.
- C. On Monday afternoon.
- D. On Friday morning.

**Answer** D

## A7-07 借书两周

**Dialogue**
Librarian: Can I help you?
Boy: I'd like to borrow that book about the sea.
Librarian: I'm afraid you can't. You can only read it here.
Boy: Then I'll take this one about birds.
Librarian: You may keep it for two weeks. One week is too short, and one month is too long.
Boy: Two weeks. Thank you.

**Question**
How long may the boy keep the book about birds?
- A. Two weeks.
- B. One week.
- C. One month.
- D. Three weeks.

**Answer** A

## A7-08 平寄五日到

**Dialogue**
Boy: Hello. I want to send this box to my aunt in Nanjing.
Clerk: It weighs three kilos. Do you want express or post?
Boy: Post, please. I have no express money.
Clerk: Then it will take five days. Express takes one day, the train two days.
Boy: Five days is fine. My aunt's birthday is on Sunday.

**Question**
How long will the box take to reach Nanjing?
- A. One day.
- B. Two days.
- C. Five days.
- D. Three days.

**Answer** C

## A7-09 周一上午报名

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Li, how can I join the photography club?
Mr. Li: Bring your camera to Room 6 on Monday morning.
Girl: What time should I come? My lunch is at twelve.
Mr. Li: At nine, before lunch. The room is free then. After nine it is busy.

**Question**
When will the girl come to Room 6?
- A. On Tuesday morning.
- B. On Monday morning.
- C. On Monday after lunch.
- D. On Friday morning.

**Answer** B

## A7-10 第几节上地理

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Fang, which period is our geography lesson today?
Mr. Fang: The fourth, not the third. The third is free study.
Girl: I have music in the fourth then.
Mr. Fang: Music moved to Thursday. Take your geography book.

**Question**
Which period will the class have geography today?
- A. The second.
- B. The third.
- C. The fourth.
- D. The fifth.

**Answer** C

## A7-11 雨伞不要帽子

**Dialogue**
Boy: Mum, may I wear my new shoes and a hat today?
Mum: It may rain this afternoon. Take an umbrella, not a hat.
Boy: Then what shall I wear? My thick coat?
Mum: No. It's only fifteen degrees. Wear your old shoes, not the new ones.

**Question**
What will the boy take with him?
- A. An umbrella.
- B. A hat.
- C. His thick coat.
- D. His new shoes.

**Answer** A

## A7-12 决赛对手

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Pei, our class is Class Two. Who do we play against in the final?
Ms. Pei: Class Two against Class Five. The match starts at three on Friday.
Boy: Is it on the big playground?
Ms. Pei: No, the small one. The big one is under repair this week.
Boy: Fine. Friday, then.

**Question**
Who will the boy's class play against in the final?
- A. Class One.
- B. Class Three.
- C. Class Five.
- D. Class Six.

**Answer** C

## A7-13 七月十五夏令营

**Dialogue**
Girl: Mum, when does the summer camp start?
Mum: On the fifteenth of July, not on the first.
Girl: My cousin goes on the fifth. May I go with him?
Mum: No. You have an exam on the tenth. Then come to the camp on the fifteenth.

**Question**
On which day will the girl go to the summer camp?
- A. On the fifteenth of July.
- B. On the first of July.
- C. On the fifth of July.
- D. On the tenth of July.

**Answer** A

## A7-14 周六校门集合

**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wei, where do the volunteers meet on Saturday?
Ms. Wei: At the school gate at eight. Not at the library or the bus stop.
Boy: What shall we do there?
Ms. Wei: Read to the children in the old people's home. We finish at eleven.

**Question**
Where will the volunteers meet on Saturday?
- A. At the library.
- B. At the bus stop.
- C. At the children's park.
- D. At the school gate.

**Answer** D

## A7-15 家长会提前半小时

**Dialogue**
Teacher: Ms. Li, the parents' meeting is on Tuesday at four. Can you come?
Ms. Li: I work on Tuesday evening. Is there another meeting?
Teacher: No, only one. But you can come at half past three.
Ms. Li: Then I'll come early. Which room?
Teacher: Room 2 on the second floor, not Room 5 on the ground floor.

**Question**
When will the mother go to the parents' meeting?
- A. On Monday at four.
- B. On Tuesday at half past three.
- C. On Tuesday at four.
- D. On Wednesday at four.

**Answer** B
