- **位置**：C3-01 Passage 第 4 句 与 第 10 句
- **问题**：两句话对同一台**累计式**水表的读数给出了互斥的描述。
  - 第 4 句：`When they compared their first reading with the number their science teacher had copied in January, **the gap was far larger than any change they had seen before**.`
  - 第 10 句：`The class went on reading the meter for one more month. **That reading was almost the same as the January one**, and the students put a short report on the classroom wall.`
- **为什么是问题**：水表读数**只增不减**。既然「3 月读数与 1 月读数的差距 far larger」，那么 4 月（换新龙头满一个月后）的读数必然**更大**，绝不可能 almost the same as the January one。这不是表达瑕疵，而是**同一篇材料内两句话直接打架**，任何读完的学生都会在这里卡住；同时它也让 C3-01 Q3 所依托的时间段（one more month）失去可信度。属规范 1.2「读起来说不通」→ 严重。
- **建议改法（只改第 10 句前半）**：

  原文：`That reading was almost the same as the January one, and the students put a short report on the classroom wall.`

  改为：`The number rose by only about the same amount as in an ordinary month, and the students put a short report on the classroom wall.`

  - **是否真的修到问题点**：是。新句把比较对象从「绝对读数」换成「**周增量**」，而第 4 句已经确立了「1 月→3 月的**增量**很大」，因此「4 月的**增量**恢复正常」与前文完全自洽，且正好落在「漏水被堵住」这一因果上，比原句更贴题。
  - **上下文边界**：仅替换 Passage 内这一句，**不触碰**第 4 句、第 6 句（30 L/日 × 90 日 = 2700 L）与第 11 句。
  - **耦合影响**：Q3 题干与选项 A **均不受影响**（动作未变）；Q2 依据第 5 句，Q4/Q5 不涉此处。**C3-01 词数由 186 词变为 197 词，仍在 150–220 内**（+11 词）。

## 中等缺陷

### M1. C3-02 一周 180 kg 与「两只布袋 + 老人步行」承载力不匹配 —— 量级矛盾

- **位置**：C3-02 Passage 第 1、5、7 句
- **问题**：`walks slowly around Block Nine with two cloth bags`（随身两只布袋）→ `His record for one week is a hundred and eighty kilograms`。同时材料**从未解释**他为何既随身带布袋、又有一个市民投放的红箱，构成两条**并行而无交代**的收集渠道。
- **为什么是问题**：180 kg 不可能由一位步行的老人用两只布袋搬运；学生一旦算出量级会怀疑材料。属规范 1.2「数字/量级前后讲不通」。
- **建议改法**：把布袋定位为「取走错放物」的工具，删掉与红箱重复的收集职能。改第 2 句：
  原文：`He has done this for six years.`
  改为：`He has done this for six years, and he now carries two cloth bags for the things he takes out of the box.`
  - **是否真的修到问题点**：是。把布袋的用途锚定在「从箱里取出错放物」上，与后文 `He simply takes them out and shows them to the owner` 直接呼应，消除双渠道矛盾；重量只由红箱承担，与布袋脱钩，量级矛盾随之消失。
  - **上下文边界**：只改这一句，**不动**第 5、7 句的 180 kg / 50 kg 与第 10 句的 400 families。
  - **耦合影响**：Q1–Q5 的材料依据均不涉及第 1 句的布袋用途描述，**全部不受影响**。
  - ⚠️ **词数耦合提示**：C3-02 现为 **215 词，距 220 上限仅 5 词**，该改法净增约 12 词会**超限**。**必须同时等量压掉约 12 词**（推荐压缩 `and other things that people should not put in the normal rubbish bin` → `and things that should not go in the normal bin`，-6 词；再删 `sudden` -1 词；再把 `walks slowly around Block Nine` → `walks slowly around the block` -1 词），**否则本条修复会把 C3-02 顶出 220 上限**。

### M2. C3-04 待机耗电的因果被写反 —— 材料内部逻辑倒置

- **位置**：C3-04 Passage 第 3 句
- **问题**：`The set is waiting for a signal from the remote control, **the computer slowly loses its settings**, and the little box keeps a light burning all night.` 该句本是解释「为什么待机要耗一点电」，但「电脑慢慢丢失设置」恰恰是**断电之后**的后果，不是保持电流的**结果**。
- **为什么是问题**：材料自相矛盾——保留备用电正是为了**不丢设置**，句中却把它写成丢设置。且 C3-04 Q3 的正确项 B 正是同段第 2 句的目的从句，学生一旦注意到第 3 句的反转，会动摇对 B 的判断。
- **建议改法**：
  原文：`the computer slowly loses its settings`
  改为：`the computer slowly goes to sleep`
  - **是否真的修到问题点**：是。「进入休眠」是待机耗电的正确结果，与前两项（等信号、指示灯常亮）构成同向的三项解释，因果不再倒置。
  - **上下文边界**：仅 Passage 第 3 句中一个短语，**不动**同句另两项。
  - **耦合影响**：**等词数替换（4 词 → 4 词），C3-04 词数仍为 176，不变** ✅。Q3 的选项与答案**不依赖本句**（依据在第 2 句），**不受影响**。

### M3. C3-04「水表读数一周后变小」物理不可读 —— 逻辑缺陷

- **位置**：C3-04 Passage 第 7 句
- **问题**：`One week later **the number on the meter was one unit lower than the week before**.` 电表是累计表，一周后读数只会更高，绝不会更低。
- **为什么是问题**：与 S1 同族。此处**尚无第二句与之互斥**，且真实意图（一周少用 1 度电）可从 `That one unit was enough to run a rice cooker for two hours` 还原，故定中等而非严重；但学生读到会卡住。
- **建议改法（低耦合最小修复）**：
  原文：`One week later the number on the meter was one unit lower than the week before.`
  改为：`One week later the number on the meter showed one unit less than the week before.`
  - **是否真的修到问题点**：**部分**。`showed … less` 把比较对象从「表上的数字本身」改为「表显示的**用量**」，消除了「累计值变小」的物理错误。
  - **⚠️ 自查与边界（如实声明）**：改后语义仍可被读作「读数变小」，**歧义未彻底根除**。更彻底的版本是 `In that week they had used one unit less than in the week before.`，但该写法会**使 Q2 的四个选项（Ten/One units less than the week before 与 Exactly the same）失去逐字锚点**，属强耦合改动，**不建议在本轮一并做**。**因此本报告给出的是低耦合最小修复，并明确声明它只能做到「消除物理错误、保留轻微歧义」，而非彻底根除。**
  - **耦合影响**：`was` → `showed`（+1 词），C3-04 变为 177，仍在区间内。Q2 答案 C 不变。

### M4. C3-02 代词单复数不匹配 —— 单复数矛盾

- **位置**：C3-02 Passage 第 9 句
- **问题**：`many neighbours still put a paper cup or a broken phone into the red box. He does not get angry. He simply **takes them out and shows them to the owner** at the next gate.` 前句主语为复数 many neighbours、物品为 a paper cup or a broken phone（可数、可多件），后句却用单数 the owner。
- **为什么是问题**：严格说应为 their owners；现写法使 them（复数）与 the owner（单数）在同一句内对不上，属规范 1.3 的单复数问题。C3-02 Q1 选项 C 抄的是单数版 `He takes it out and shows the owner.`。
- **建议改法**：
  原文：`He simply takes them out and shows them to the owner at the next gate.`
  改为：`He simply takes each one out and shows it to its owner at the next gate.`
  - **是否真的修到问题点**：是。`each one … its owner` 与前句的 many neighbours 兼容，且**与 Q1 选项 C 的单数形式完全同构**，改后题干与答案反而更贴合。
  - **上下文边界**：仅本句，**不动**第 10 句的 `those short talks`（复数仍与下文 four hundred families learned 相容）。
  - **耦合影响**：净增约 2 词，C3-02 变为 217 词——**仍在 220 内，但余量只剩 3 词**，故本条**必须与 M1 的等量压缩合并执行**。

- D `More than four hundred kilograms.` — 混淆末句的 four hundred **families** ✅排除
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

**Q3**（答 D）｜事实
- 依据：`Last year four hundred families learned to use the box correctly`
- A `forty` / B `ninety` / C `nine hundred` — 材料中**仅** 400 一处家族数 ✅
- **结论：唯一，四项全为 About + 数词 + families ✅**

**Q4**（答 A）｜事实（非推断，见 M5）
- 依据：`a sudden rise in the number tells him that someone has put the wrong thing in the box`
- B `The city pays him for every kilogram.` — 与 `free of charge` 及 `not because the numbers are important` **双重冲突** ✅排除
- C `His family wants him to collect more.` — 全篇无家庭 ✅排除
- D `The things must be sent to another city.` — 无材料依据 ✅排除
- **结论：唯一 ✅**（但考点为逐字细节，见 M5）

**Q5**（答 C）｜主旨
- 依据：全篇
- A `How a city office measures the rubbish each year.` — 主体是老人非市府；且是每周非每年 ✅
- B `How two cloth bags can be used every Wednesday.` — 只覆盖细节 ✅
- D `How old medicine should be given to a shop.` — 无材料依据 ✅
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

### C3-03 十六次日出的地方（183 词）

**Q1**（答 D）｜计算型推断
- 依据：`about sixteen times in twenty-four hours` → 24÷16 = 1.5 h = **90 min**
- A `About once every three hours.`（=24÷8）/ C `About once every four hours.`（=24÷6）— 学生可自行验算排除 ✅
- B `About twice in twenty-four hours.` — 与 sixteen times 直接矛盾 ✅
- **结论：唯一，四项全带 About ✅**

**Q2**（答 B）｜事实
- 依据：`Every drop is packed in a small bag, and the crew drinks it through a straw.`
- A `From an open cup on a table.` — 与「绝不从壶里倒水、每滴水装袋」矛盾 ✅
- C `From a bottle with a wide mouth.` — 无材料依据 ✅
- D `From a pipe fixed to the wall.` — 材料中固定在墙上的是**睡袋**，跨句干扰 ✅（优质干扰项）
- **结论：唯一，四项同为 From + 名词短语 ✅**

**Q3**（答 A）｜事实
- 依据：`it is never served on a plate, because anything loose would slowly float to the ceiling`
- B `Because there is no table in the station.` — 与 `nobody leaves the table` **直接矛盾** ✅
- C `Because the crew eats much faster than usual.` — 与 `one of the quietest meals` 矛盾 ✅
- D `Because the plates cannot be washed in space.` — 无材料依据 ✅
- **结论：唯一，四项同为 Because + 从句 ✅**

**Q4**（答 C）｜推断
- 依据：`the crew first count the boxes together and then sit down to eat at the same time … nobody leaves the table until everyone has finished`
- A `They always eat alone in their own rooms.` — 与 at the same time 矛盾 ✅
- B `They eat only after the boxes are empty.` — 材料是 **count**（清点）非 empty（清空）✅
- D `They eat in a hurry to save the food.` — 与 quietest meals 矛盾 ✅
- **结论：唯一，学生可完成 ✅**

**Q5**（答 D）｜主旨
- 依据：全篇（太阳周期 / 睡眠 / 饮食 / 到访餐）
- A `How the sun moves around the earth each day.` — 材料明说是**空间站**绕地球转，且非「每天一次」；且主旨应为乘员生活 ✅（弱干扰项，见 L2）
- B `How a supply ship is filled by the crew.` — 只覆盖末句 ✅
- C `How the crew grows fresh fruit in space.` — 与 `Fresh fruit and warm bread are the things they **miss** most`（吃不到才想）**语义相反**，优质干扰项 ✅
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

### C3-04 拔掉的插头（176 词）

**Q1**（答 A）｜事实
- 依据：`they pulled out the plug of every machine in the living room except the fridge`
- B `every machine in the kitchen` — 地点错（living room）✅
- C `the machine in the bedroom` — 同上 ✅
- D `The old meter on the ground floor.` — 类别错；材料拔的是机器插头，Building Six 是住户楼 ✅
- **结论：唯一，三项同为 The plug of… ✅**

**Q2**（答 C）｜事实
- 依据：`the number on the meter was one unit lower than the week before`（逻辑问题见 M3）
- A `Ten units less` — 无材料依据 ✅
- B `Exactly the same` — 与原文矛盾 ✅
- D `One unit more` — 方向相反 ✅
- **结论：唯一，四项同为「数量 + than the week before」 ✅**

**Q3**（答 B）｜事实（**本轮被重写，重点复核**）
- 依据：`A television, a computer and a small TV box all keep a little electricity going, so that they can answer at once when you press a button.` —— **与选项 B 逐字相同**
- A `So that they use more power when they are working.` — 与 `a machine which is not in use takes no electricity. That is not true.` 方向对立 ✅
- C `So that the family can save one unit a week.` — 因果倒置（省电是拔插头的**结果**）✅
- D `So that they can make cold by themselves.` — 会制冷的是冰箱，且冰箱被**排除在测试外** ✅
- **结论：唯一；四项句式统一；B 有逐字材料依据 ✅**
- ⚠️ 附带问题：同段第 3 句 `the computer slowly loses its settings` 存在因果倒置（见 M2），但**不破坏 B 的唯一性**。

**Q4**（答 D）｜推断
- 依据：`The family had expected a much bigger number, so the father … found that the numbers matched.`
- A `believed that the test would prove nothing` — 与「在意结果、回头翻旧账单核对」矛盾 ✅
- B `bought a new machine for every room` — 无材料依据 ✅
- C `then stopped using electricity in the flat` — 与冰箱整周运行矛盾 ✅
- D `They trusted a small number more than a belief.` — 可推出 ✅（措辞歧义见 M7）
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

**Q5**（答 A）｜主旨
- 依据：全篇
- B `How an old man learned to repair his own meter.` — 人物与行为均不符 ✅
- C `How people should choose a rice cooker for a home.` — 只覆盖一句 ✅
- D `How a long summer holiday changed a street.` — 无材料依据 ✅
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

### C3-05 树荫下的地面（207 词）

**Q1**（答 C）｜事实
- 依据：`At three o'clock the playground was eleven degrees hotter than the ground under the tree.`
- A `Two degrees.` = 草地比树高 2 度 ✅排除
- D `Four degrees.` = 墙边比树高 4 度 ✅排除
- B `Seven degrees.` = 操场比墙边高 7 度（11−4，学生可自行算出）✅排除
- **结论：唯一。** 这是全卷质量最高的一题：**三个干扰项都能在材料中找到真实数字，却都不是所问的差值** ✅

**Q2**（答 A）｜事实
- 依据：`at one o'clock, two o'clock, three o'clock and four o'clock` → 四次
- B `Once, at one o'clock` — 与四个时间点矛盾 ✅
- C `Twice` — 同上 ✅
- D `Every half hour from ten to four` — 材料只列 1/2/3/4 点 ✅
- **结论：唯一 ✅**

**Q3**（答 B）｜事实
- 依据：`Now they measure the same four places every May`
- A `In every July.` — **优质干扰项**：July 是本次测试的时间（`last July`），但「再测」是 May，学生须区分两个时间层 ✅
- C `In every January.` / D `In every September.` — 无材料依据 ✅
- **结论：唯一，四项同为 In every + 月份 ✅**

**Q4**（答 D）｜事实（非推断，见 M5）
- 依据：`Nobody in the club had known this before.`
- A `They had already known the whole answer.` — 与上句**逐字相反** ✅排除
- B `They then gave up the club after the test.` — 无材料依据，且与 `Now they measure … every May` 矛盾 ✅
- C `They no longer wanted to talk about trees.` — 与「仍在坚持测量」矛盾 ✅
- **结论：唯一 ✅**（但退化为查词题，见 M5）

**Q5**（答 C）｜主旨
- 依据：`Then the teacher showed them how a tree cools the ground … When water turns into gas, it takes heat away from the leaf and from the ground around it.`
- A `How a school chooses young trees for its ground.` — 只覆盖末句 ✅
- B `Why a summer afternoon is long at school.` — 无关 ✅
- D `How pupils count the heat of hot ground.` — 只覆盖测温部分 ✅
- **结论：唯一；How/Why 各 2 项，格式均衡 ✅**（措辞对齐见 L5）
