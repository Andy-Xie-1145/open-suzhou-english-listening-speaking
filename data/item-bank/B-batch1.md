## B-01 读书角志愿者
**Dialogue**
Ann: Hi, Leo. Have you signed up for the Book Swap on Friday afternoon?
Leo: Not yet. Our class still needs two volunteers to look after the reading corner.
Ann: The same here. My teacher asked us to move the books from Classroom 302 today.
Leo: Are you free on Thursday morning then? We can put them on the shelves first.
Ann: I think so. But my art teacher wants me to finish a poster by Wednesday.
Leo: How is the poster going? You told me you had already started it.
Ann: Slowly. My hand got tired, and I kept changing the colours.
Leo: Perhaps you should finish it on Tuesday. Then Thursday will be free.
Ann: Good idea. I'll bring the poster to art class tomorrow morning.
Leo: Then let's meet at the school library at nine on Thursday.
Ann: Fine. I'll tell our monitor that I can come.
Leo: Great. Two helpers are just what we need for the reading corner.
**Q1**
When will Ann and Leo meet at the school library?
- A. On Thursday morning.
- B. On Tuesday morning.
- C. On Friday afternoon.
- D. On Wednesday afternoon.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
What made the poster difficult for Ann?
- A. She did not have enough time to work on it.
- B. Her art teacher asked her to finish it early.
- C. Her hand got tired and she kept changing the colours.
- D. She had too many other subjects to study.
**Answer** C

## B-02 更换笔记本
**Dialogue**
Emma: Excuse me. I'd like to exchange this notebook, please.
Clerk: Sorry, we can only change a notebook bought within seven days. May I see your receipt?
Emma: Here it is. I bought it last Saturday, but I chose the wrong size.
Clerk: Of course. Would you like a larger notebook or a different colour?
Emma: A larger one, please. This one has only ten pages, and I need more.
Clerk: Sorry, the thirty-page version just sold out this morning. A fifty-page one is still here.
Emma: That sounds good. How much more should I pay?
Clerk: Two yuan. But you can also keep this one and use it for small notes.
Emma: No, thanks. I'd rather have one big notebook this term.
Clerk: All right. Would you like a plastic cover as well?
Emma: Yes, that will protect it. How much is that?
Clerk: Three yuan, but it is free today because you are a student.
**Q1**
Why does Emma want to exchange the notebook?
- A. Because the cover of it was broken.
- B. Because she chose the wrong size.
- C. Because she did not want to pay two yuan more.
- D. Because the shop had sold out all the blue ones.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What does the clerk say about the plastic cover?
- A. It is free today because she is a student.
- B. It costs five yuan.
- C. It has just sold out.
- D. It must be bought with a smaller notebook.
**Answer** A

## B-03 去图书馆的路线
**Dialogue**
Tom: Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the City Library?
Woman: Of course. Go straight along this road and keep going.
Tom: Should I turn left at the traffic lights?
Woman: Yes, but turn left at the second set of lights, not the first one.
Tom: I see. And then what should I do?
Woman: You'll see a small park on your right. The library is the white building across it.
Tom: Is there a bus stop nearby? I don't want to be late for my lesson.
Woman: Yes, there's a stop at the corner. Buses number 3 and 12 stop there.
Tom: Which one should I take?
Woman: Take number 12. It goes to the library square every ten minutes.
Tom: Can I walk there instead?
Woman: About fifteen minutes on foot, but I wouldn't advise it in the rain.
**Q1**
Where should Tom turn left?
- A. At the first set of traffic lights.
- B. At the second set of traffic lights.
- C. At the bus stop on the corner.
- D. At the entrance of the small park.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
Which bus goes to the library square every ten minutes?
- A. Number 3.
- B. Number 8.
- C. Number 11.
- D. Number 12.
**Answer** D

## B-04 校运会800米
**Dialogue**
Peter: Lucy, are you going to run the 800 metres in the school sports meeting?
Lucy: I am, but I'm not sure. My leg still hurts from the training.
Peter: How far do you run each day now?
Lucy: About three kilometres, but I train after dinner, and my leg aches in the morning.
Peter: Maybe you should run earlier, before breakfast.
Lucy: My mother says the air is cleaner at that time, too.
Peter: Our coach told me to stop the night training a week before a race. Have you asked him about your leg?
Lucy: I did this morning. He said I should see the school doctor.
Peter: Then go this afternoon. The sports meeting is only three weeks away.
Lucy: All right. If the doctor says I need rest, I'll run the 400 metres instead.
Peter: Better to finish the season safely than to run with a bad leg.
**Q1**
Why does Lucy's leg ache in the morning?
- A. Because she runs three kilometres every morning.
- B. Because she trains after dinner every day.
- C. Because the air in the early morning is too cold.
- D. Because she has been ill for several days.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What does Lucy say she will do if the doctor tells her to rest?
- A. She will run the 800 metres and take medicine.
- B. She will give up all the races this term.
- C. She will run the 400 metres instead.
- D. She will train twice as hard every morning.
**Answer** C


## B-05 自做月饼
**Dialogue**
Mia: Dad, what are we going to do for the Mid-Autumn Festival this year?
Dad: I was thinking of buying a box of mooncakes from the shop near the station.
Mia: But Grandma always says those cakes are too sweet. Last year she only ate one.
Dad: That's true. What does she suggest instead?
Mia: She wants us to make them at home together. She has a good recipe.
Dad: Making them at home? I've never done that before.
Mia: Neither have I, but Grandma says it is easy. We need eggs, sugar, milk and something for the middle.
Dad: That sounds difficult. How long will it take, and can my sister join us?
Mia: About three hours. We can start in the afternoon, and your sister is welcome.
Dad: Then we'll start early. But we only have one big bowl at home.
Mia: Don't worry. We can borrow a big bowl from Uncle Wang on Saturday. He has six in his kitchen.
Dad: Then let's do it. A shop box is nice, but home-made ones taste better.
**Q1**
Why doesn't Mia want to buy mooncakes from the shop?
- A. Because her father does not like mooncakes.
- B. Because that shop sells cakes only in winter.
- C. Because the cakes are sold out every year.
- D. Because her grandmother thinks the shop cakes are too sweet.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
What will Mia and her father borrow from Uncle Wang?
- A. A big bowl.
- B. A box for the cakes.
- C. A wooden spoon.
- D. A pair of gloves.
**Answer** A

## B-06 周六去公园拍照
**Dialogue**
Chris: Nina, why do you always carry that camera to the park?
Nina: Because the light there is best in the late afternoon. I take pictures of the old trees.
Chris: Don't you think a phone is good enough for that?
Nina: I tried that last year, but most of my pictures were too dark.
Chris: How did you learn to use a real camera?
Nina: My aunt taught me. She has been a photographer for ten years.
Chris: What's the best picture you have taken so far?
Nina: One of the birds in a tree. I waited for nearly an hour in the cold.
Chris: It sounds exciting, but doesn't that take a lot of time?
Nina: It does, but I only go there on Saturdays, so it doesn't affect my study.
Chris: Maybe I can come with you next time and learn from you.
Nina: Sure. Bring a notebook. I will show you how to use the light first.
**Q1**
Why were most of Nina's phone pictures unsatisfactory last year?
- A. Because her phone often ran out of power.
- B. Because her aunt did not let her use it.
- C. Because most of them were too dark.
- D. Because the birds flew away from her.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
What will Chris bring when he goes to the park with Nina?
- A. A notebook.
- B. A pair of gloves.
- C. A camera of his own.
- D. Some bread for lunch.
**Answer** A

## B-07 眼睛不舒服
**Dialogue**
Kate: Daniel, why are you rubbing your eyes all the time?
Daniel: They hurt after a long evening of study.
Kate: How long do you usually read before bed?
Daniel: About two hours, with my phone right beside the book.
Kate: That light is too close. Reading on a screen like that is bad for your eyes.
Daniel: I know, but I always think I can finish the last chapter first.
Kate: You need to see a doctor. My mother used to work in a hospital.
Daniel: Really? She knows an eye doctor, doesn't she?
Kate: Yes, she does. You can go to see him with her on Sunday.
Daniel: That's kind of you. But what about the homework? It's really heavy.
Kate: Tell your teacher. My sister once asked for less homework and got a lighter schedule.
Daniel: All right. I'll talk to her this week, and then see the doctor on Sunday.
**Q1**
What does Kate suggest Daniel do about his eyes?
- A. He should finish his homework earlier in the evening.
- B. He should see an eye doctor with Kate's mother on Sunday.
- C. He should buy a new phone for reading at night.
- D. He should use a brighter lamp at his desk.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What will Daniel probably do this week?
- A. He will ask Kate to do his homework for him.
- B. He will read with a brighter lamp at his desk.
- C. He will talk to his teacher about the heavy homework.
- D. He will ask Kate's mother to check his eyes on Friday.
**Answer** C

## B-08 环保活动改期
**Dialogue**
Jason: Amy, are you going to the school clean-up activity this Saturday?
Amy: I plan to, but I heard the plan has changed again.
Jason: What happened? Mr. Green said we would clean the park near the river.
Amy: Yes, but the weather is too windy, so he moved it to next Wednesday.
Jason: Then our poster about recycling is ready too early. What should we do with it?
Amy: We could put it up in the school hall instead. Students will see it every day.
Jason: That's a good idea. But will anyone stop and read it?
Amy: We can offer a small gift — a paper bag that we make ourselves.
Jason: I like that. People usually take posters home, but they keep useful things.
Amy: Let's make twenty of them before Wednesday. I can draw the animal pictures.
Jason: I'll cut the paper and write the words. My handwriting is better than yours.
Amy: That's not true, but I'll let you think that way. See you on Monday then.
**Q1**
Why has the school clean-up activity been moved to Wednesday?
- A. Because most students have classes on Saturday.
- B. Because Mr. Green is ill on Saturday.
- C. Because the park near the river is closed.
- D. Because the weather is too windy.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
How will Amy and Jason encourage students to read the poster?
- A. They will ask Mr. Green to give a talk in the hall.
- B. They will put up a second poster at the school gate.
- C. They will ask other classes to vote for the best poster.
- D. They will give away paper bags that they make themselves.
**Answer** D
