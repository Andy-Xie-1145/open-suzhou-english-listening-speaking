## A-01 问询音乐教室位置
**Dialogue**
Girl: Excuse me, where is the music room?
Boy: It's on the third floor, beside the art room.
Girl: Thank you. Is the library on the same floor?
Boy: No, it's on the first floor, near the school gate.
**Question**
Where is the music room?
- A. On the first floor.
- B. On the third floor.
- C. Beside the school gate.
- D. Near the library.
**Answer** B

## A-02 挑选衬衫尺码

Girl: That's OK. I'll take L, please.
**Question**

- A. A blue shirt of size S.
- B. A blue shirt of size M.

**Answer** C
