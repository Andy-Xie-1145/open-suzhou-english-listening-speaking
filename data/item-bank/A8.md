> A8 社区生活与服务（15 题）

## A8-01 顺带买两袋面包
**Dialogue**
Shop assistant: Good morning. Is that all for the washing powder?
Boy: Yes. Is the milk on sale today?
Shop assistant: No. The milk is eight yuan, and the bread is three.
Boy: I'll take two loaves then. How much is that?
Shop assistant: Six yuan for two, so fourteen yuan in all.
**Question**
How much will the boy pay for the milk and the bread?
- A. Six yuan.
- B. Eleven yuan.
- C. Seventeen yuan.
- D. Fourteen yuan.
**Answer** D

## A8-02 裤腿改短三厘米
**Dialogue**
Girl: Good afternoon. Can you shorten these trousers?
Ms. Zhou: Of course. How much shorter do you want them?
Girl: Three centimetres. My cousin's wedding is on Friday.
Ms. Zhou: Come on Thursday afternoon, and bring white shoes.
Girl: Both? My shoes are white too.
Ms. Zhou: Yes. They'll be ready at four.
**Question**
When will the girl get the trousers?
- A. On Thursday morning.
- B. On Thursday at four in the afternoon.
- C. On Friday at four in the afternoon.
- D. On Friday morning.
**Answer** B

## A8-03 请邻居代喂狗
**Dialogue**
Mrs. Sun: Mr. Xu, I'm away for two days. Can you feed my dog?
Mr. Xu: Of course. How often does he eat?
Mrs. Sun: Twice a day, seven and six. Never at noon.
Mr. Xu: Can I take him out too?
Mrs. Sun: Only round the block. The park is too far.
**Question**
What will Mr. Xu do for Mrs. Sun's dog?
- A. Feed him three times a day and walk him in the park.
- B. Feed him once a day and walk him round the block.
- C. Feed him twice a day and walk him round the block.
- D. Feed him at noon and take him to the park.
**Answer** C

## A8-04 改看六点那场
**Dialogue**
Boy: Two tickets for the seven o'clock show, please.
Clerk: Sorry, that show is full. We still have the two and the six o'clock show.
Boy: Six o'clock, then. How long does the film last?
Clerk: Two hours and thirty minutes, I'm afraid.
Boy: Good. I can still catch the nine o'clock bus home.
**Question**
At what time will the film end?
- A. At half past eight o'clock.
- B. At eight o'clock.
- C. At half past nine o'clock.
- D. At nine o'clock.
**Answer** A

## A8-05 约好下午一点半
**Dialogue**
Guide: Welcome to the zoo. The elephants are fed at half past ten.
Mr. Li: My daughter wants the pandas. When do they eat?
Guide: At nine and at two. The keeper is off today.
Mr. Li: Then we'll come after lunch, at one thirty.
Guide: Right. Meet me at one thirty.
**Question**
When will Mr. Li meet the guide?
- A. At half past ten in the morning.
- B. At half past one in the afternoon.
- C. At two o'clock in the afternoon.
- D. At nine o'clock in the morning.
**Answer** B

## A8-06 钢琴课练到几点
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Hu, may I begin with the easy piece today?
Mr. Hu: No. Finish the hard one first, for twenty minutes, not ten.
Girl: Then how long will the whole lesson be?
Mr. Hu: Forty minutes. We start at half past three and stop at ten past four.
**Question**
At what time will the lesson end?
- A. At ten past three.
- B. At four o'clock.
- C. At half past four.
- D. At ten past four.
**Answer** D

## A8-07 带猫去打针
**Dialogue**
Vet: Is this your cat?
Owner: Yes. She has not eaten since Sunday.
Vet: Then she needs a check-up first. Come at half past two.
Owner: Can I come in the morning? I work then.
Vet: No mornings. The check-up is ten minutes, and the injection takes twenty minutes.
**Question**
How long will the injection take?
- A. Forty minutes.
- B. Half an hour.
- C. Twenty minutes.
- D. Ten minutes.
**Answer** C

## A8-08 住两晚多少钱
**Dialogue**
Receptionist: Good evening. Do you have a booking?
Mr. Wei: Yes, one single room for two nights, Monday to Wednesday.
Receptionist: Fifty yuan a night, with breakfast. Check-out is at eleven.
Mr. Wei: Can I eat there on Monday evening?
Receptionist: The kitchen stops at eight, but tea is served all day.
**Question**
How much will Mr. Wei pay for the room in all?
- A. One hundred yuan.
- B. Fifty yuan.
- C. One hundred and fifty yuan.
- D. Two hundred yuan.
**Answer** A

## A8-09 自行车链条掉了
**Dialogue**
Repair man: What's wrong with the bike?
Boy: The front wheel slows down. My father says the brakes are fine.
Repair man: It's the chain. It came off on Tuesday.
Boy: I need it for the art show on Saturday.
Repair man: Bring it in before nine on Thursday. I'll finish it by noon.
**Question**
When will the boy get his bike back?
- A. Before nine on Thursday.
- B. At noon on Saturday.
- C. At noon on Thursday.
- D. Before nine on Saturday.
**Answer** C

## A8-10 订小号生日蛋糕
**Dialogue**
Baker: What can I do for you?
Ms. Qian: A cake for my mother's birthday on Sunday.
Baker: The small one is sixty yuan. The big one is a hundred and twenty.
Ms. Qian: The small one, please. How many candles do I need?
Baker: Twenty candles, one for each year.
**Question**
How much will Ms. Qian pay for the cake?
- A. A hundred and twenty yuan.
- B. Eighty yuan.
- C. A hundred and forty yuan.
- D. Sixty yuan.
**Answer** D

## A8-11 先拿五张十元
**Dialogue**
Cashier: Good morning. How can I help you?
Mr. Gao: I need ten ten-yuan notes for the class party.
Cashier: We have little cash this afternoon. Come back tomorrow.
Mr. Gao: Then give me five notes now and the rest on Monday.
Cashier: All right. Five notes now, five on Monday morning.
**Question**
How much money will Mr. Gao get this afternoon?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Fifty yuan.
- C. A hundred yuan.
- D. Two hundred yuan.
**Answer** B

## A8-12 护照照要白衬衣
**Dialogue**
Photographer: What kind of photos do you need?
Girl: Passport photos. I have a blue shirt, but they ask for white ones.
Photographer: We don't sell shirts. We're closed on Fridays, not Saturdays.
Girl: What time do you open on Saturday?
Photographer: At nine. Eight photos take twenty minutes.
**Question**
What should the girl do?
- A. Come on Saturday and bring a white shirt.
- B. Come on Friday and bring a white shirt.
- C. Come on Saturday and buy a white shirt.
- D. Come on Friday and buy a white shirt.
**Answer** A

## A8-13 还剩两节驾驶课
**Dialogue**
Mr. Ren: Ready for lesson five of six?
Boy: Yes. May we drive on the highway today?
Mr. Ren: Not until you pass the road test. Practise parallel parking.
Boy: How many lessons do I still need?
Mr. Ren: Two, not three. The next one is at nine on Wednesday.
**Question**
How many lessons does the boy still need?
- A. Three.
- B. Six.
- C. Two.
- D. Five.
**Answer** C

## A8-14 搬家上五楼
**Dialogue**
Mover: Where are you moving to, Ms. Yan?
Ms. Yan: A flat on the fifth floor, with six boxes and a fridge.
Mover: We don't move fridges on Sunday. Saturday is free.
Ms. Yan: Does that price include the stairs?
Mover: The third floor is free. Twenty yuan for each floor above it.
**Question**
How much will Ms. Yan pay for the stairs?
- A. Twenty yuan.
- B. Forty yuan.
- C. Sixty yuan.
- D. Nothing.
**Answer** B

## A8-15 两条船押金多少
**Dialogue**
Visitor: Two boats, please. How long can we keep them?
Mr. Fang: Half an hour. That is forty yuan for the two, plus a ten-yuan deposit for each.
Visitor: My friend can't swim. Is there a life jacket?
Mr. Fang: Yes, one is free with each boat. We stop at five.
**Question**
How much will the visitor pay in all?
- A. Sixty yuan.
- B. Fifty yuan.
- C. Forty yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** A
