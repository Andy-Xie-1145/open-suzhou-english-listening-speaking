## B2-01 音乐社招新咨询
**Dialogue**
Tom: Good morning, Ms. Green. I want to join the school music group.
Ms. Green: Of course. We rehearse on Wednesday afternoons, from four to five.
Tom: I'm free then. Where do we meet?
Ms. Green: We meet in the music room on the second floor.
Tom: Thanks a lot, Ms. Green.
Ms. Green: You are welcome. Just come and give your name to me before the rehearsal.
**Q1**
When and where does the music group have its rehearsal?
- A. On Wednesday morning, in the music room.
- B. On Wednesday afternoon, in the music room.
- C. On Friday afternoon, in the art room.
- D. On Wednesday afternoon, on the playground.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
How long does the rehearsal last?
- A. It lasts about two hours.
- B. It lasts about three hours.
- C. It lasts about one hour.
- D. It lasts about half an hour.
**Answer** C

## B2-02 元旦联欢会准备窗花
**Dialogue**
Ms. Brown: Lucy, our New Year party is on Friday. We still need to buy some paper cuts for the windows.
Lucy: I can buy them on Thursday afternoon after school.
Ms. Brown: Good. Please buy six of them, one for each window on our floor.
Lucy: Six. I'll put them up on Friday morning before class.
Ms. Brown: Perfect, thank you.
**Q1**
When will Lucy put up the paper cuts?
- A. On Friday morning, before class.
- B. On Thursday afternoon, after school.
- C. On Friday afternoon, after the party.
- D. On Thursday morning, before school.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
How many paper cuts will Lucy buy for the windows?
- A. Six paper cuts.
- B. Four paper cuts.
- C. Eight paper cuts.
- D. Twelve paper cuts.
**Answer** A

## B2-03 班级值日分工
**Dialogue**
Peter: Ms. Miller, what should I do for today's cleaning duty?
Ms. Miller: You usually clean the windows, but Tom is free this afternoon, so he can do it.
Peter: Then can I clean the blackboard? Who takes out the rubbish?
Ms. Miller: Yes, but please finish it before four o'clock. Sam takes out the rubbish.
Peter: All right, thank you.
**Q1**
What will Peter do today?
- A. Clean the windows this afternoon.
- B. Take out the rubbish before four o'clock.
- C. Clean the blackboard after four o'clock.
- D. Clean the blackboard before four o'clock.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
Who will take out the rubbish?
- A. Tom.
- B. Sam.
- C. Peter.
- D. Jack.
**Answer** B

## B2-04 缺勤后领演讲赛选题
**Dialogue**
Amy: Ms. Clark, it's Monday morning. I missed the club meeting on Friday. I had a fever.
Ms. Clark: That's all right. The club chose a topic for the English speech contest.
Amy: What is the topic? I need to start preparing.
Ms. Clark: "My school life." Please write it down and bring it to the first lesson on Monday.
Amy: Thank you very much.
**Q1**
What is Amy going to do with the topic?
- A. She will bring it to the first lesson on Monday.
- B. She will give it to the club leader on Monday.
- C. She will practise it in the club meeting on Friday.
- D. She will write a speech about it before Friday.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
What is the topic of the English speech contest?
- A. "My home town."
- B. "My favourite food."
- C. "My school life."
- D. "My best friend."
**Answer** C

## B2-05 校运会报名缴费
**Dialogue**
Mr. Nelson: Ben, the sports meeting is on Wednesday. Which events will you enter?
Ben: The long jump and the four-hundred-metre race.
Mr. Nelson: Please fill in this form and pay ten yuan for each event.
Ben: Ten yuan each, twenty yuan in all. Who takes the money?
Mr. Nelson: Give it to Ms. Wright in the school office tomorrow morning.
**Q1**
Where and when should Ben pay for his events?
- A. To Mr. Nelson on Wednesday morning.
- B. To Ms. Wright in the school office tomorrow morning.
- C. To Ms. Wright in the school office tomorrow afternoon.
- D. To Ms. Wright at the sports meeting on Wednesday.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
How much will Ben pay in all?
- A. Ten yuan.
- B. Thirty yuan.
- C. Forty yuan.
- D. Twenty yuan.
**Answer** D

## B2-06 班级墙报分工
**Dialogue**
Kate: Mr. Turner, what about our class wall newspaper?
Mr. Turner: Please draw a picture about the sports meeting. David will write the article.
Kate: All right. How big should the paper be?
Mr. Turner: Two large sheets, about one metre wide.
Kate: When should we put it up?
Mr. Turner: On Tuesday morning, before the first lesson. Put it on the back wall.
**Q1**
What will Kate do for the class wall newspaper?
- A. She will write the article about the sports meeting.
- B. She will cut the paper into small pieces.
- C. She will draw a picture about the sports meeting.
- D. She will put the newspaper on the front wall.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
When will they put up the class wall newspaper?
- A. On Monday morning, before the first lesson.
- B. On Wednesday morning, before the first lesson.
- C. On Tuesday afternoon, after the last lesson.
- D. On Tuesday morning, before the first lesson.
**Answer** D
