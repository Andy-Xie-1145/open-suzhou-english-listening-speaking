## B5-01 邮局寄一个生日包裹
**Dialogue**
Girl: Good morning. I want to send this box to my grandmother in Nanjing.
Ms. Zhou: Certainly. Did you pack it yourself?
Girl: Yes. Her birthday is on Saturday, so it should arrive by then.
Ms. Zhou: Let me weigh it for you. The van to Nanjing leaves at eight on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and each trip takes three days on the road.
Girl: It is already nine now, so this morning's van has gone.
Ms. Zhou: I'm afraid so. Post the box before ten tomorrow and it will go on Wednesday.
Girl: Wednesday plus three days is Saturday. That will just arrive in time.
Ms. Zhou: Good. Now the price. A box of two kilos or less costs eight yuan, and every kilo after that costs four yuan.
Girl: Mine is three kilos, so that is eight yuan plus four.
Ms. Zhou: Twelve yuan. You will also need this strong outer box, which is two yuan more.
Girl: Here you are.
**Q1**
How much will the girl pay for the parcel in all?
- A. Twelve yuan.
- B. Fourteen yuan.
- C. Sixteen yuan.
- D. Ten yuan.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
When will the box reach her grandmother?
- A. On Wednesday morning, when the van leaves.
- B. On Thursday morning, one day after the van leaves.
- C. On Friday morning, two days after the van leaves.
- D. On Saturday morning, three days after the van leaves.
**Answer** D

## B5-02 期中试卷与订正本
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Clark, may I have my English paper back?
Ms. Clark: Here it is. You got eighty-six, but you left the last three questions blank.
Boy: I ran out of time in the reading part.
Ms. Clark: Then please correct them in your exercise book.
Boy: Shall I copy the whole text?
Ms. Clark: No, only the sentences you left blank, each one time, with the right answer beside it.
Boy: Shall I bring the exercise book back tomorrow?
Ms. Clark: Not tomorrow. I collect the exercise books on Friday afternoon, and then the students take them home.
Boy: Then my mother will see it on Saturday morning?
Ms. Clark: Not until this evening, once I have handed them all back. She should sign page forty and give the book back to me on Monday morning.
Boy: Thank you. I'll tell her which page to sign.
**Q1**
What does Ms. Clark ask the boy to do about the three questions he left blank?
- A. Copy the whole reading text three times for her.
- B. Write the three right answers on a separate sheet.
- C. Copy each of those sentences once with the right answer.
- D. Ask a classmate to correct the work before Friday.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
The boy first thought his mother would see it on Saturday. When will she actually see it?
- A. On Friday evening, before she gives it back on Monday morning.
- B. On Saturday morning, because she collects them on Friday.
- C. On Friday afternoon, while Ms. Clark is collecting the books.
- D. On Monday morning, when she gives it back to Ms. Clark.
**Answer** A

## B5-03 周末大扫除
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mum, shall we do the spring cleaning on Saturday morning?
Mum: Yes. I can start at nine, but I must catch the bus at one, because my afternoon job starts at half past one.
Girl: Then we have four hours for five things: the living room, the kitchen, the bathroom and the two balconies.
Mum: The living room takes me two hours and the bathroom one, so I shall do those two first.
Girl: And what about me?
Mum: You can do the kitchen and the two balconies. The kitchen takes an hour, and so does each balcony.
Girl: That is three hours for me. Three hours from nine is twelve.
Mum: Then do the two balconies first and leave the kitchen for Sunday morning.
Girl: All right. Then what shall we have for lunch?
Mum: There are eggs and noodles in the kitchen, so I shall start them at twelve.
Girl: That's a good idea. Thank you, Mum.
**Q1**
What will the girl clean on Saturday morning?
- A. The two balconies only.
- B. The living room and the kitchen.
- C. The kitchen and the bathroom.
- D. The bathroom and one balcony.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
At what time will the girl stop cleaning on Saturday morning?
- A. At ten o'clock.
- B. At half past ten o'clock.
- C. At eleven o'clock.
- D. At twelve o'clock.
**Answer** C

## B5-04 手机屏幕坏了
**Dialogue**
Boy: Mr. Stone, my phone fell to the floor on Monday and the screen is broken.
Mr. Stone: The glass is cracked. Do you still have the receipt?
Boy: Here it is. Can you fix it before Friday?
Mr. Stone: We do not keep that screen in the shop. The new ones come on Friday morning, so Friday is the first day I can do it.
Boy: Then I'll come on Friday. How much will it cost?
Mr. Stone: A new screen costs one hundred and eighty yuan, and we add twenty yuan for the work. But if you wait here while we do it, we take ten yuan off.
Boy: All right. What time do you open on Friday?
Mr. Stone: At nine. We are busiest before ten, so please come at half past ten.
Boy: I'll come at half past ten then. How long will the work take?
Mr. Stone: It takes one hour, so please stay here with the phone.
Boy: Then I'd better wait. Please keep my old screen.
Mr. Stone: We keep old screens for three months.
**Q1**
How much will the boy pay for the screen in all?
- A. Two hundred and ten yuan.
- B. Two hundred yuan.
- C. One hundred and eighty yuan.
- D. One hundred and ninety yuan.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
At what time will the boy get his phone back on Friday?
- A. At eleven o'clock.
- B. At half past eleven o'clock.
- C. At twelve o'clock.
- D. At half past ten o'clock.
**Answer** B

## B5-05 春游集合
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Yang, what time do we start for the spring outing on Saturday?
Ms. Yang: Our bus to the South Park leaves at ten past eight, so please come twenty minutes earlier than that.
Boy: Twenty minutes earlier? Where exactly?
Ms. Yang: At the east gate. The west gate is locked in the morning, so we always use the east one.
Boy: What shall we do when we get there?
Ms. Yang: We stay two hours in the park, and the ride takes forty minutes each way.
Boy: Then we must be back before lunch at twelve.
Ms. Yang: Yes. Leave the park at ten to eleven and you will be back in time.
Boy: Shall I bring a water bottle?
Ms. Yang: Of course, and a hat as well. The sun is strong, and there is no shop inside the park.
**Q1**
Where and when will the class meet on Saturday morning?
- A. At the east gate at ten to eight.
- B. At the school hall at ten past eight.
- C. At the west gate at ten to eight.
- D. At the bus stop at half past eight.
**Answer** A
**Q2**
At what time will the class get back to school?
- A. At ten to eleven.
- B. At a quarter past eleven.
- C. At twelve o'clock.
- D. At half past eleven.
**Answer** D

## B5-06 失物招领处的伞
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Zhang, I think I left my umbrella at the sports meeting on Wednesday.
Ms. Zhang: Let me look in the lost property box. We have four things from Wednesday: a black umbrella, a dark green one, a blue schoolbag and a pair of white gloves.
Girl: Mine is dark green.
Ms. Zhang: Then we have found it. The handle is wood, not metal, and the name on it says Lily Chen.
Girl: That's my name! So somebody handed it in for me.
Ms. Zhang: She also wrote her own name in our book. Would you like me to put up a notice?
Girl: No need. Could you tell our monitor for me?
Ms. Zhang: Our monitor puts up the lost property list on Friday morning, and she keeps a copy for the head teacher.
Girl: Thank you. Then my umbrella can stay here a little longer.
Ms. Zhang: We keep lost things for one month. After that, anything unclaimed goes to the school shop on the last Friday of the month.
**Q1**
Which of these things is the girl's umbrella?
- A. The black one with a metal handle.
- B. The dark green one with a wooden handle.
- C. The black one with a wooden handle.
- D. The dark green one with a metal handle.
**Answer** B
**Q2**
What will happen to the umbrella if nobody takes it away?
- A. It will be given to the class monitor.
- B. It will be sent to the head teacher.
- C. It will be sold in the school shop.
- D. It will stay in the office for ever.
**Answer** C

## B5-07 买两张去南京的火车票
**Dialogue**
Boy: Hello, I'd like two tickets to Nanjing for Saturday morning.
Clerk: Certainly. Are you both children? An adult ticket is seventy yuan, and a ticket for a child under fourteen is fifty yuan.
Boy: I am fifteen and my cousin is eleven. We want to get there for my grandmother's birthday.
Clerk: Then I can sell you one adult ticket and one child ticket. The train leaves at nine from Platform 3.
Boy: How long does the journey take?
Clerk: Two hours and a half, and you do not have to change trains. Now please give me both student cards.
Boy: Mine is for senior high, so do I need it?
Clerk: At fifteen you pay as an adult, so only your cousin's card is needed.
Boy: Here it is. My mother will meet us at the station in Nanjing.
**Q1**
How much will the boy pay for the two tickets?
- A. One hundred and forty yuan.
- B. One hundred and ten yuan.
- C. Ninety yuan in total.
- D. One hundred and twenty yuan.
**Answer** D
**Q2**
At what time will the train arrive in Nanjing?
- A. At half past eleven o'clock.
- B. At ten o'clock in the morning.
- C. At half past ten o'clock.
- D. At twelve o'clock in the morning.
**Answer** A

## B5-08 周末去博物馆看恐龙化石
**Dialogue**
Boy: Shall we go to the museum on Saturday morning?
Girl: Yes. The museum opens at nine, but the new dinosaur room only opens at ten.
Boy: Then what time shall we meet?
Girl: Let's meet at the school gate at half past nine. The ride is twenty-five minutes each way, and the buses come every fifteen minutes.
Boy: Good, then we will see the dinosaur room first.
Girl: The museum closes at eleven on Saturdays, so we have two hours.
Boy: Two hours is enough for me. I only want to see the dinosaur room.
Girl: And I want to buy a small model in the shop, but it closes at half past ten on Saturdays.
Boy: Then we should go to the shop after the dinosaur room, not before.
Girl: That's a good plan. See you at the gate.
**Q1**
At what time will the boy and the girl meet at the school gate?
- A. At nine o'clock on Saturday.
- B. At ten o'clock on Saturday.
- C. At half past nine o'clock.
- D. At half past ten o'clock.
**Answer** C
**Q2**
How long will they spend on the bus altogether?
- A. Fifteen minutes.
- B. Fifty minutes.
- C. Twenty-five minutes.
- D. One hour.
**Answer** B
