## A9-01 剪短一点就好
**Dialogue**
Boy: Excuse me, I'd like a haircut. My hair is too long.
Barber: How much shall I take off? A little, or a lot?
Boy: Only a little. A short cut suits my face.
Barber: Then I'll cut it to your shoulder, not above the ear.
Boy: Good. Please leave it long at the front.
**Question**
How will the barber cut the boy's hair?
- A. To about his shoulder, not above the ear.
- B. Above the ear, not to the shoulder.
- C. Very short, close to the skin.
- D. Short at the front and long at the back.
**Answer** A

## A9-02 剪发加染发多少钱
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mum, how much is a haircut at the salon?
Mother: The sign says forty yuan for a cut and wash.
Girl: I want to dye it black too. How much is that?
Mother: Dyeing is sixty yuan, but students get ten yuan off.
Girl: Forty plus sixty, less ten. I'll bring my card.
**Question**
How much will the girl pay for the cut, wash and dyeing?
- A. One hundred yuan.
- B. One hundred and ten yuan.
- C. Sixty yuan.
- D. Ninety yuan.
**Answer** D

## A9-03 预约周六烫发
**Dialogue**
Mother: I want to book a perm for my daughter on Saturday.
Stylist: Saturday is busy. I can take you at half past one.
Mother: Her class ends at one o'clock. Is that enough time?
Stylist: A perm takes two hours, so you'd finish at half past three.
**Question**
When will they finish the perm?
- A. At half past one.
- B. At half past two.
- C. At half past three.
- D. At one o'clock.
**Answer** C

## A9-04 丢了银行卡
**Dialogue**
Girl: My bank card is gone. I lost it this morning.
Clerk: When did you use it? We'll stop it now.
Girl: Yesterday evening, near my school.
Clerk: Fill in this form. Your money is safe. We'll send a new card.
Girl: When will the new card reach me?
Clerk: Five working days, not three.
**Question**
How long will the new card take to reach the girl?
- A. Three working days.
- B. Five working days.
- C. Five weeks.
- D. Three months.
**Answer** B

## A9-05 给外地亲戚转账
**Dialogue**
Girl: Can I send money to my cousin in another city?
Clerk: Yes. The charge is two yuan for each transfer.
Girl: Then I had better send it all in one go tomorrow.
Clerk: Do that. After three o'clock the line is too long.
**Question**
What will the girl do?
- A. Send the money in two smaller transfers.
- B. Wait until after three o'clock.
- C. Send the money the day after tomorrow.
- D. Send the whole amount tomorrow in one transfer.
**Answer** D

## A9-06 账户里还剩多少
**Dialogue**
Boy: How much money is left in my account?
Clerk: You had three hundred yuan. You took out eighty this morning.
Boy: So there are two hundred and twenty yuan, right?
Clerk: Right, but twenty yuan of it is in another account.
**Question**
How much money is left in this account?
- A. Two hundred yuan.
- B. Two hundred and twenty yuan.
- C. Two hundred and forty yuan.
- D. Two hundred and eighty yuan.
**Answer** A

## A9-07 鸡肉卖完了改点别的
**Dialogue**
Waiter: Good evening. Are you ready to order?
Boy: Yes, please. Two bowls of beef noodles and a dish of chicken.
Waiter: We have no chicken today. We have fried rice and beef noodles.
Boy: Then one bowl of noodles and one fried rice, please.
Waiter: Anything to drink? Orange juice or lemon tea.
**Question**
What will the boy order?
- A. Two bowls of beef noodles.
- B. One dish of chicken and one fried rice.
- C. One bowl of beef noodles and one fried rice.
- D. One bowl of noodles and one dish of chicken.
**Answer** C

## A9-08 周五订一张四人桌
**Dialogue**
Boy: I'd like to book a table for four on Friday.
Waiter: Friday is fully booked at seven. What time would suit you?
Boy: Half past six, please. We must leave by half past eight.
Waiter: Then you'll have two hours. Would you like the window?
Boy: Yes, but not the one near the kitchen door.
**Question**
How long will they stay in the restaurant?
- A. Ninety minutes.
- B. One hour.
- C. Two and a half hours.
- D. Two hours.
**Answer** D

## A9-15 装书桌要四十分钟
**Dialogue**
Boy: The desk I ordered arrived this morning.
Dad: Read the instructions first. It takes forty minutes, not an hour.
Boy: One bag has screws, one has boards. We need only one bag of screws.
Dad: Keep the other bag for the shelf. Finish before lunch.
**Question**
How long will it take them to put the desk together?
- A. An hour.
- B. Forty minutes.
- C. Twenty minutes.
- D. One hour and forty minutes.
**Answer** B
