> A6 健康与运动（15 题）

## A6-01 熬夜后该几点睡
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wu, I stayed up until twelve last night. I feel sleepy now.
Ms. Wu: Then how did you get to school?
Boy: My father drove me. I slept in the car on the way.
Ms. Wu: Go to bed at about ten, not at twelve. You left at six.
Boy: I see. Class begins at eight.
**Question**
What time should the boy go to bed?
- A. At about six o'clock.
- B. At about eight o'clock.
- C. At about ten o'clock.
- D. At about twelve o'clock.
**Answer** C

## A6-02 跑前热身十分钟
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Liu, can I run on the track right now?
Mr. Liu: Not yet. Do some warm-up exercises first.
Girl: How long should the warm-up last? My friend says three minutes.
Mr. Liu: Run slowly for five minutes, then five minutes of jumping. Ten minutes in all.
Girl: Then I'll run for twenty minutes and stop.
**Question**
How long should the girl warm up before running?
- A. Ten minutes.
- B. Three minutes.
- C. Five minutes.
- D. Twenty minutes.
**Answer** A

## A6-03 牛奶过期了
**Dialogue**
Boy: Hi, Ms. Chen. Is there any milk in your fridge?
Ms. Chen: Yes, but look at the date on the milk box. The orange juice is for the picnic.
Boy: The milk says the tenth, and today is the eighteenth.
Ms. Chen: Then you can't drink it. Buy a small box at the shop, not a big bottle.
**Question**
What will the boy do now?
- A. Drink the orange juice now.
- B. Buy a big bottle of milk at the shop.
- C. Drink the milk in Ms. Chen's fridge.
- D. Buy a small box of milk at the shop.
**Answer** D

## A6-04 扭伤膝盖要冰敷
**Dialogue**
Girl: Mr. Zhao, my knee hurts. I fell off my bike.
Mr. Zhao: Can you walk?
Girl: Slowly. I'd like to go to the gym and swim after school.
Mr. Zhao: No. The gym is closed today, so no swimming. Put ice on it for fifteen minutes and rest at home. See a doctor tomorrow if it still hurts.
**Question**
What will the girl do this evening?
- A. Go to the gym after school.
- B. Use ice and rest at home.
- C. Go swimming after school.
- D. See a doctor tomorrow.
**Answer** B

## A6-05 接力赛训练安排
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Wang, where and when shall we train for the relay race?
Ms. Wang: The track is closed for repairs all week. In the gym tomorrow at four.
Boy: What about Wednesday morning? I have no classes then.
Ms. Wang: Good. Meet in the gym at seven in the morning.
Boy: Four tomorrow, then seven on Wednesday. Understood.
**Question**
How will they train on Wednesday?
- A. In the gym at seven in the morning.
- B. On the track at four in the afternoon.
- C. In the gym at four in the afternoon.
- D. On the track at seven in the morning.
**Answer** A

## A6-06 体检先做哪一项
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Gao, what shall we do at the health check?
Ms. Gao: A blood test first, then an eye test, a tooth check and a height test.
Girl: Shall I eat my breakfast first?
Ms. Gao: No. Come at seven forty. We'll give you bread and an egg after the test.
Girl: All right. Thank you, Ms. Gao.
**Question**
Which test will the students do first?
- A. The eye test.
- B. The tooth check.
- C. The blood test.
- D. The height test.
**Answer** C

## A6-07 看手机后护眼
**Dialogue**
Girl: Hello, Ms. Yuan. My eyes hurt after I finish my homework.
Ms. Yuan: How long do you spend on your homework every evening?
Girl: About two hours. Half an hour of that is looking at my phone.
Ms. Yuan: Look away for a minute every twenty minutes, not every ten. Reading in the car is bad too.
**Question**
How often should the girl look away from her homework?
- A. Every ten minutes.
- B. Every twenty minutes.
- C. Every half an hour.
- D. Once every two hours.
**Answer** B

## A6-08 考前紧张睡不着
**Dialogue**
Boy: Ms. Yang, I'm so nervous about tomorrow's test that I can't sleep.
Ms. Yang: Don't drink coffee, and don't read in bed.
Boy: I asked my classmates about the test, but I'm still worried.
Ms. Yang: Write your worries on paper, then walk around the room for ten minutes.
Boy: Writing? I never thought of that.
**Question**
What will the boy do to help himself sleep?
- A. Read his book in bed.
- B. Drink a cup of coffee.
- C. Ask his classmates about the test.
- D. Write down his worries on paper.
**Answer** D

## A6-09 换牙刷挑软毛
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Shen, my toothbrush is nearly a year old. Should I buy a new one?
Ms. Shen: Yes. Change it every three months. Buy a soft one, not a hard one.
Girl: What about the handle?
Ms. Shen: A big handle is fine. Don't buy a small one.
Girl: OK. A soft brush with a big handle.
**Question**
What will the girl buy?
- A. A soft brush with a big handle.
- B. A hard brush with a big handle.
- C. A soft brush with a small handle.
- D. A hard brush with a small handle.
**Answer** A

## A6-10 早上没时间吃早饭
**Dialogue**
Boy: Hi, Mr. Guo. I often skip breakfast on school days.
Mr. Guo: When do you leave?
Boy: At seven ten. I sleep until six fifty, then hurry out.
Mr. Guo: Set the clock ten minutes earlier. Milk isn't breakfast, and an apple is only a snack. Have some bread and an egg.
**Question**
What will the boy have for breakfast from now on?
- A. An apple only.
- B. A glass of milk.
- C. Some bread and an egg.
- D. Nothing in the morning.
**Answer** C

## A6-11 发烧在家休息
**Dialogue**
Girl: Excuse me, Ms. Xu. I have a fever and a headache.
Ms. Xu: How long have you had the fever?
Girl: Since last night. My friends want to play basketball after school.
Ms. Xu: Then stay home and miss PE class. The school clinic is closed this afternoon, so take your medicine at home after breakfast and rest.
**Question**
What will the girl do this afternoon?
- A. Play basketball with her friends.
- B. Go to the PE class at school.
- C. Take the medicine at the school clinic.
- D. Stay at home and rest all day.

## A6-14 朋友不开心怎么办

## A6-15 四百米跑步节奏
**Dialogue**
Girl: Ms. Pan, in the 400-metre race, should we start fast?
Ms. Pan: No. Start slowly and run fast in the last hundred metres.
Girl: My friend starts fast and gets tired.
Ms. Pan: Practise on Monday and Wednesday. Not on Friday. You'll be tired from the sports meeting on Thursday morning.
Girl: I see. Slow first, then fast.
**Question**
When will they practise for the 400-metre race?
- A. On Thursday morning.
- B. On Friday afternoon.
- C. On Wednesday and Friday.
- D. On Monday and Wednesday.
**Answer** D
