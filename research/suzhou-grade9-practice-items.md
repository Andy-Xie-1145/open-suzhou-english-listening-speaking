# 一、听对话回答问题（10 小题 · 每题 1 分 · 共 10 分）

**结构**：10 段短对话，每段完成 1 道小题。
**格式设定**：屏幕显示题干 + 三个选项 A/B/C；播放 2 遍；听前 5 秒浏览。
**内容域**：问路、购物、时间、健康、购物清单、道歉、交通、邀约、教师评价、乘车

**1.** Woman: Excuse me, where is the reading room?
Man: It's on the second floor, next to the library.
> **Where is the reading room?**
> A. On the first floor.　B. On the second floor.　C. Next to the playground.
> **答案 B**

**2.** Girl: How much is this schoolbag?
Boy: It's ninety-nine yuan. The blue one over there is only sixty-five.
> **How much is the blue schoolbag?**
> A. 99 yuan.　B. 65 yuan.　C. 199 yuan.
> **答案 B**

**3.** Boy: What time does the film start?
Girl: It was supposed to start at seven, but it's already half past seven and the lights are still off.
> **Has the film started?**
> A. Yes, it has.　B. No, it hasn't.　C. Yes, ten minutes ago.
> **答案 B**

**4.** Teacher: You look tired, Jim. Are you feeling all right?
Jim: Not really. I have a bad headache.
Teacher: You should see the doctor after school.
> **What is wrong with Jim?**
> A. He has a headache.　B. He has a bad cold.　C. He hurt his leg.
> **答案 A**

**5.** Wife: I'm going to the supermarket. Do you need anything?
Husband: Yes, please. Get some milk and a loaf of bread.
> **What does the man want the woman to buy?**
> A. Some milk and bread.　B. Some rice and eggs.　C. Some orange juice.
> **答案 A**

**6.** Ben: I'm sorry, Kate. I broke your cup.
Kate: It's all right. Just please be more careful next time.
> **How does Kate react?**
> A. She asks Ben to buy a new cup.　B. She says it does not matter.　C. She is terribly angry.
> **答案 B**

**7.** Mum: How do you go to school every day?
Tom: I used to ride a bike, but now I take the bus because my new school is much farther away.
> **How does Tom go to school now?**
> A. By bike.　B. By bus.　C. On foot.
> **答案 B**

**8.** Ann: Would you like to join us for a picnic on Saturday?
Ben: I'd love to, but I'm afraid I'll be busy then.
Ann: How about Sunday morning?
Ben: That works perfectly for me.
> **When will the picnic take place?**
> A. On Saturday.　B. On Sunday morning.　C. It has not been decided.
> **答案 B**

**9.** Peter: How is your new English teacher getting on?
Teacher: Pretty well. At first I was afraid to speak in class, but she often asks us simple questions and never laughs when we make mistakes.
> **How does Peter feel about the new teacher now?**
> A. Pleased.　B. Worried.　C. Bored.
> **答案 A**

**10.** Girl: Excuse me, does this bus go to the Railway Station?
Driver: No, it goes to the Railway Station North. You want the number 15.
Girl: Thank you very much.
> **Which bus should the girl take?**
> A. This one.　B. Number 15.　C. Number 50.
> **答案 B**

# 二、听对话和短文答题（10 小题 · 每题 1 分 · 共 10 分）

**结构**：1 段长对话完成 2 小题 + 1 篇短文完成 3 小题 + 1 篇短文完成 5 小题。
**格式设定**：屏幕显示题干 + 三个选项 A/B/C；材料播放 2 遍。

## 材料 1 · 长对话（第 11–12 题）

Ann: Hi, Ben. Have you decided which club to join this term?
Ben: Not yet. I looked at the list just now.
Ann: What are you interested in?
Ben: I used to play the guitar, but now I spend most of my free time taking photos. The Photography Club sounds right.
Ann: It meets only on Wednesday afternoons, and I'm free then.
Ben: Then why don't we join it together?
Ann: Good idea. Wait — does it need anything special?
Ben: I'm afraid so. Members have to bring their own camera. Mine is quite old.
Ann: Mine is new. I can bring it when we meet.
Ben: That would be great. By the way, did you see the Science Club? They had a trip to the museum last term.
Ann: Yes, my cousin went on it. She said it was really interesting.

**11.** Which club has Ben decided to join?
A. The Guitar Club.　B. The Photography Club.　C. The Science Club.
**答案 B**

**12.** What problem do they mention about the Photography Club?
A. It is too expensive to join.　B. Members need to bring their own cameras.　C. It rarely has new members.
**答案 B**

## 材料 2 · 短文（第 13–15 题）

> Last spring, our school started a "Green Corner" project. Students collect used bottles and old paper at the school gate every Friday afternoon. The bottles are sold to a recycling centre, and the money is used to buy books for the school library. Last year the project collected 1,200 bottles and raised 480 yuan. This year more students have joined, and the school has already bought over sixty new books. Our maths teacher, Ms. Wang, says she hopes the project will continue long after she retires. "It teaches children that small actions can make a big difference," she said.

**13.** When do students collect the bottles?
A. Every Monday morning.　B. Every Friday afternoon.　C. Every Friday morning.
**答案 B**

**14.** How much money did the project raise last year?
A. 1,200 yuan.　B. 480 yuan.　C. 60 yuan.
**答案 B**

**15.** What does Ms. Wang think of the project?
A. It should stop when she retires.　B. It teaches children that small actions matter.　C. It wastes students' time.
**答案 B**

## 材料 3 · 短文（第 16–20 题）

> The city has more than three hundred small parks. Last year, students from a middle school in the city visited all of them in one week as part of a school project. They took photos, counted the trees and talked to the park keepers. Their report surprised everyone. Twenty of the parks had no drinking fountains, and forty-three had no lights after dark. The students then wrote to the city council and asked for help. Three months later, the council agreed to put in twelve fountains and ninety new lamps. A teacher, Ms. Chen, was pleased. "Students often think small problems are useless to report," she said, "but they wrote to the government, and the government answered." The school has now decided to publish the project in its school newspaper.

**16.** How many small parks are there in the city?
A. More than three hundred.　B. About thirty.　C. About three thousand.
**答案 A**

**17.** What did the students do during the project?
A. They cleaned the parks.　B. They collected information about the parks.　C. They planted trees.
**答案 B**

**18.** What surprised everyone in the report?
A. Many parks were too small.　B. Few people visited the parks.　C. Many parks lacked facilities.
**答案 C**

**19.** What did the city council agree to do?
A. To close some parks.　B. To build new parks.　C. To add fountains and lights.
**答案 C**

**20.** What has the school decided to do?
A. To publish the project in its school newspaper.　B. To stop the project.　C. To visit more parks next year.
**答案 A**

# 三、情景问答（4 组 · 每组 2 小题 · 每小题 1 分 · 共 8 分）

**格式设定**：屏幕上先给一段中文情景提示（10 秒可读完）→ 听到两个英文问题（各问两遍）→ 每题用英文回答 15 秒。

## 第 1 组（笔友问周末安排 · 考查 be going to / why）
**情景提示**：你的美国笔友 Peter 在信里问你周末的安排和原因。
1. What **are you going to do** this weekend?
2. **Why** do you like doing that?

## 第 2 组（书店买书 · 考查 want to / how much）
**情景提示**：你在书店，想买的书已经卖完了。你想知道有没有别的版本，还要问价格。
1. What **do you want to buy**?
2. How much **is** it?

## 第 3 组（同学缺勤 · 考查过去时与 be 动词）
**情景提示**：你的同学 Tom 昨天没来上学。老师让你去问清原因，并了解他今天的安排。
1. Why **was** Tom **absent** yesterday?
2. What **is** Tom **going to do** today?

## 第 4 组（机场送别 · 考查 how long / what time）
**情景提示**：你在机场送朋友，他要去上海，问你航班要飞多久、几点到。
1. How long **will the flight take**?
2. What time **will you arrive**?

> 覆盖点：计划（be going to）、原因（why）、价格（how much）、过去时 + be 动词、时长（how long）、时间（what time）——对应公开材料指出的失分点「人称、时态、单复数、语法不完整」。

# 四、话题简述（12 题 · 每题 5 分）

**形式**：屏幕上**仅显示一个英文标题**，无中文要点、无范文；90 秒内连续说**不少于 7 句**；不得出现真实姓名、校名。
**以下 12 个标题为官方《指引》附录四公布的话题原文**【真题池】。

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | My favourite sport | 7 | My best friend |
| 2 | My day at school | 8 | My plan for a day out |
| 3 | Spring Festival | 9 | A DIY story |
| 4 | My dream home | 10 | My favourite season |
| 5 | Good neighbours | 11 | Great changes in my hometown |
| 6 | My favourite pet | 12 | Teenage problems |

## 参考范文

### 话题 1 · My favourite sport【真题池】
> I love sports, and I like basketball very much. I'm a member of our school basketball team. After school, I often play basketball with my friends. We often talk about basketball and watch basketball matches on TV. Playing basketball always makes me happy. To me, it is more than a sport. It's a way for me to make friends and learn about teamwork.

### 话题 2 · My day at school【真题池】
> Every day at school is exciting and full of learning. In the morning, we start our class at 8:00. After that, we begin studying different subjects like math, science, and history. I enjoy lunchtime because I get to eat with my friends and talk about funny things that happened during the day. In the afternoon, we usually have P.E. or an art class, which are both fun and creative. Sometimes, the lessons are difficult, but I like learning new things and asking my teachers questions. When the day ends, I feel a little tired but happy that I worked hard. School may be challenging at times, but it helps me grow and chase my dreams.

### 话题 6 · My favourite pet【仿写】
> I have a lovely pet. It is a little dog named Coco, and I got it two years ago on my birthday. Coco is very friendly and clever. It can do some tricks, such as shaking hands and catching a ball. Every evening, I take it to the park near my home and play with it for about half an hour. Coco always makes me happy when I feel sad. Taking care of it also teaches me to be patient and responsible. Next year, when I am older, I would like to learn how to train dogs properly.

### 话题 9 · A DIY story【仿写】
> Last summer, I decided to make a bookshelf by myself instead of buying one. It was my first DIY project. My father helped me measure the wood and cut it, but I did all the painting myself. I painted the shelf light blue, because blue is my favourite colour. At first, it was not easy. I spilled some paint on my T-shirt, and the pieces did not fit together well. I wanted to give up, but my father said, "Don't worry, try it again." Finally, I finished it on my own. Now it stands beside my bed and holds all my storybooks. This experience taught me that a good result needs patience and a plan.

> 共同骨架：**亮明话题 → 具体细节（时间/动作/数字）→ 一次小波折 → 用连接词收尾表态**。
> 可数的语言信号：and / but / because / after that / at first / finally，以及时间状语从句。

**检索后新增的三条认知**：

1. **江苏的听力子结构在公开网络上无解。** 官方四份发文只到题型级；各教辅只给「共 10 小题」，不给「哪几段材料、各几题」。
